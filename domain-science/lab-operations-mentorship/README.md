# `domain-science/lab-operations-mentorship/`

The lab-operations-and-mentorship layer for PIs and senior postdocs running groups, trainees, and timelines. Covers individual development, mentoring cadence, onboarding, committee/exam preparation, the postdoc→PI transition, and the norms that hold a group together.

**Stance:** trainee dignity and psychological safety are first-class — stuck projects are surfaced without blame, mistakes are disclosed without fear, expectations are explicit, and well-being concerns route to professional support (these are not clinical tools). Nothing about people, policies, or performance is fabricated.

## Floor (per [`../README.md`](../README.md))

Every prompt requires discipline + career stage; forbids fabricated institutional policies, named people, performance facts, exam content, or career/startup figures (`[user-supplied]`); locks the output format; aligns to the relevant framework (NIH IDP / myIDP, CIMER/Entering Mentoring competencies, mentor–mentee compact, psychological safety, DORA); keeps trainee dignity and realistic expectations central; defaults to good-mentorship + Open-Science norms (attribution, well-being, mistake disclosure); and ends with a verification checklist + false-positive matrix.
