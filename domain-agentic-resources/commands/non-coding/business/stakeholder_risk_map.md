# Stakeholder Risk Map

## Trigger phrase
Run when planning change initiatives that require proactive stakeholder risk management.

## Required inputs
- Initiative summary and timeline.
- Stakeholder list (internal/external) with influence and impact context.
- Known concerns, dependencies, and communication constraints.

## Output schema
- `stakeholder_risk_map`: stakeholder-by-stakeholder risk profile with impact and likelihood.
- `engagement_plan`: targeted messaging and engagement actions by stakeholder segment.
- `early_warning_indicators`: measurable signals that risk is increasing.

## Validation checklist
- [ ] All critical stakeholders are represented, including dissenting groups.
- [ ] Risk ratings are justified by concrete impact/influence factors.
- [ ] Engagement actions are tailored, not generic broadcast steps.
- [ ] Indicators are observable and tied to escalation thresholds.
