# Beat Sheet Generator

## Trigger phrase
Run when creators need a structured narrative beat sheet from a concept or draft.

## Required inputs
- Story premise, protagonist, and central conflict.
- Preferred structure model (three-act, hero’s journey, etc.).
- Target length/format (short story, episode, feature, novel).

## Output schema
- `beat_sheet`: ordered beats with purpose, turning point, and emotional shift.
- `pacing_notes`: estimated proportion or placement guidance for major beats.
- `development_questions`: unresolved story decisions to address before drafting.

## Validation checklist
- [ ] Beats form a coherent causal progression from setup to resolution.
- [ ] Structure model is applied consistently for the chosen format.
- [ ] Major character arc movement is visible across beats.
- [ ] Open questions identify blockers to next drafting step.
