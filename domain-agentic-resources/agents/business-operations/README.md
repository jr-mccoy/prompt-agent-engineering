# Business Operations Agents

> Specialized agents for business analysis, HR, legal, sales, finance, and customer support operations.

## Available Agents

| Agent | Model | Description |
|-------|-------|-------------|
| [business-analyst](business_analyst.md) | SONNET | Master modern business analysis with AI-powered analytics, real-time dashboards, and data-driven insights. Build comprehensive KPI frameworks, predictive models, and strategic recommendations. |
| [customer-support](customer_support.md) | HAIKU | Elite AI-powered customer support specialist mastering conversational AI, automated ticketing, sentiment analysis, and omnichannel support experiences. |
| [hr-pro](hr_pro.md) | SONNET | Professional, ethical HR partner for hiring, onboarding/offboarding, PTO and leave, performance, compliant policies, and employee relations. |
| [legal-advisor](legal_advisor.md) | SONNET | Draft privacy policies, terms of service, disclaimers, and legal notices. Creates GDPR-compliant texts, cookie policies, and data processing agreements. |
| [payment-integration](payment_integration.md) | SONNET | Integrate Stripe, PayPal, and payment processors. Handles checkout flows, subscriptions, webhooks, and PCI compliance. |
| [quant-analyst](quant_analyst.md) | INHERIT | Build financial models, backtest trading strategies, and analyze market data. Implements risk metrics, portfolio optimization, and statistical arbitrage. |
| [risk-manager](risk_manager.md) | INHERIT | Monitor portfolio risk, R-multiples, and position limits. Creates hedging strategies, calculates expectancy, and implements stop-losses. |
| [sales-automator](sales_automator.md) | HAIKU | Draft cold emails, follow-ups, and proposal templates. Creates pricing pages, case studies, and sales scripts. |

## Model Assignments

- **SONNET**: Used for balanced business tasks requiring analysis and documentation
- **HAIKU**: Used for fast, high-volume content generation tasks
- **INHERIT**: Allows user to choose model for specialized financial analysis
