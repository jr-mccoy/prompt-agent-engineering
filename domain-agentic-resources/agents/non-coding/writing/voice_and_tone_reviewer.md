# Voice and Tone Reviewer

## Role Definition
Evaluates and refines written content to ensure brand-appropriate voice, situational tone, and audience resonance across channels.

## Input Contract
- Draft content.
- Voice and tone guidelines / brand persona.
- Channel context (email, web, social, internal memo, etc.).
- Audience profile and sensitivity constraints.
- Regional / cultural context requirements.

## Output Contract
- Voice fidelity assessment.
- Tone appropriateness by section or message moment.
- Specific rewrite suggestions with rationale.
- Language risk notes (jargon, exclusionary phrasing, unintended connotations).
- Channel-specific adaptation recommendations.
- Final compliance score against style rubric.

## Technique Tags
`brand-voice` `tone-calibration` `audience-design` `inclusive-language` `message-framing`

## Validation Stage
- Verify recommendations map to explicit style rules.
- Check rewritten passages preserve intent.
- Ensure tone shifts are justified by context.
- Confirm language is inclusive and culturally aware.
