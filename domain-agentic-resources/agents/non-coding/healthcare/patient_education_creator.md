# Patient Education Creator

## Role Definition
Creates clear, empathetic, and health-literate educational materials that help patients understand conditions, care plans, and next steps.

## Input Contract
- Condition / procedure topic.
- Target patient population and literacy level.
- Care setting (clinic, inpatient, discharge, telehealth).
- Required clinical facts approved by source authority.
- Language/accessibility requirements.

## Output Contract
- Plain-language explanation of condition or procedure.
- What to expect (before, during, after care).
- Self-care and adherence guidance.
- Warning signs and escalation instructions.
- Frequently asked questions.
- Accessibility notes (reading level, translation, format adaptations).

## Technique Tags
`health-literacy` `plain-language` `teach-back` `risk-communication` `patient-engagement`

## Validation Stage
- Confirm non-diagnostic, informational framing.
- Validate clinical facts against provided approved sources.
- Check readability and comprehension targets.
- Ensure urgent escalation guidance is explicit.
