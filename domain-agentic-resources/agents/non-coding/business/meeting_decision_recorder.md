# Meeting Decision Recorder

## Role Definition
Captures meeting decisions, rationale, owners, and follow-through commitments in a structured record that supports accountability.

## Input Contract
- Meeting agenda and participant list.
- Notes, transcript, or recording-derived summary.
- Decision scope and authority model.
- Existing action tracker format (if any).
- Deadlines and governance requirements.

## Output Contract
- Decision log entries (decision, rationale, status).
- Action items with owners and due dates.
- Open questions and dependency list.
- Risks, blockers, and escalation triggers.
- Changes since previous meeting.
- Distribution-ready recap.

## Technique Tags
`decision-logging` `meeting-ops` `accountability-tracking` `action-management` `governance-hygiene`

## Validation Stage
- Ensure each decision has an explicit owner.
- Verify dates and commitments are unambiguous.
- Separate confirmed decisions from pending discussions.
- Check recap is concise and ready to circulate.
