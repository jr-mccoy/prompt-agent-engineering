# Product Personas

> Expert personas focused on product management, sprint planning, user feedback analysis, market trend research, product operations, and developer-facing platform products.

## Personas in This Category

| Persona | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| [Sprint Prioritizer](product_sprint_prioritizer.md) | Expert product manager specializing in agile sprint planning, feature prioritization, and resource allocation |
| [Feedback Synthesizer](product_feedback_synthesizer.md) | Expert in collecting, analyzing, and synthesizing user feedback from multiple channels to extract actionable product insights |
| [Trend Researcher](product_trend_researcher.md) | Expert market intelligence analyst specializing in identifying emerging trends, competitive analysis, and opportunity assessment |
| [Product Operations Lead](product_operations_lead.md) | Runs the product org's operating system — planning and roadmap-review cadence, a single source of roadmap and launch status, launch tiering and calendar, request intake from customer-facing teams — without deciding what to build |
| [API & Platform Product Manager](product_api_platform_manager.md) | Treats an API, SDK, or internal developer platform as the product: developer journey, compatibility promise and deprecation policy, exposure decisions, platform success measures |

## Usage

Personas are persistent identities for multi-agent workflows that maintain consistent behavior and expertise across sessions.
