# Pattern Knowledge Base — Index

*For informational and research purposes only. Not financial, investment, or tax advice.*

This is the durable, git-tracked memory of the toolkit. It contains **no secrets**
and **no market data** — only pattern records and prediction journal entries. Every
row below points at a `patterns/PATTERN-<id>.md` record. Keep this index in sync
when patterns are created, promoted, or retired (the `pattern-knowledge-base` skill
does this; update by hand if editing records directly).

## Status legend

- **hypothesis** — registered, may have in-sample lift, but NOT validated out-of-sample. Cannot drive position sizing (Gate A). May appear on the watchlist as a "paper-only signal."
- **validated** — passed Gate A: out-of-sample test with ≥ configured minimum sample size, beating the base rate. May contribute to screener scores.
- **retired** — edge decayed below base rate, failed re-test, or capacity/cost makes it unusable. Kept for the record with a dated reason.
