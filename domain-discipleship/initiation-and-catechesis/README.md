# Initiation and Catechesis

**3 prompts** for the moments a community formally receives someone — and the instruction that
surrounds them.

This is the subdirectory with the **highest tradition divergence in the domain**. Baptism, membership,
and catechesis are exactly the places where Christian traditions differ most sharply and most
self-consciously. These prompts resolve none of it. They take the community's own practice as an input
and design the preparation around it, while showing the candidate honestly where the disagreement lies —
the multi-view treatment modelled on
[`biblical_learner_compare_traditions_on_practice.md`](../../domain-biblical-studies/learner-self-study/biblical_learner_compare_traditions_on_practice.md).

## The three shared commitments

1. **Nothing is adjudicated.** Infant versus believer baptism, what membership confers, whose polity is
   right — all presented, attributed, and left open. The community's practice runs plainly *as its own*.
2. **Disclosure precedes commitment.** Discipline, authority, exit, obligations, and the existence of
   disagreement are all in the candidate's hands before they decide, not after.
3. **Belonging is never the lever.** No candidate's welcome, care, friendship, or standing depends on
   proceeding, and every prompt carries a cost-free "not yet" with words for both sides.

Nothing here is quoted from memory. A catechism, creed, or confession comes from the user's own text, and
what they do not supply is marked `[VERIFY]` rather than reconstructed.
