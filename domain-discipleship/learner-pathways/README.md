# Learner Pathways

**6 prompts** for the disciple's own journey — usable alone, or run by a mentor with them.

These are the only prompts in the domain written primarily in the *learner's* voice. Every one carries
the boundary guardrail: these are formation tools, not counseling, and distress routes out to
[`domain-psychology/`](../../domain-psychology/) and licensed professionals.

Three of the six are **entry points in their own right** — people rarely arrive at the tidy start of a
pathway. Someone who has stalled, someone returning after years away, and someone whose life will not
fit a standard plan each need a different door.

## Prompt Index

### Orientation & planning
| Prompt | What It Produces |
|---|---|
| [`discipleship_growth_self_assessment.md`](discipleship_growth_self_assessment.md) | A narrative, non-scored picture of where someone is across formation domains, with named next edges |
| [`discipleship_personal_growth_plan.md`](discipleship_personal_growth_plan.md) | A next-season plan: one focus, chosen practices, rhythms, people, and a review date |
| [`discipleship_spiritual_practices_designer.md`](discipleship_spiritual_practices_designer.md) | A sustainable practice set matched to temperament, season, and tradition — with a deliberately small floor |

### Entry points for people not at the start
| Prompt | What It Produces |
|---|---|
| [`discipleship_stalled_growth_diagnostic.md`](discipleship_stalled_growth_diagnostic.md) | A differentiated read on *why* growth stopped, and the specific route for each cause |
| [`discipleship_returning_believer_reengagement.md`](discipleship_returning_believer_reengagement.md) | A non-shaming re-entry plan that takes the reasons for leaving seriously |
| [`discipleship_life_constraints_adaptation.md`](discipleship_life_constraints_adaptation.md) | An existing pathway rebuilt around real capacity — shift work, caregiving, disability, limited access |
