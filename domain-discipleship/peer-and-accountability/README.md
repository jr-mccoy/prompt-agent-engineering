# Peer Relationships and Accountability

**4 prompts** for the relationships in this domain that run **sideways** rather than from mentor to
mentee — disciplers supporting each other, and peers helping each other keep what they have chosen to
keep.

Two pairs, each with a designer and a runner:

- **The mentors' own cohort** — where disciplers are formed rather than only resourced, and where the
  load-bearing rule is that their mentees are not in the room.
- **Accountability partnerships** — designed explicitly against the failure these relationships are
  prone to: surveillance and shame.

## The two failure modes these are built against

**In the cohort:** it becomes a case conference. Mentees are discussed by people they never met, peer
support quietly becomes supervision without either structure's safeguards, and what is said starts
reaching program decisions.

**In the partnership:** it becomes inspection. Checking arrives one reasonable mechanism at a time, a
streak appears, and the honest answer starts costing more than the vague one — after which the
partnership is an elaborate way of lying to a friend.

Both are consistent with the domain's third load-bearing convention: **formation is not a metric.** No
prompt here counts anything about anyone.
