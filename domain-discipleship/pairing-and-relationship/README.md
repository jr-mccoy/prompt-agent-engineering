# Pairing & Relationship

**8 prompts** for matching two people and building the container the relationship lives in — including
the three cases where there is no program doing the matching, no template to hand over, and nothing that
will ever end the relationship unless somebody decides to.

This is the subdirectory a pairing platform leans on hardest. The sequence is deliberate: criteria
before matches, covenant before depth, cadence before commitment, and a designed ending before anyone
needs one. Relationships that end badly usually had no agreed way to end at all.

`discipleship_relationship_covenant.md` carries the **limits of confidentiality** — the single clause
most often left implicit and most damaging when it is. A mentee should know before they disclose
anything what a mentor is obliged to pass on.
