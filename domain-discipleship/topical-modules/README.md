# Topical Formation Modules

**8 prompts** designing the modules every discipleship curriculum eventually needs and few handle well.

Each one is a **module designer**, not a doctrinal position paper. Every prompt here presents the
contested ground as contested, attributes positions to identifiable streams, and hands the exegesis of
every passage to [`domain-biblical-studies/`](../../domain-biblical-studies/). None of them resolves a
disputed question — that is why these were Wave 2 rather than Wave 1.

Three carry heavier guards than the domain baseline: **sexuality and singleness** (STRONG-GUARD — no
disclosure pressure, no change effort, and a run/don't-run decision that can return "not yet"),
**forgiveness and reconciliation** (safety screen before content; reconciliation is never owed), and
**witness in a hostile setting** (no security guidance, no country or group characterization, and every
risk decision left with the person who bears it).

## Prompt Index

### The three that arrive whether or not they are planned for
| Prompt | What It Produces |
|---|---|
| [`discipleship_module_sexuality_and_singleness.md`](discipleship_module_sexuality_and_singleness.md) | **STRONG-GUARD.** A module that presents disagreement, requires no disclosure, and builds singleness as a strand that is not about waiting |
| [`discipleship_module_forgiveness_and_reconciliation.md`](discipleship_module_forgiveness_and_reconciliation.md) | Forgiveness, reconciliation, and restored trust held apart, with a safety screen before any content |
| [`discipleship_module_suffering_and_lament.md`](discipleship_module_suffering_and_lament.md) | Lament as a practice with a form, the theodicy accounts as a range, and an explicit refusal to explain anyone's pain |

### The three that form daily conduct
| Prompt | What It Produces |
|---|---|
| [`discipleship_module_anger_and_conflict.md`](discipleship_module_anger_and_conflict.md) | Anger as information, the conflict passages with their power-imbalance limits attached, and four rehearsed capabilities |
| [`discipleship_module_digital_life.md`](discipleship_module_digital_life.md) | Judgment rather than screen rules, the platform's design taught first, and no mentor-held monitoring |

## How these compose

`anger_and_conflict` sits **upstream** of `forgiveness_and_reconciliation`: the first is for a live
dispute, the second for an injury already done. `suffering_and_lament` is downstream of both when the
cost turns out to be permanent. `digital_life` defers anything past consumption to
`sexuality_and_singleness`. `money` and `work` surface each other's questions constantly and are
usually best sequenced adjacently.
