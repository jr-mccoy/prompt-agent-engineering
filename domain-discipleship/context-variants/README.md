# Context-Specific Program Variants

**4 prompts** for settings where the generic program blueprint under-serves — because someone else's
rules govern, the power gap is structural, or the constraint is distance and risk rather than design.

Each of these **extends** `../program-operations/discipleship_program_design_blueprint.md` and stays
subordinate to `../program-operations/discipleship_safeguarding_and_conduct_policy.md`. None of them
replaces either.

## Prompt Index

| Prompt | Whose rules govern | The specific harm it is designed against |
|---|---|---|
| [`discipleship_context_prison_and_reentry.md`](discipleship_context_prison_and_reentry.md) | The facility and its chaplaincy | An implied institutional benefit, an unkept promise at the gate, and a program that ends at release |
| [`discipleship_context_campus_ministry.md`](discipleship_context_campus_ministry.md) | The institution | A student exposed by participation, an undeclared supervisory pairing, and a group only enterable in week one |
| [`discipleship_context_workplace_and_marketplace.md`](discipleship_context_workplace_and_marketplace.md) | The employer | A manager discipling their report, a downward invitation, and a group that becomes a career channel |
| [`discipleship_context_remote_and_diaspora.md`](discipleship_context_remote_and_diaspora.md) | The participant and people who know their setting | A participant list that should not exist, and crisis planning attempted during the crisis |

## The shared move

All four restate **someone else's rules as that body states them**, with a source and date, and then
design inside them — with a standing principle that where the design and the governing body differ, the
governing body wins and the question is asked rather than resolved locally.

Three carry an explicit refusal to state facts the model cannot verify: no correctional policy, no
institutional or employment law, no assessment of what is legal or safe in any country, and — in the
remote variant — no security guidance or platform recommendation of any kind.
