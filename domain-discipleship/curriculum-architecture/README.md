# Curriculum Architecture

**7 prompts** for designing the discipleship curriculum itself — what someone moves through, in what
order, aimed at what, whether the whole thing is balanced, and what it has become once it has multiplied
beyond the people who wrote it.

These operate at *design* altitude. They produce the blueprint; `../session-and-lesson/` produces what
happens in the room. All Scripture engagement inside a module hands off to
[`domain-biblical-studies/`](../../domain-biblical-studies/).

## When to use these vs. the church-teaching prompts

Use these when you are designing a **formation pathway for individuals or pairs** — a curriculum
someone walks through with a mentor. Use
[`domain-biblical-studies/church-staff-ministry-ops/`](../../domain-biblical-studies/church-staff-ministry-ops/)
when you are designing a **church's teaching program** — services, classes, quarters, volunteers.
