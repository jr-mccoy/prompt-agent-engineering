# Cross-Cultural and Multilingual Discipleship

**3 prompts** for discipling across a culture, a language, or a medium you do not share.

The organizing commitment is the same across all three: **ask rather than assert.** These prompts refuse
to generate or accept generalizations about how people of a nationality, ethnicity, region, or religious
background think, believe, or behave — the same guard carried by
[`negotiation_cross_cultural.md`](../../domain-negotiation/channels/negotiation_cross_cultural.md).
Everything used about a person's culture comes from that person; everything used about a language comes
from a competent bilingual reader.

## Prompt Index

| Prompt | What it refuses to do | What it produces instead |
|---|---|---|
| [`discipleship_crosscultural_relationship.md`](discipleship_crosscultural_relationship.md) | Profile anyone by their group | A question set to ask this person over months, the mentor's own culture written down first, and the power — including language advantage — named |
| [`discipleship_oral_preference_learners.md`](discipleship_oral_preference_learners.md) | Assess anyone's literacy, or build a simplified route | An oral pathway shown to reach the same outcomes, built from the community's own forms, with every Scripture wording sourced rather than remembered |
| [`discipleship_translated_material_pitfalls.md`](discipleship_translated_material_pitfalls.md) | Judge a translation's quality or claim what a word means in a language it cannot read | An item-by-item checking brief for bilingual readers, with the receiving community as the decider |

## The three failure modes these guard against

1. **The profile.** A mentor arrives having read about a culture and spends the relationship correcting
   a picture the disciple never drew.
2. **The remedial route.** An oral pathway built as the simple version — warmer language, thinner
   content — which the disciple detects immediately.
3. **The unmarked import.** A sending tradition's distinctives, psychological vocabulary, and model of
   maturity travelling inside translated material and arriving as Christian teaching.
