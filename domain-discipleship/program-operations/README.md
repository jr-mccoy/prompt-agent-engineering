# Program Operations

**7 prompts** for running a discipleship program — many pairs and many mentors over time, but also the
three-pair version run by one person who has a job, and an audit of whether the program's own design has
drifted toward pressure.

This is the human-operations layer, not the software layer. It designs roles, intake, safety rules,
review cadence, and mentor supply. Product and platform design (PRDs, matching algorithms, onboarding
UX, terms of service) is deliberately out of scope and routes to
[`domain-idea-to-product/`](../../domain-idea-to-product/),
[`domain-product-management/`](../../domain-product-management/), and
[`domain-legal/`](../../domain-legal/).

[`discipleship_safeguarding_and_conduct_policy.md`](discipleship_safeguarding_and_conduct_policy.md)
is load-bearing and comes **before** pairing criteria. It never states a statute, a mandated-reporter
threshold, a background-check requirement, or a retention period — every such claim is flagged
`[VERIFY]` and routed to qualified local counsel.
