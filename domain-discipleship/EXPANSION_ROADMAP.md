## Conventions Wave 2 added

The eight load-bearing conventions in the README are unchanged. Wave 2 added four structural moves that
any future wave in these subdirectories should follow:

1. **Structure before content.** Every life-stage track and context variant settles its binding
   constraint — safeguarding sheet, residence window, abuse screen, honest capacity, access design,
   governing rule set — *before* a session is written. Several of these prompts can return "cannot be
   designed yet" as a legitimate deliverable.
2. **Someone else's rules govern, restated with a source and a date.** The context variants each
   reproduce the facility's, institution's, or employer's own rules in the design document, above a
   standing principle that where the design and the governing body differ, the governing body wins and
   the question is asked rather than resolved locally.
3. **The refusal is part of the deliverable.** Several prompts are defined as much by what they will not
   produce: no security or communications guidance and no country characterization (witness module,
   remote variant), no assessment of translation quality or of what a word means in a language the model
   cannot read (translated material), no assessment of anyone's literacy (oral learners), no
   generalization about any national, ethnic, or religious group (cross-cultural, witness).
4. **Contested is shown, not settled.** `initiation-and-catechesis/` runs a community's own practice
   plainly *as its own* while presenting the divergence in each position's strongest form — the
   `biblical_learner_compare_traditions_on_practice.md` pattern, applied to preparation rather than
   study.

## Permanently out of scope

- **Adjudicating doctrine.** This domain never rules on contested theological questions. Multi-view
  description routes to `domain-biblical-studies/theology-research/`.
- **Numeric spiritual-maturity scoring.** No instrument, index, percentile, or level. See load-bearing
  convention 3 in the README.
- **Assessing whether an individual is genuinely converted.** Not a judgment these prompts make or
  help anyone make.
- **Clinical assessment or treatment of any kind**, including addiction, trauma, and mental health.
- **Naming specific hotlines, agencies, counselors, or services** — always `[VERIFY]`.
- **Stating legal requirements** for safeguarding, screening, reporting, or records retention.
- **Church discipline, membership adjudication, or governance decisions.** Polity routes to
  `domain-biblical-studies/theology-research/biblical_theology_church_government_polity.md`.

## Conventions for whoever authors from this list

1. **Match an exemplar.** `curriculum-architecture/discipleship_curriculum_architecture.md` is the
   house-style reference for design prompts;
   `mentor-equipping/discipleship_mentor_boundaries_and_referral.md` for safety-gated prompts.
   Match its section count and depth, not just its headings.
2. **Frontmatter field order is fixed:** `title`, `category`, `description`, `techniques`,
   `difficulty`, `tags`, `updated`, `related_prompts`. No `reasoning:` block in this domain.
3. **`category` matches the subdirectory** — `discipleship/<subdir>`, always.
4. **Exactly 5 technique IDs**, all resolving in `techniques/MASTER_TECHNIQUE_INDEX.md`. Never the
   deprecated `OC-01`, `QA-03`, `SV-04`, `DD-01`, `DD-08`.
5. **`related_prompts`: 4–5 verified repo-root-relative paths**, ≥1 intra-domain and ≥1 into
   `domain-biblical-studies/`. Confirm each file exists before writing the link.
6. **Ten `##` headings, in this order:** `Inputs / Context`, `Constraints`, `Instructions`,
   `Output Format`, `Verification`, `False-Positive Prevention`, `Techniques Used` — with
   `**Objective:**`, `**When to use:**`, `**When NOT to use:**`, and `**Audience:**` as bold-label
   paragraphs, never headings. Horizontal rules between every top-level section.
7. **`## Constraints` always carries three `###` subsections:** `Must`, `Must Not`, and
   `Tradition-neutral stance (Must / Must Not)`. The third is non-optional.
8. **`## Inputs / Context` always includes** a `**Declared tradition (optional).**` numbered item.
9. **Every mentor-facing, pair-facing, and learner-facing prompt** carries the boundary-guardrail
   blockquote immediately after `**Objective:**`.
10. **Do not add:** numeric maturity scores, named hotlines or agencies, statutory claims, fabricated
    research or statistics, Scripture text quoted from memory, or a "recommended tradition."
11. **After adding files:** update this roadmap, the domain `README.md` (subdirectory map,
    per-subdirectory tables, quick routing), the subdirectory `README.md`, the routing rows in root
    `CLAUDE.md`, the count in root `README.md` and `REPO_MAP.md`; then regenerate with
    `python3 scripts/generate_prompt_index.py`.

## Wave 3 — shipped (2026-08-04)

**A prompts-only, gap-closure wave. The orchestrator originally proposed as Wave 3 was deferred to
Wave 4** on the grounds that a router across an incomplete surface routes confidently to the wrong
place: at 60 prompts the domain had structural holes an orchestrator would have papered over.

Waves 1 and 2 built a grid — six actor/artifact subdirectories crossed with six cross-cuts. A grid is
always extensible, which is why the original Wave 3 list read as it did: it proposed more topics, more
stages, more settings because those axes accept more, not because anyone was unserved. Wave 3 instead
closed gaps, and gaps are finite. **9 of the 13 exist because of something already in the repo** — a
route that pointed at nothing, a risk named but never handled, a policy row covering only one party, a
convention with no audit, or an audience line that assumed an institution.

### What the wave closed

1. **Dangling destinations.** `learner-pathways/discipleship_stalled_growth_diagnostic.md` differentiated
   *unresolved doubt* and *unaddressed harm* as causes and routed both to prompts that did not exist.
   Both now exist, and Step 5 points at them.
2. **The harm end of the arc.** The domain had safeguarding as policy and boundaries as scope, and harm
   as one of five ending types — but nothing for dependency below the safeguarding line (named as a risk
   in five files, unwound in none), nothing for someone harmed in a previous discipling relationship, and
   nothing for the mentees and cohort left after a removal. `after-harm/` is the thirteenth subdirectory.
3. **Convention 6 had no compliance check.** Seven of the eight load-bearing conventions are enforced
   per-prompt; *no spiritual coercion* was enforced nowhere.
   `program-operations/discipleship_program_control_drift_audit.md` audits the program's own artifacts
   for exit cost, information asymmetry, penalty for dissent, and unaccountable authority — and routes
   the personal version of the question to `domain-psy-ops/` rather than re-deriving it.
4. **The institution assumption.** Every audience line in `pairing-and-relationship/` and
   `program-operations/` named a program lead or platform designer, leaving the two-people-and-a-coffee
   case and the bivocational or church-plant leader unserved by the whole domain.
5. **Accessibility was learner-side only.** `life_constraints_adaptation.md` adapts one named person's
   pathway; nothing designed a session to work in the first place.

### Convention Wave 3 added

**A prompt may refuse to produce its own artifact.** Wave 2 established that structure precedes content
and that "cannot be designed yet" is a legitimate deliverable. Wave 3 extends this to a gate that stops
the prompt outright: `discipleship_mentor_own_mistake_repair.md` returns a reporting route instead of an
apology; `discipleship_informal_pairing_without_a_program.md` returns "not like this" with no consolation
plan attached; `discipleship_after_a_mentor_is_removed.md` refuses to run before a referral has been
made. In each the gate is a table with a yes/no per row rather than a prose judgement, because prose is
where a motivated reading gets in.

## Wave 4 — candidate future work (not yet built)

- **A guided orchestrator** (`orchestrator_discipleship_program.md`) in the style of
  `agentic-system-factory/` or `childrens-book-studio/`. It would interview, classify the user's starting
  point (standing up a program vs. building content vs. running one pair vs. being discipled vs. after
  something went wrong), route to ≤3 prompts, and enforce the domain's hard gates. Wave 3 added two to
  the list it must carry: **a safeguarding floor before any informal pairing**, and **the safety and
  clinical screen before `discipleship_harmed_by_previous_discipling.md`** — alongside the existing
  safeguarding policy before any youth track, abuse screen before any couples work, and governing rules
  before any context variant.
  **The condition for building it:** the routing surface is now 73 prompts across 13 subdirectories with
  no known structural gap, which was the objection that deferred it. What remains is evidence that
  practitioners are routing badly by hand — mis-entry at the top of the domain rather than difficulty
  inside a prompt.
- **Slash commands** for the highest-traffic entry points, once the orchestrator exists.
