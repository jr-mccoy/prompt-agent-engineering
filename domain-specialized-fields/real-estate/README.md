# Real Estate — Pricing, Offers, Underwriting, and Due Diligence

Four prompts for the **non-legal side** of a residential transaction, written for
working agents and for serious buyers and small investors. Each one turns
user-supplied evidence (MLS comps, leases, quotes, an inspection report) into a
decision with the arithmetic shown.

## Contents

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| [`realestate_comparative_market_analysis.md`](realestate_comparative_market_analysis.md) | Comp screen, line-item adjustment grid with net/gross limits, weighted range; actives read as competition, not value |
| [`realestate_buyer_offer_strategy.md`](realestate_buyer_offer_strategy.md) | Offer packages priced in worst-case cash: escalation cap, appraisal-gap coverage, contingencies kept/shortened/waived, reserve floor |
| [`realestate_rental_property_underwriting.md`](realestate_rental_property_underwriting.md) | 1–4 unit rental: NOI, cap rate, DSCR, cash-on-cash, break-even occupancy, reserves, stress test, traffic-light verdict |
| [`realestate_inspection_report_triage.md`](realestate_inspection_report_triage.md) | Inspection findings sorted into safety / material defect / maintenance / info / not inspected, routed to licensed specialists inside the contingency window |

The four chain on one worked scenario: the CMA's range sets the offer's cap and
appraisal test; the inspection triage feeds renegotiation under the offer's
contingency and the underwriting stress test.

## Guards (every prompt)

- **No fabricated market data.** Comps, rents and costs come from the user and are
  tagged by source; nothing is filled in from memory. A gap is `[NOT PROVIDED]`
  or `[get quote]`.
- **Not an appraisal, not legal or tax advice.** A CMA is an indicated range;
  offer terms are priced, not drafted; tax effects go to a CPA.
- **Licensed judgments stay licensed.** Code compliance, structural adequacy and
  contract enforceability are routed to the licensed trade, engineer, local
  authority or attorney.
