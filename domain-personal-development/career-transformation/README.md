# Career & Work Transformation

Prompts for the hard, adult work of evaluating and repositioning a career in an era where roles are under structural pressure — not generic career-advice prompts. These are diagnostic-first: audit your week, grade your role's vulnerability, inventory what's actually scarce in your skillset, then build a 90-day plan you can execute.

## When to Use This Cluster

- You sense your role is shifting and want an evidence-grounded read on what's actually happening.
- You're preparing a repositioning toward adjacent or surviving roles and need a staged plan, not a resume rewrite.
- A manager is evaluating a role (their own or someone's on their team) before restructuring.
- The agency prompts (`domain-personal-development/prompts/agency/`) have helped with project-level execution, and you now need career-level diagnostics.

## Prompts

| # | Prompt | Use When |

| 1 | [`career_coordination_tax_audit.md`](career_coordination_tax_audit.md) | You feel busy but unproductive; classify a real week's blocks into coordination-tax categories to see where the time actually goes. |
| 2 | [`career_role_structural_vulnerability.md`](career_role_structural_vulnerability.md) | Grade a role on four independent axes (economic function, coordination-tax capture, scarce-input control, substitution slope) — not a "safe/not safe" verdict. |

| 5 | [`career_ai_era_skill_moat.md`](career_ai_era_skill_moat.md) | Split your skillset into a durable moat (judgment, taste, relationships, context) vs automatable surface area, then pick the one moat component to concentrate investment in. |

| 7 | [`career_internal_vs_external_move.md`](career_internal_vs_external_move.md) | Decide whether the next move is internal (new role/scope where you are) or external (new employer), compared on growth, leverage, risk, and time-to-impact. |

## Recommended Sequence

Run in order when doing a full assessment:

1. `career_coordination_tax_audit.md` → grounds you in what your week actually is.
2. `career_role_structural_vulnerability.md` → grades the role using the audit as input.
3. `career_residual_skills_inventory.md` → identifies what you hold that's load-bearing.
4. `career_90_day_repositioning_plan.md` → builds the executable plan.

Each prompt can also be run standalone.

Prompts 5–8 are **follow-ons** to the core assessment: once you know your vulnerability and residual inventory, use `career_ai_era_skill_moat.md` to decide where to concentrate, `career_positioning_statement.md` to articulate it, `career_internal_vs_external_move.md` to choose the move, and `career_reskilling_roadmap.md` to close the gap to a target role.

## Design Principles

- **Evidence over affirmation.** These prompts refuse generic career advice. They require specific inputs (real calendar, real episodes, real vulnerability data) and downgrade claims without evidence.
- **Diagnostic, not prescriptive.** The audit / vulnerability / inventory prompts describe; only the repositioning prompt prescribes — and only with stop conditions.
- **Honest asymmetry.** Grades can come out worse than the user hoped; plans can discover there's no viable bridge. The prompts surface both rather than soften.
- **Four tiers of skill classification** (Residual, Compounding, Commoditized, Aspirational, Credential-only) with forced reclassification when defensibility is weak.
