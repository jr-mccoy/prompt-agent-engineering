# Job Search (Candidate Side)

Prompts for running an external job search from the candidate's side: choosing what to aim at, making the documents true and targeted, reaching people, preparing for interviews, and running the search as a pipeline. Evidence-first throughout — no prompt here will invent a metric, title or credential, and each one flags claims the candidate cannot substantiate.

## When to Use This Cluster

- You have decided to look externally and need to turn that into applications, conversations and interviews.
- Your search is producing activity but not results, and you want to know which stage is failing.
- You are changing role family and your résumé, profile and stories still describe the old one.

Not here: deciding *whether* to move (`../career-transformation/career_internal_vs_external_move.md`), evaluating an offer (`../major-decisions/personal_career_offer_evaluation.md`), negotiating terms (`domain-negotiation/`), recovering from a layoff before searching (`../prompts/life-transitions/lifetransition_job_loss_recovery_plan.md`), or the employer side of hiring (`domain-hr-management/hiring/`).

## Prompts

| # | Prompt | Use When |
|---|--------|----------|
| 1 | [`jobsearch_target_role_and_market_map.md`](jobsearch_target_role_and_market_map.md) | Turn real postings into one primary role family and one fallback, with the evidence gap and a dated kill signal. |
| 2 | [`jobsearch_resume_evidence_rewriter.md`](jobsearch_resume_evidence_rewriter.md) | Rewrite bullets as action–scope–result from facts you can substantiate; every figure tagged verified, estimate or missing. |
| 3 | [`jobsearch_job_posting_fit_decoder.md`](jobsearch_job_posting_fit_decoder.md) | Tier one posting's requirements into screening, core and wish-list, map your evidence, and decide apply / apply with angle / skip. |
| 4 | [`jobsearch_cover_letter_builder.md`](jobsearch_cover_letter_builder.md) | Write a half-page letter for one posting: angle first, two or three traced proof points, at most one gap. |
| 5 | [`jobsearch_linkedin_profile_audit.md`](jobsearch_linkedin_profile_audit.md) | Audit the profile section by section against recruiter search and check it against the résumé. |
| 6 | [`jobsearch_networking_outreach_plan.md`](jobsearch_networking_outreach_plan.md) | Rank contacts by tie and proximity, give each one easy-to-decline ask, and cap the week at real hours. |
| 7 | [`jobsearch_behavioral_interview_story_bank.md`](jobsearch_behavioral_interview_story_bank.md) | Build eight to twelve true STAR stories mapped to the role's competencies, stress-tested against follow-ups. |
| 8 | [`jobsearch_pipeline_tracker_and_cadence.md`](jobsearch_pipeline_tracker_and_cadence.md) | Track stages and conversion by source, find the leaking stage, and make one change per week; stops at the offer. |

## Recommended Sequence

1. `jobsearch_target_role_and_market_map.md` → what you are aiming at.
2. `jobsearch_resume_evidence_rewriter.md` and `jobsearch_linkedin_profile_audit.md` → documents that match the target and each other.
3. `jobsearch_networking_outreach_plan.md` alongside `jobsearch_job_posting_fit_decoder.md` → `jobsearch_cover_letter_builder.md` per application.
4. `jobsearch_behavioral_interview_story_bank.md` → before the first screen, not the first loop.
5. `jobsearch_pipeline_tracker_and_cadence.md` → weekly from the first application; it sends you back to whichever prompt fixes the leaking stage.

Each prompt can also be run standalone.

## Design Principles

- **Honesty guard.** Nothing is added to a résumé, profile, letter or story that the candidate did not supply or confirm. Figures carry provenance; unsupported claims are flagged with a fix, never smoothed over.
- **One target, one change.** Every prompt converges — one primary role family, one angle per posting, one ask per contact, one weekly change — rather than handing back a menu.
- **Skip is a valid answer.** The fit decoder and outreach plan are allowed to say "don't apply" and "don't ask yet".
- **Candidate side only.** Hiring-side design lives in `domain-hr-management/`; offer terms live in `domain-negotiation/`.
