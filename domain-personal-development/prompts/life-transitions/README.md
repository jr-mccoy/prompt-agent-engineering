# Life Transitions

Prompts for navigating a life change **during or after it happens** — living through it, not deciding on it. This is reconstruction work: recalibrating capacity, rebuilding routine and belonging, reconciling identity, and locating yourself in the arc of a change that is already underway. Written for an individual doing their own transition — not for advising a partner, child, or employee through theirs, and not for clinical treatment of grief, depression, or trauma.

## When to use these

Use this subfolder when:

- The change has already happened (you moved, the job ended, the child left, the relationship ended) and you're rebuilding.
- You feel unsettled, unmoored, or "not myself" and can't tell whether it's ordinary adjustment or genuine stuckness.
- A role that defined you has ended or transformed and the question is "who am I now."
- You're re-entering (a role, work after leave) and feel pressure to snap back to a pace the change made impossible.
- You can't tell which stage of a transition you're in — start with `lifetransition_transition_map_and_timeline.md`.

**Not the right subfolder when:**

- You're **deciding whether to make the change** (take the job, move, have a child, end the relationship, retire) — see `../../major-decisions/` (`personal_*.md`).
- The distress is **clinical** — grief/bereavement, depression, trauma, postpartum mood disorders, or any crisis or self-harm thought — see `domain-psychology/` and a licensed professional or crisis line. Every prompt here carries that routing line; honor it.
- You're **assessing or coaching someone else** through their transition — these are first-person tools.
- The problem is a **broken habit or a stuck project** rather than a life change — see `../agency/` and `../resilience/`.

## How the prompts relate

`lifetransition_transition_map_and_timeline.md` is the meta-navigator: run it first if you don't know which phase you're in, and it will route you to the specific prompt for your transition. `lifetransition_identity_after_major_change.md` is the general identity engine underneath the specific cases (retirement, empty nest, job loss, breakup) — use it for the parts those don't cover. The event-specific prompts (relocation, parenthood, job loss, breakup, leave, new role) each carry their own fixed taxonomy and converge on one decisive move.

## Related domains in this repo

- `../../major-decisions/` — deciding whether to make the change (upstream of every prompt here).
- `../identity/` — values, purpose, self-talk, and life-audit work that identity-after-change draws on.
- `../resilience/` — setback recovery, momentum rebuild, and failure reframing (complementary to job-loss and breakup work).
- `../agency/` — stuck-diagnosis and next-action tools when the transition has stalled into inaction.
- `domain-psychology/` — clinical grief, depression, trauma, postpartum mood disorders, and crisis support (the boundary every prompt here routes to).
