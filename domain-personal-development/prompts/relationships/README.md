# Relationships Prompts

Communication skills and relationship-maintenance tools for **personal, non-work relationships** — partners, family, friends, roommates, neighbors. These prompts help the user say the hard thing well, keep relationships warm, get better at specific social skills, and repair after a rupture.

They assume the user is willing to look honestly at their own contribution (behavior, tone, follow-through) rather than aiming the work entirely at changing the other person.

## Scope

**In scope:**
- Designing and rehearsing a boundary-setting conversation
- Preparing for an emotionally hard personal conversation
- Making new friends as an adult (from zero, via repeated proximity)
- Deepening one specific shallow-but-pleasant friendship
- Cultivating and maintaining personal relationships over time
- Deliberate practice for a specific, named social skill
- A durable strategy for one difficult (non-abusive) family relationship
- Constructing a real apology for a specific rupture
- Repairing a personal relationship after a fight or breach
- Diagnosing the type of loneliness and building a targeted connection plan
- A lightweight health-check of a single relationship

**Out of scope (refuse and refer):**
- **Abuse, coercion, threats, monitoring, financial control, or any safety risk** → this is not a communication-skills problem. Route to a licensed counselor, a domestic-violence hotline, or trusted local resources. These prompts will not coach negotiation, boundary-setting, or "repair" with someone who is unsafe.
- **Relationship trauma, severe social anxiety, or possible clinical conditions** → a licensed mental-health professional. These prompts prepare conversations and build skills; they are not therapy.
- **Couples therapy / partner conflict work** → relationship counseling. These prompts coach one person's communication and maintenance, not joint clinical work.

## Boundary: personal vs. professional

This cluster is **personal**. For the work/professional equivalents:

- Hard conversations at work (firing, hard feedback, peer conflict) → `domain-negotiation/difficult-conversations/`
- Professional networking, building career contacts/collaborators → `domain-personal-development/prompts/solo-dev/solo_dev_network_building.md`
- Workplace stakeholder navigation → `domain-personal-development/prompts/stakeholder/`

## Non-clinical stance

These prompts coach **communication and relationship maintenance**, not mental health. They do not diagnose either party, do not promise a particular reaction from the other person (the user controls their own words and follow-through, not the outcome), and route safety/abuse/trauma concerns to professional help rather than coaching around them.

## Composition patterns

- **Recurring-friction route:** `relationships_relationship_audit` → drain is a specific behavior → `relationships_boundary_setting_script`.
- **Rupture route:** `relationships_conflict_repair_guide` → recurring pattern → pair with `relationships_boundary_setting_script` (forward boundary).
- **Big-conversation route:** `relationships_hard_conversation_prep` → if it narrows to one limit → `relationships_boundary_setting_script`.
- **Drift route:** `relationships_network_cultivation_plan` → a tie surfaces as strained → `relationships_relationship_audit` → `relationships_conflict_repair_guide`.
- **Stay/leave route:** any prompt → genuine doubt about continuing → escalate to `major-decisions/personal_difficult_relationship_audit` (decision-grade).
- **Skill route:** repeated trouble with a specific behavior → `relationships_social_skill_development`.
- **Thin-circle route:** few or no friends → `relationships_loneliness_diagnostic_plan` (diagnose the type) → `relationships_making_friends_as_adult` (relational gap) → `relationships_deepening_friendship` (intimate gap).
- **Family route:** a recurring difficult (non-abusive) relative → `relationships_difficult_family_member_strategy` → if it narrows to one limit → `relationships_boundary_setting_script`.
- **Apology route:** you caused a specific rupture and are ready to own it → `relationships_apology_that_lands`; if the fight was co-authored and needs fuller sorting → `relationships_conflict_repair_guide`.

## What the prompts refuse

- Coaching contact, boundaries, or "repair" with an unsafe or abusive person
- Diagnosing the user or the other person (mental-health or character labels)
- Scripts designed to win, punish, score points, or extract an admission
- Promising the other person will respond a certain way
- Treating relationships as a utility/CRM pipeline to be optimized
- Substituting for therapy when trauma or clinical anxiety is the real issue

## File map

| Prompt | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `relationships_boundary_setting_script.md` | Turn a recurring frustration into one statable boundary, with a spoken script, pushback replies, and a self-owned follow-through. |
| `relationships_hard_conversation_prep.md` | Prep one emotionally hard personal conversation: one achievable outcome, facts-vs-story, opening, the other's perspective, an escalation plan. (Personal counterpart to `domain-negotiation/difficult-conversations/`.) |
| `relationships_making_friends_as_adult.md` | Turn "I want more friends" into a plan: score venues for repeated proximity, place acquaintances on a friendship ladder, pick 2–3 conversion targets, script the exact first invitations. (New friends, from zero.) |
| `relationships_deepening_friendship.md` | Move one pleasant-but-shallow friendship toward depth via graduated, reciprocation-matched self-disclosure and shared experience — without forcing it. |
| `relationships_network_cultivation_plan.md` | A sustainable, low-guilt system to keep personal relationships warm: tiered inventory, realistic cadence, dated gestures. (Distinct from professional networking.) |
| `relationships_social_skill_development.md` | A deliberate-practice loop for one observable social skill: behavioral markers, a graduated rep ladder, after-action review, calibration. |
| `relationships_difficult_family_member_strategy.md` | A durable strategy for one difficult (non-abusive) family relationship: classify the difficulty, name what to stop trying to change, design contact + boundaries, pre-plan the flashpoints. |
| `relationships_apology_that_lands.md` | Construct a real apology for one specific rupture: name the actual harm, strip every if/but/because, attach a concrete repair — producing the exact words. (Surgical; narrower than the full repair guide.) |
| `relationships_conflict_repair_guide.md` | Repair after a rupture: reconstruct to the root, sort responsibility honestly, calibrate the apology, draft the attempt, plan for the response you don't control. |
| `relationships_loneliness_diagnostic_plan.md` | Diagnose which loneliness (intimate / relational / collective) the user has and build a type-matched connection plan; routes depression/grief/purpose-loss look-alikes out. |
| `relationships_relationship_audit.md` | Lightweight health-check of one relationship (working / draining / balance / one small move). Escalates to the decision-grade `major-decisions/personal_difficult_relationship_audit.md`. |
