# Emotional Fitness

Everyday, non-clinical prompts for building emotional skill: naming what you feel, processing a specific letdown, reading jealousy as a pointer, sorting worry from action, catching over-reactions, holding mixed feelings, resetting after a mistake or a charged moment, sorting shame from guilt, and debriefing an emotional event. Written for an individual practicing on their own real situations — one moment or one recent event at a time — not for interpreting anyone else's emotions.

These are situational, skill-level tools. Each one takes a specific feeling or event, works it into named parts, and converges on one concrete move. None of them assess, score, or diagnose.

## BOUNDARY — this is non-clinical skill-building, not therapy

This subfolder is for the ordinary emotional work of a general-population adult: the bad day, the sting, the swirl, the moment you snapped. It is deliberately **not** a mental-health toolkit. It does not use symptom scoring (PHQ-9, GAD-7), CBT/ERP protocols, panic plans, safety planning, or any clinical assessment, and it never diagnoses.

**Use `domain-psychology/client-self-use/` instead of this folder when** the feeling is **persistent** (most days for two or more weeks), **intense or overwhelming** (panic, dissociation, flashbacks, feeling unable to function), **safety-relevant** (any thought of harming yourself or others), **trauma-linked**, or **already the subject of care** (you're in therapy, or a clinician is involved). Those situations need the clinical resources and a licensed professional — not a skill prompt. Every prompt in this folder carries this routing in its **Audience** line, and several route to a specific `client-self-use` resource by name. If there are thoughts of harming yourself, contact a crisis line or emergency services now.

## When to use these

Use this subfolder when:

- You feel "off" or "bad" and want to name what's actually there before you act.
- A specific disappointment keeps replaying and you want it to land cleanly.
- A flash of jealousy or envy is telling you something and you want to convert it.
- Your head is looping on many worries at once the night before something.
- You keep over-reacting to the same kind of trigger and want to catch it next time.
- You feel two opposed things at once and it makes you feel confused or fake.
- You messed up and the self-punishment has grown bigger than the mistake.
- A charged moment keeps bleeding into the rest of your day.
- You can't tell whether you owe an apology or you're just spiraling.
- An emotional event is over and you want to learn from it, not just relive it.

## Not the right subfolder when

- **You're in therapy, considering it, in distress, or there are any safety concerns** → `domain-psychology/client-self-use/` and a licensed professional. This is the load-bearing boundary above.
- **The feeling is persistent, intense, trauma-linked, or interfering with daily life** → same: `domain-psychology/` + professional help, not a skill prompt.
- **You want deep values, purpose, self-worth, or identity work** (not a single moment, but who you are and what you're for) → `domain-personal-development/prompts/identity/`.
- **You want to recover motivation, discipline, or momentum after a setback** → `domain-personal-development/prompts/resilience/`.
- **You're interpreting or managing someone else's emotions** → this folder is first-person only; see negotiation, relationships, or communication domains.

## Prompts in this subfolder

| `emotionalfitness_self_compassion_reset.md` | After a mistake, separate the fact, the self-attack, and the fair correction — drop the punishment, keep the correction. |

## How the prompts relate

These compose in a few common sequences:

- **In the moment / same day:** `emotionalfitness_emotion_labeling_practice.md` to name what's there, then `emotionalfitness_worry_vs_action_sorter.md` or `emotionalfitness_jealousy_channeling.md` depending on what the feeling turns out to be.
- **After a charged moment:** `emotionalfitness_emotional_reset_ritual.md` first (cool down), then `emotionalfitness_charged_event_debrief.md` once cool.
- **After a mistake:** `emotionalfitness_shame_vs_guilt_sorter.md` to sort the feeling, then `emotionalfitness_self_compassion_reset.md` for the mistake-specific reset.
- **A recurring reaction:** `emotionalfitness_reactivity_trigger_audit.md` for the pattern and the intercept.
- **Recurring comparison specifically:** `emotionalfitness_jealousy_channeling.md` handles the moment and routes deeper patterns to `identity/identity_comparison_envy_diagnostic.md`.

## Related domains in this repo

- `domain-psychology/client-self-use/` — the clinical counterpart for persistent, intense, or safety-relevant distress (the boundary destination above).
- `domain-personal-development/prompts/identity/` — values, self-talk, comparison/envy, confidence, purpose (deeper, self-image-level work).
- `domain-personal-development/prompts/resilience/` — setback recovery, motivation, discipline, momentum (non-clinical).
- `domain-personal-development/prompts/relationships/` — boundaries, hard conversations, conflict repair (when the feeling is about a relationship and needs action toward another person).
- `domain-negotiation/difficult-conversations/` — preparing for and debriefing a hard conversation with someone else.
