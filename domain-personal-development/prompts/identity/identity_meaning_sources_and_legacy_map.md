# Meaning Sources and Legacy Map

**Objective:** Show where your sense of meaning actually comes from today, which
part of meaning is thin, how exposed you are if one source fails, what you want to
leave behind in concrete terms, and one experiment to build meaning somewhere new.

**When to Use:**
- You reached the thing you worked toward (the exit, the title, the book, the
  house) and the expected satisfaction did not arrive.
- Around midlife, the question shifted from "how do I get ahead?" to "what is this
  for?" or "what will be left of it?"
- Almost all of your meaning comes from one place — usually work or children — and
  that place is changing.
- You want a meaning practice that does not depend on a particular religion, and
  does not exclude one.
- **Not this prompt if** drive has faded on one specific project or role — that is
  `identity_purpose_reignition.md`, which diagnoses *why* motivation went quiet.
  For a full audit of identity, relationships, work, health, and time left at an
  inflection, use `identity_life_audit_reckoning.md`; this prompt goes deep on the
  meaning dimension only. For the specific loss of work structure after stopping
  work, use `prompts/life-transitions/lifetransition_retirement_purpose_redesign.md`;
  for deferred someday items, `identity_mortality_reflection_memento_mori.md`.

**Audience:** An individual examining their own life. Not therapy. If emptiness
comes with low mood most days for two weeks or more, loss of pleasure in almost
everything, hopelessness, or changes in sleep and appetite, that may be depression
rather than a meaning question — use
`domain-psychology/client-self-use/crisis-self-triage/clientself_am_i_in_crisis_self_triage.md`
and see a doctor or therapist before continuing. If there are thoughts of ending your
life, contact a crisis line (988 in the US) or emergency services now.

## Inputs / Context

**Required:**
1. **The trigger** — what happened, or what you noticed, that raised the question.
2. **Recent meaningful moments** — five to ten moments from the last six months
   when life felt worthwhile, and three when it felt empty. What were you doing,
   with whom, for whom?
3. **Where your weeks go** — rough hours across work, family, friends, community,
   learning, body, solitude, faith or practice (if any).
4. **What you would want said about you** by three people at different distances —
   someone close, a colleague, someone you helped once.

**Optional:** Beliefs or traditions that matter to you; earlier periods of life that
felt most meaningful and why.

## Method

1. **Assess the three facets of meaning (RT-02).** Psychology research commonly
   splits meaning into **coherence** (my life makes sense), **purpose** (I am
   heading toward something), and **significance** (my life matters to someone or
   something). Rate each low / medium / high and cite moments as evidence. They fail
   differently: post-achievement emptiness is often a purpose gap (the goal is done);
   midlife questioning is often a significance gap.
2. **Map the sources.** Sort the meaningful moments by source — work/craft,
   close relationships, contribution/service, growth/mastery, belonging to
   something larger (community, tradition, cause, nature, faith), and experience
   (beauty, play, presence). Estimate the share of meaning from each.
3. **Find concentration risk (RT-05).** If one source supplies more than about
   half of the meaningful moments, name what happens when it changes (children
   leave, the job ends, the body slows). Cross-check against hours: a source with
   many hours and few meaningful moments is a drain worth noting.
4. **Name the arrival effect if present.** If the trigger was reaching a goal,
   say plainly that a finished goal stops supplying purpose — this is expected, not a
   personal failing — and that the fix is a new direction, not a bigger version of
   the old goal.
5. **State a legacy in concrete terms.** Turn input 4 into *who* benefits, *what*
   they receive (a skill taught, a thing built, a way of being treated), and *how
   it continues* without you. Midlife "generativity" — Erikson's term for investing in
   what outlasts you — is usually about specific people, not monuments.
6. **Pick one under-built source** that would raise the weakest facet and fits
   your real hours.
7. **Design a 90-day experiment (DP-11).** One recurring commitment with a
   start date and a check at 30, 60, 90 days: "Did this produce meaningful moments,
   by the same test as input 2?" Keep it reversible.

## Output Format

```
# Meaning map — [date]

## Facets
| Facet | Rating | Evidence (moments) |
Weakest facet: [..]

## Sources of meaning
| Source | Meaningful moments | Share | Weekly hours |
Concentration risk: [source, share, what changes it]

## Arrival effect: [present / not present] — [one line]

## Legacy, concretely
Who: [..]   What they receive: [..]   How it continues: [..]

## Under-built source: [..] — raises [facet] because [..]

## 90-day experiment
Commitment: [..]  Start: [date]  Checks: [30/60/90-day dates]
Test: produces meaningful moments by the input-2 standard?
```

## Verification

- [ ] All three facets rated, each with cited moments.
- [ ] Source shares come from the person's moments, not a generic list.
- [ ] Concentration risk named with what would change it.
- [ ] Legacy names people and a mechanism, not an abstraction.
- [ ] One experiment, recurring, dated, reversible, with a check standard.
- [ ] Depression signs checked and routed; no mission statement produced.

## False-Positive Prevention

1. **Depression read as a meaning problem.** Pervasive emptiness with low mood and
   loss of pleasure needs clinical assessment first; no reflection exercise
   substitutes.
2. **One meaning source privileged.** Work is not more legitimate than play, and
   faith is not required or dismissed. Map what the person reports.
3. **Mission statements.** A sentence about "my purpose" changes nothing; a
   recurring commitment does.
4. **Grand legacy only.** Legacy at the scale of a foundation excludes most
   people. A grandchild who can fix a bicycle is a legacy.
5. **Bigger version of the old goal.** After an exit, "a bigger exit" often
   repeats the arrival effect. Test whether the next goal adds a new source.
6. **Low ratings without evidence.** A bad week colours everything; ratings
   must point to moments across the six months.

## Example Output

```
# Meaning map — 2026-10-03

## Facets
| Coherence    | High   | clear story of the 12 years building the company; no confusion about choices |
| Purpose      | Low    | sale closed in June; no goal since; 6 of 8 empty moments are "Monday with nothing to build" |
| Significance | Medium | daughter's science fair (mattered to her); mentoring call with ex-employee |
Weakest facet: Purpose (arrival effect after the sale).

## Sources of meaning
| Work / building        | 5 of 9 moments (before June) | 55% | 10 h now (advisory) |
| Close relationships    | 2 of 9 | 22% | 15 h |
| Contribution / service | 1 of 9 (mentoring call) | 11% | 1 h |
| Growth / mastery       | 1 of 9 (sailing course) | 11% | 3 h |
| Belonging to larger    | 0 | 0% | 0 h |
| Experience             | 0 | 0% | screens ~14 h |
Concentration risk: 55% came from building, which ended with the sale.

## Arrival effect: present — the company was the purpose; finishing it removed the
direction, not the competence.

## Legacy, concretely
Who: first-time founders in the city; my daughter.
What they receive: how to hire the first ten people without wrecking the culture;
a parent who is present on weekdays.
How it continues: founders I advise hire well and pass it on.

## Under-built source: Contribution — the single mentoring call was the highest-
rated moment since June and draws on the building skill without needing a new company.

## 90-day experiment
Commitment: two founder office-hours sessions a week through the local accelerator,
Tuesdays and Thursdays, 9–11 am. Start 2026-10-14. Checks: 11-13, 12-13, 2027-01-12.
Test: at least one session a week rated a meaningful moment by my input-2 standard.
Note: not starting a second company before 2027-01-12 — test a new source first.
```

## Techniques Used

- **RT-02 Multi-Dimensional Analysis Framework** — coherence, purpose, and significance rated separately.
- **RT-05 Evidence-Based Reasoning** — facets and source shares come from the person's own moments and hours.
- **DP-11 Safe Experiment Design** — a reversible 90-day commitment with dated checks.
- **QA-12 False Positives Identification** — screens out depression, mission statements, and "bigger old goal" traps.
