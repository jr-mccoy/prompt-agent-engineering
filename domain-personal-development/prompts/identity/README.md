# Identity Prompts

Identity work that doesn't fit cleanly into agency (action) or thinking (cognition) — the third axis. Values, self-talk, comparison/envy, confidence, purpose, life-audit, taste.

These prompts assume the user is willing to look honestly at their own evidence (decisions, time, money, feedback) rather than retreat to affirmations or generic frameworks.

## Scope

**In scope:**
- Revealed-vs-stated values
- Self-talk and inner-critic patterns
- Comparison, envy, status-anxiety, FOMO
- Confidence calibration (impostor + overconfidence)
- Purpose / motivation diagnosis
- Life audit at major inflections
- Taste development in a specific domain

**Out of scope (refuse and refer):**
- Clinical mental health (depression, anxiety, eating disorders, addiction, suicidal ideation) → professional support
- Specific financial planning → out of repository
- Medical decisions → physician
- Relationship therapy / partner conflict → relationship counseling

## Composition patterns

These prompts are designed to compose. Common chains:

- **Stuckness route:** `agency_stuck_diagnosis` → category 12 (loss of why) → `identity_purpose_reignition` → may route to `identity_values_clarification` if Pattern 4 (mismatch).
- **Burnout-recovery route:** `agency_burnout_recovery` → if Stage 5 (mismatch) → `identity_values_clarification` + `identity_purpose_reignition`.
- **Big-decision route:** `identity_values_clarification` → `identity_confidence_calibration` (about the capability the decision tests) → run the decision.
- **Inflection route:** `identity_life_audit_reckoning` → routes to whichever dimension surfaced (values, purpose, comparison, work, health).
- **Comparison-spike route:** `identity_comparison_envy_diagnostic` → if Pattern 1/2 signal → may route to `identity_values_clarification` to validate the desire.

## What the prompts refuse

- Affirmations and "you are enough" reassurance
- Vision statements, mission statements, "find your purpose" exercises
- Clinical interpretation
- Generic value labels ("integrity," "growth") without grounding in user evidence
- Multi-step "transformation" plans
