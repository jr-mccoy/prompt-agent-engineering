# Major Decisions (Personal, High-Stakes)

Structured analysis for the small number of personal decisions whose consequences run in years or decades: a job offer, a relocation, quitting something you've invested in, an education program, family planning, a major commitment (marriage/partnership), how to care for an aging parent, a sabbatical or career break, starting a business vs. staying employed, a major financial move, a non-emergency health decision, a major purchase, a cofounder, or a difficult relationship. These decisions share a profile — high stakes, sparse evidence, heavy emotional distortion, and strong biases (sunk cost, anchoring, credential default, romanticized freedom, partnering with people you like) — and each prompt here is built around the specific distortions of its decision type.

These prompts help you make the choice **before** it's committed; they are research and structuring aids, explicitly **not** professional (legal/financial/medical/therapeutic) advice — every one routes its specifics to the relevant professional and leaves the decision with you. For **navigating the change after** a decision is made, see `domain-personal-development/prompts/life-transitions/`.

The prompts are direct without prescribing. They surface the dimensions users systematically under-weight (identity, network, optionality, reversibility, the no-program or do-nothing alternative), force comparison against a BATNA or base case, and end in a structured recommendation or decision frame — but the decision stays with the user. Several draw explicit professional boundaries: the health prompt is research scaffolding ("the user makes the decision with their clinician"), the financial prompt ends with a fee-only-fiduciary smell test rather than giving investment advice, and the relationship audit defers entirely when physical safety is at risk. None of these prompts substitutes for licensed financial, medical, or legal advice.

Users are individuals, couples, and households at a genuine decision point — plus founders, in the cofounder and quit-or-persist cases.

## When to use this domain

- Evaluating a job offer (or competing offers) beyond the compensation line.
- Weighing a move: city, country, neighborhood, or hybrid arrangement.
- Seriously considering quitting a job, business, program, or path you've invested in — and unsure whether the pull to quit is signal or noise.
- Comparing degree programs, bootcamps, certificates, and the no-program alternative against a concrete goal.
- Weighing a major commitment (marriage, long-term partnership, moving in together) on load-bearing compatibility rather than intensity of feeling.
- Deciding how to care for an aging parent (in-home, move-in, assisted living, sibling-shared, professional care).
- Evaluating a sabbatical or extended career break — purpose, runway, and re-entry risk.
- Choosing between starting a business and staying employed, including the staged/reversible middle path.
- A financial decision (mortgage sizing, leverage, concentration, equity-vs-cash) large enough that a wrong answer has multi-year consequences.
- Researching a non-emergency health decision, a major purchase, a prospective cofounder, or a consequential move in a difficult relationship.

## When NOT to use this domain (use a different one)

- **The decision is general/professional rather than personal-life shaped** → `domain-decision-making/` (tradeoff and decisioning prompts) or `domain-deep-analysis/deepthink_decision.md` for the full multi-perspective treatment.
- **You're stuck or unmotivated, not at a decision point** → `domain-personal-development/prompts/agency/` (`agency_stuck_diagnosis.md`, `agency_burnout_recovery.md`).
- **The question is identity (values, purpose, life audit), not a choice between options** → `domain-personal-development/prompts/identity/`.
- **You want a sanity check on reasoning you've already done** → `domain-productivity/validation/` (`validation_am_i_being_nuts.md`, `validation_final_gate.md`).
- **The decision is already made and you're navigating the change** → `domain-personal-development/prompts/life-transitions/` (adjustment to a move, a new role, new parenthood, an empty nest).
- **Negotiating the offer or deal rather than evaluating it** → `domain-negotiation/preparation/negotiation_batna_analysis.md`.
- **Acute crisis, active distress, or safety risk** → not an analysis domain at all; several prompts here explicitly refuse and defer in these conditions.

## How prompts in this domain compose

Prompts here chain laterally when decisions entangle — the `related_prompts` graph encodes the real couplings: career offer ↔ relocation ↔ quit-or-persist (an offer often implies a move and a quit), education ↔ financial framework (tuition and opportunity cost), purchase ↔ financial framework (when the purchase is large enough to be a financial decision), cofounder ↔ relationship audit ↔ quit-or-persist, marriage-commitment ↔ family-planning ↔ financial framework (a commitment is also a joint-finances and a children decision), sabbatical ↔ quit-or-persist ↔ financial framework (a break tests the same money and re-entry math), business-vs-employment ↔ career-offer ↔ quit-or-persist (the leap is scored against the job you'd otherwise take), and aging-parent care ↔ family-planning ↔ financial framework (caregiving reshapes geography, money, and siblings). The commitment, sabbatical, and business prompts each route their "escape vs. toward" cases to `personal_quit_or_persist.md`, and the aging-parent prompt routes its legal/financial/medical specifics to the relevant professional rather than answering them. Nearly every prompt also points at the same `domain-decision-making/` trio for deepening: `tradeoff_reversibility_stakes_grid.md` to size the decision, `tradeoff_multi_criteria_decision_analysis.md` for formal scoring, and `decisioning_regret_minimization.md` for the long-horizon check. Downstream, several recommend documenting the decision with `domain-decision-making/documentation/decisiondoc_options_memo.md` and reviewing it later with `agency_decision_post_mortem.md` (`prompts/agency/`) — quit-or-persist even writes today's calibration anchor specifically for that future review.

## Frontmatter conventions specific to this domain

All prompts carry the machine-readable `reasoning:` block. The domain signature: `stakes: high` across the board, `horizon: years` (decades for family planning), `uncertainty: ambiguity` or `risk`, and `collaboration` reflecting who is actually in the decision (`solo`, `solo_or_pair`, `pair` for family planning and cofounder selection). Two styles recur that are rare elsewhere in the repo: `identity-aware` (relocation, family planning, relationship audit — decisions that change who you are, not just what you have) and `counterfactual` (the BATNA / base-case / fresh-observer machinery). `mode` is consistently `[decide, audit, ...]` — these are pre-decision instruments, and several state explicitly that they are not for decisions already made.
