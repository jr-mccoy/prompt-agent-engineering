# Career Changers Guide

For adults pivoting careers or building a substantially new skill domain — whether through self-directed study, a certificate, a bootcamp, a degree program, or some hybrid. If you're not yet sure whether to pivot at all, work through [`../../../domain-personal-development/career-transformation/`](../../../domain-personal-development/career-transformation/) first; this guide picks up after the strategic decision is made.

## What This Guide Does

It picks up after "I'm going to pivot" and helps you decide *how* — the credentialing route, the self-study structure, the portfolio strategy, and the proof-of-work that lands the destination role. Three of the workflows below depend on net-new prompts in [`../../adult-learner/`](../../adult-learner/).

## Pivot Stages and Which Workflow Fits

| Stage | What you're trying to do | Workflow |
|-------|--------------------------|----------|
| Undecided on credentialing route | Choose between degree, bootcamp, cert, MOOC, OJT, self-study | [`workflow_credential_pathway_decision.md`](workflow_credential_pathway_decision.md) |
| Route chosen, plan needed | Design a 3, 6, or 12-month self-study plan | [`workflow_skill_pivot_self_study_plan.md`](workflow_skill_pivot_self_study_plan.md) |
| Mid-pivot, no public work yet | Build a portfolio while you're still learning | [`workflow_portfolio_while_learning.md`](workflow_portfolio_while_learning.md) |
| Late-pivot, applying for roles | Frame proof-of-work for the pivot context | [`workflow_proof_of_work_for_pivot.md`](workflow_proof_of_work_for_pivot.md) |

The workflows are sequential. Most career changers run them in this order over 9–18 months.

## The Three Common Pivot Failures

This guide is built around preventing the three most common ways adult career pivots fail:

1. **Tutorial hell.** Years spent learning without shipping. Cure: shipping deadlines + portfolio cadence (workflows 2 and 3).
2. **Invisible work.** Building skill in private; expecting the market to find you. Cure: distribution plan in every artifact (workflow 3).
3. **Credential mismatch.** Spending two years and $80K on a degree the target field doesn't actually weight, or skipping credentials in a field that requires them. Cure: credential decision before the time investment (workflow 1).

## Prerequisites

Before starting the workflows, you should have:

- A specific target role or field (not "tech" — but "junior data analyst at mid-size B2B SaaS")
- A few job postings you'd aspire to (paste them into the workflows)
- An honest read on your time and financial constraints
- A reason for the pivot you can articulate beyond "the current job is annoying"

If you don't have these, work through:

## The Stance: Self-Directed, Andragogy-Aware

Career changers are typically self-directed by necessity — you're not enrolled in a structured program (or you are, but it's part-time and the rest is on you). The prompts in this guide assume you can manage your own time, accept honest feedback, and choose between options without being prescribed.

If you want a more structured stance, see [`../adult-returning/GUIDE.md`](../adult-returning/GUIDE.md) — overlap is significant.

## Realistic Time Horizons

Career pivots are slower than the discourse suggests. Approximate timelines for a real adult learner with 10–20 hr/week of pivot time:

| Pivot type | Realistic timeline to first role |
|-----------|--------------------------------|
| Tech-to-tech adjacent (e.g., backend to data engineering) | 6–9 months |
| Non-tech to tech (e.g., marketing to product analytics) | 12–18 months |
| Industry to industry, same function (e.g., retail PM to SaaS PM) | 3–9 months |
| Function to function, same industry | 6–12 months |
| Major life shift (corporate to nursing, finance to teaching) | 18–36 months (often requires formal credentialing) |
| Knowledge-worker to trades | 2–4 years (apprenticeship + cert) |
| Trades to knowledge-worker | 2–5 years (often degree + portfolio) |

Plan for the realistic timeline. Faster pivots happen but aren't the median.

## What's NOT in This Guide

- **Job search tactics** — resumes, interview prep, salary negotiation. Different problem; different domain.
- **Visa / immigration considerations** for international moves
- **Spousal / family conversations** about the financial implications of pivoting — talk with the people involved
- **Specific industry connections in [your city]** — that's networking, which the workflows point to but don't replace
