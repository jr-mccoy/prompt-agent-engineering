**Purpose:** Generate comprehensive, self-contained substitute teacher plans that enable a substitute to run your classroom effectively — covering daily schedule, procedures, lesson activities, student information, and emergency protocols for both planned and emergency absences.

**When to use:** Preparing for a planned absence (PD, appointment, personal day); creating emergency sub plans to keep on file; updating standing substitute binder; leaving plans for a long-term substitute.

**Input needed:**
- Type of absence (emergency/planned)
- Grade level and subject(s)
- Day's schedule
- Current unit/topic
- Class-specific information

---

## Your Input

**Absence Type:** [Emergency (same-day, generic activities) / Planned (specific lessons) / Long-term (multi-day with progression)]
**Grade Level:** [Grade or age range]
**Subject(s):** [If departmentalized, list subject; if self-contained, note "All subjects"]
**Date(s) of Absence:** [Specific date(s) or "Emergency — file plan"]
**Daily Schedule:** [Period times, specials, lunch, recess, transitions]
**Current Topics:** [What each class is working on]
**Activities Prepared:** [Optional — worksheets left, pages to cover, ongoing projects]
**Technology Available:** [Projector, student devices, document camera, none]
**Special Circumstances:** [Optional — field day, assembly, testing week, class party]

---

> **PRIVACY REMINDER:** Include only information a substitute needs to ensure student safety (allergies, medical needs, behavioral plans). Do not include detailed disability information, family situations, or confidential IEP details. Use first names or student numbers only.

## Instructions

Generate the substitute plan through these structured phases:

### Step 1: Quick Reference Cover Sheet

Create a one-page overview the substitute sees first:

```
╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║  SUBSTITUTE TEACHER PLAN                         ║
║  Teacher: [Name]           Date: [Date]          ║
║  Grade/Subject: [Grade/Subject]                  ║
║  Room: [Room #]                                  ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  KEY CONTACTS:                                   ║
║  • Neighbor teacher (emergencies): [Name, Room]  ║
║  • Office: [Extension/Room]                      ║
║  • Admin on duty: [Name]                         ║
║  • Grade-level lead: [Name, Room]                ║
║                                                  ║
║  SCHEDULE AT A GLANCE:                           ║
║  [Time] — [Subject/Activity]                     ║
║  [Time] — [Subject/Activity]                     ║
║  [Time] — Lunch (Room [X], duty [Y/N])           ║
║  [Time] — [Subject/Activity]                     ║
║  [Time] — Dismissal (procedure: [bus/walker/etc])║
║                                                  ║
║  FIRE DRILL EXIT: [Route]                        ║
║  LOCKDOWN PROCEDURE: [Location in room]          ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════╝
```

### Step 2: Classroom Procedures

**Arrival & Morning Routine:**
- [What students do upon entering — unpack, morning work, attendance]
- [Where morning work is located]
- [How attendance is taken — system name, paper backup]
- [Lunch count procedure if applicable]

**Classroom Rules & Expectations:**

**End of Day:**
- [Pack-up time and procedure]

### Step 4: Lesson Plans by Period/Subject

**For Each Period/Subject Block:**

**[Time] — [Subject]**

| Element | Details |
|---------|---------|
| **Learning target** | [Simple, clear objective] |
| **Materials location** | [Exact location — "on my desk," "in the blue bin," "loaded on computers"] |
| **Procedure** | [Step-by-step instructions — numbered, specific] |
| **Key instructions to students** | [What to say verbatim if needed] |
| **Early finishers** | [What to do if done early] |
| **Collect or not** | [Whether to collect work and where to put it] |

**Procedure Detail Level:**
```
1. Have students take out [material] from [location].
2. Read aloud the instructions on the board / top of worksheet.
3. Students work [independently / in pairs / in groups of X].
4. Circulate to check progress. Look for [specific thing].
5. With 5 minutes left, have students [clean up / share / turn in].
6. Collect work in the [location — tray, folder, basket].
```

### Step 5: Emergency/Backup Activities

For unplanned absences or if lessons finish early, include self-contained activities:

**Activity Bank (no prep needed):**

| Activity | Duration | Materials | Subject | Instructions |
|----------|----------|-----------|---------|-------------|
| Silent reading + journal response | 20-30 min | Classroom library + lined paper | ELA | "Read silently. Write 3 sentences about what happened in your book." |
| Math fact practice sheet | 15-20 min | [Location of copies] | Math | Self-explanatory worksheet |
| Drawing and labeling activity | 20-30 min | Blank paper, pencils | Any | "Draw and label 5 things you learned about [current topic]." |
| Write a letter to your teacher | 15-20 min | Lined paper | Writing | "Write me a letter about your day. Tell me what you learned and what went well." |
| Word search / crossword on topic | 15-20 min | [Location of copies] | Any | Self-explanatory |

### Step 6: Notes for the Teacher

**Please leave me a note about:**
- [ ] How the day went overall
- [ ] Which activities were completed
- [ ] Any behavior issues (student name and what happened)
- [ ] Any student who was absent
- [ ] Any visitors, calls, or messages received
- [ ] Anything else I should know

**Leave notes:** [Location — on my desk, in my mailbox, email to X]

## Output Format

Structure the substitute plan as:
1. **Quick Reference Cover Sheet** (one page, at-a-glance)
2. **Classroom Procedures** (routines, rules, transitions)
3. **Student Need-to-Know** (medical, behavioral, pull-outs, helpers)
4. **Period-by-Period Lesson Plans** (detailed, step-by-step)
5. **Emergency Activity Bank** (backup plans)
6. **Sub Feedback Form** (end-of-day notes template)

---

## Quality Indicators

**Effective substitute plans include:**
- [ ] A substitute with no prior knowledge of your classroom can follow them
- [ ] Emergency/safety information is immediately visible
- [ ] Step-by-step procedures (not "do the usual routine")
- [ ] Materials locations are specific ("blue bin on the counter" not "in the room")
- [ ] Activities are self-contained (don't require sub to teach new content)
- [ ] Student information is limited to safety-relevant need-to-know
- [ ] Backup activities included for every time block
- [ ] Sub feedback template provided

**Common Substitute Plan Pitfalls:**

| Pitfall | Sign | Prevention |
|---------|------|------------|
| **Assumed knowledge** | "Follow the normal schedule" | Spell out every routine step explicitly |
| **Missing materials** | "Use the worksheet" — but where is it? | State exact location of every material |
| **No backup plan** | Students finish early with nothing to do | Include emergency activities for every block |
| **Over-ambitious lessons** | Sub expected to teach new content | Leave practice/review activities, not new instruction |
| **No behavior system** | Sub has no tools for management | Include attention signal, rules, and consequence sequence |
| **Incomplete schedule** | Missing specials, recess, or transitions | Account for every minute of the day |
