# Career Changers: Cross-Domain Prompt Kit

Career changers need prompts from at least 7 of the repo's domains. This kit collects the ones that actually show up during a pivot, organized by when you'll need them.

## Before the Pivot — Strategic

Before committing to a pivot, validate the strategic call:

| Prompt | What it does | When |
|--------|--------------|------|
| [`../../../../domain-personal-development/career-transformation/career_role_structural_vulnerability.md`](../../../../domain-personal-development/career-transformation/career_role_structural_vulnerability.md) | Assesses risk to your current role from automation, market shifts, structural changes | Year before considering a pivot |
| [`../../../../domain-personal-development/career-transformation/career_residual_skills_inventory.md`](../../../../domain-personal-development/career-transformation/career_residual_skills_inventory.md) | Inventories what transfers and what doesn't | Before deciding on a target field |
| [`../../../../domain-personal-development/career-transformation/career_coordination_tax_audit.md`](../../../../domain-personal-development/career-transformation/career_coordination_tax_audit.md) | Audits how much of your current week is coordination overhead vs. real work | Before assuming you're "too busy to pivot" |
| [`../../../../domain-personal-development/career-transformation/career_90_day_repositioning_plan.md`](../../../../domain-personal-development/career-transformation/career_90_day_repositioning_plan.md) | Builds a 90-day plan to reposition within or out of your current role | Once the pivot direction is clearer |

## Burnout (Real)

If the pivot is breaking you:

[`../../../../domain-personal-development/prompts/agency/agency_burnout_recovery.md`](../../../../domain-personal-development/prompts/agency/agency_burnout_recovery.md) — stages burnout; helps choose between push, rest, retreat, restructure.

If you're in clinical distress, no prompt is the right tool. Talk to a person.

## Sample Sequence for a 12-Month Pivot

Month 0 (decide):
- `career_role_structural_vulnerability.md`
- `career_residual_skills_inventory.md`
- `identity_values_clarification.md`
- `adult_credential_pathway_decision.md` (in this guide)

Month 1 (set up):
- `adult_skill_pivot_self_study_plan.md`
- `adult_working_learner_time_architecture.md`
- `agency_foundation_session.md`
- `deepwork_focus_parameters_estimator.md`

Months 2–9 (execute):
- Weekly: `agency_weekly_review.md` + `reviews_weekly_systems_review.md`
- Monthly: `bottleneck_locator.md` + `agency_proof_of_work_portfolio.md`
- As needed: `agency_stuck_diagnosis.md`, `bottleneck_procrastination_systems_diagnostic.md`, `identity_confidence_calibration.md`

Months 10–12 (apply):
- `adult_prior_learning_articulation.md` (in this guide)
- `agency_proof_of_work_portfolio.md`
- `agency_ship_sprint_design.md` for the application sprint
- `agency_feedback_extraction.md` for every response

Month 13+ (transition):
- `career_90_day_repositioning_plan.md`
- `agency_foundation_session.md` (re-run)

This is what the cross-domain reality looks like in calendar form.
