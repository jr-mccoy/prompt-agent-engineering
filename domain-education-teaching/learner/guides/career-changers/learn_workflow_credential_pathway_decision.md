# Workflow: Credential Pathway Decision

## Who This Is For

- Adult committed to pivoting but undecided on the credentialing route
- Looking at multiple options (a master's vs. a bootcamp vs. self-study, etc.)
- Tempted to default to "get a degree" without checking whether the field actually weights one
- Or tempted to default to "self-study, the degree is overrated" without checking whether the field actually allows that path

## What You'll Have at the End

A specific credential pathway recommendation with the reasoning shown — including the field's actual hiring signal weights, the opportunity cost math, and a counter-case with observable failure signals if the recommendation is wrong.

## What You Need to Bring

- Target role / field (specific — paste 2–3 job postings)
- Current role, income, family situation, financial budget
- Time horizon (when do you want to be employed in the target role)
- Existing related skills and credentials
- Geographic / regulatory constraints (some pathways are region-specific)

## The Chain

### Step 1 — Pre-work: Confirm the pivot is the right move (skip if already done)

**Prompts:**
- [`../../../domain-personal-development/career-transformation/career_role_structural_vulnerability.md`](../../../domain-personal-development/career-transformation/career_role_structural_vulnerability.md) — assess your current role's risk
- [`../../../domain-personal-development/career-transformation/career_residual_skills_inventory.md`](../../../domain-personal-development/career-transformation/career_residual_skills_inventory.md) — inventory what transfers

**Skip if:** you've already done strategic pivot work and just need the credential decision.

**Time:** 1–2 hours

---

### Step 2 — Run the credential pathway decision prompt

**Prompt:** [`../../adult-learner/adult_credential_pathway_decision.md`](../../adult-learner/adult_credential_pathway_decision.md)

**Input:** target role, postings, current situation, time horizon, financial constraints

**What you'll get:**
- Target field hiring signal map (what employers actually weight)
- Pathway-by-pathway diagnostic (degree, cert, bootcamp, MOOC, OJT, self-study)
- Specific recommendation with reasoning
- Opportunity cost math
- Counter-case with observable failure signals

**Carry forward:** the recommended pathway and the criteria for knowing it's working

**Time:** 60–90 minutes

---

### Step 3 — Stress-test the recommendation (Optional but recommended)

Before committing money or time, do two reality checks:

**Check A — 3 conversations.** Talk to 3 people in the target field who took the recommended pathway. Ask: "Is this pathway still working for new entrants? If you were starting today, would you pick the same path?" 30 minutes each.

**Check B — Adversarial:**
- `../../../domain-decision-making/decisioning_blind_spot_identifier.md` — run your recommendation through it

**Time:** 2–3 hours over 1–2 weeks

---

### Step 4 — Commit the decision

Document:
- The pathway chosen
- The reasoning
- The cost (financial + time + opportunity)
- The signal you'll watch for that says the pathway is working
- The signal you'll watch for that says it's not
- The pivot you'd make if it stops working

This document goes in a notes file you re-read every 60–90 days.

## Time Budget

| Step | Time |
|------|-----:|
| Pre-work (if needed) | 1–2 hr |
| Decision prompt | 1–1.5 hr |
| Stress test | 2–3 hr |
| Commit | 30 min |
| **Total** | **4.5–7 hr** |

Spread over 1–3 weeks. Don't rush a decision that costs $20K+ or 12+ months.

## Common Failure Modes

| Failure | What to do |
|---------|-----------|
| Defaulted to a master's because it felt "safest" | The decision prompt explicitly identifies fields where degrees don't move the needle. Re-run with honest financial input. |
| Defaulted to "the cheapest option" without checking signal | Cheap pathways that produce no signal cost you 12 months. Run the field signal map. |
| Decided based on someone else's pivot story | Their pathway worked for their profile. The decision prompt is calibrated to yours. |
| Ignored opportunity cost (income forgone) | The math section forces this. If you ignored it, re-do that section. |
| Decision keeps shifting week to week | Decide and commit for 60 days. Re-evaluate then with data, not more thinking. |

## After the Decision

Once the credential pathway is chosen, the next workflow depends on which pathway:

- **Self-study** → [`workflow_skill_pivot_self_study_plan.md`](workflow_skill_pivot_self_study_plan.md)
- **Bootcamp** → enroll; supplement with self-study plan during; portfolio workflow in parallel
- **Degree** → enroll; supplement with portfolio workflow during
- **Cert** → study for the exam; pair with portfolio workflow
- **OJT** → negotiate the lateral; portfolio workflow during

In all cases, the portfolio workflow ([`workflow_portfolio_while_learning.md`](workflow_portfolio_while_learning.md)) runs in parallel.
