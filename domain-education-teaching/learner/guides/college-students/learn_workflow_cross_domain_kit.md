# College Students: Cross-Domain Prompt Kit

Academic-task prompts cover the work itself. This kit covers everything around it: time, focus, motivation, stuckness, confidence, identity. These prompts live in other domains of the repo but show up constantly for college students.

## When to Use Which

| Situation | Prompt |
|-----------|--------|
| Calendar is full but you have no idea where the time goes | [Calendar audit](#calendar-and-focus) |
| Multi-week project (paper, lab report, capstone) needs to be scheduled | [Project chunking](#calendar-and-focus) |
| Procrastinating on a specific task and "just do it" isn't working | [Procrastination diagnostic](#bottlenecks-and-execution) |
| Perfectionism stopping you from shipping the essay | [Ship threshold](#bottlenecks-and-execution) |
| Stuck and can't tell why | [Stuck diagnosis](#stuckness-and-execution) |
| Comparing yourself to classmates and feeling behind | [Comparison/envy diagnostic](#identity-and-confidence) |
| Impostor feelings | [Confidence calibration](#identity-and-confidence) |
| Weekly check-in to keep the semester from drifting | [Weekly review](#review-and-cadence) |
| You shipped a paper and got the grade back — now what | [Feedback extraction](#after-shipping) |

## Burnout (Real, Not Just Tired)

| Prompt | What it does | When to run |
|--------|--------------|-------------|
| [`../../../../domain-personal-development/prompts/agency/agency_burnout_recovery.md`](../../../../domain-personal-development/prompts/agency/agency_burnout_recovery.md) | Stages burnout and picks recovery path | When productivity work has stopped helping and you've been running on empty 3+ weeks |

**If you're in actual mental health distress, no prompt is the right tool.** Use your school's counseling services. The prompts handle academic and workflow problems; they don't replace clinical care.

## Putting It Together: A Sample Week

A typical semester week using this kit:

- **Sunday evening:** `reviews_weekly_systems_review.md` (20 min) — close last week, set up this one
- **Sunday evening:** `deepwork_match_tasks_to_calendar.md` (15 min) — match what's due to what hours you have
- **Mid-week, if stuck:** `agency_stuck_diagnosis.md` (15 min) — identify what's actually blocking you
- **After a major submission:** `agency_feedback_extraction.md` (20 min) when grade comes back
- **End of focus block:** `deepwork_block_end_context_capture.md` (5 min) — leave breadcrumbs for tomorrow

Total cross-domain time: ~75 min/week. Often the highest-leverage 75 minutes you spend.
