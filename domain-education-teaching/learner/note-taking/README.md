# Note-Taking & Capture

*4 prompts · part of [`domain-education-teaching`](../../README.md)*

## Use these when

Turning source material into study material — a lecture into a study guide, a textbook chapter into a structure, raw notes into Cornell format or active-recall questions. This is the first step of the study loop; `memory-and-recall/` is the second.

## Go elsewhere when

- **You already have study material and need to memorize it** → [`../memory-and-recall/`](../memory-and-recall/)
- **Reading comprehension rather than capture** → [`../reading/`](../reading/)
