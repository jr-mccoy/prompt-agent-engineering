# Self-Assessment

*8 prompts · part of [`domain-education-teaching`](../../README.md)*

## Use these when

Checking yourself honestly and acting on what the check reveals: self-quiz loops, confidence calibration (the gap between feeling ready and being ready), mistake logs, error-correction cycles, weak-point diagnostics, and turning wrong answers into a study plan.

## Go elsewhere when

- **Practising recall rather than testing it** → [`../memory-and-recall/`](../memory-and-recall/)
- **You do not understand it yet at all** → [`../stuck-and-confused/`](../stuck-and-confused/)
- **A graded exam is imminent** → [`../exam-prep/`](../exam-prep/)
