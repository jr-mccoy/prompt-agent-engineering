# Tutoring — Teach Me This

*9 prompts · part of [`domain-education-teaching`](../../README.md)*

## Use these when

Forward instruction: you want to understand something you do not yet understand. Socratic tutoring that refuses to hand over answers, worked-example fading, stepwise problem coaching, adaptive mastery loops, and building a topic from scratch. All hold the Socratic stance — they coach, they do not produce submittable work.

## Go elsewhere when

- **You already learned it and got it wrong** → [`../stuck-and-confused/`](../stuck-and-confused/)
- **You understand it and need to memorize it** → [`../memory-and-recall/`](../memory-and-recall/)
- **You are the teacher, not the learner** → [`../../instructor/explanation-craft/`](../../instructor/explanation-craft/)
