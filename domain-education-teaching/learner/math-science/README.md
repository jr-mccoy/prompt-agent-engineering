# Maths & Science Coach

*7 prompts · part of [`domain-education-teaching`](../../README.md)*

## Use these when

Working your own quantitative problems: word problem decoding, Socratic step-by-step solving, analysing errors in your own working, lab report scaffolding, data interpretation, experimental design, and science concept maps. These never hand over the final answer.

## Go elsewhere when

- **Drilling for fluency rather than working a problem** → [`../study-by-discipline/`](../study-by-discipline/)
- **Stuck on one step specifically** → [`../stuck-and-confused/`](../stuck-and-confused/)
- **Real research design** → [`../../../domain-science/`](../../../domain-science/)
