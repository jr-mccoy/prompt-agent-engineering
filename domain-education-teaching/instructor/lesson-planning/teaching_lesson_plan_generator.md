## Instructions

Generate a comprehensive lesson plan through these structured phases:

### Band Adaptation Matrix (Apply before Step 1)

Use the selected **Learning Band** to set default constraints for language, pacing, workload, and evidence of learning.

| Band | Vocabulary Ceiling | Cognitive Load Expectations | Timing Norms | Assessment Artifact Defaults |
|------|--------------------|-----------------------------|--------------|------------------------------|
| **K-2** | Concrete high-frequency words; max 1-2 new academic terms per lesson with visuals/gestures | Single-step to short two-step directions; heavy modeling; working-memory-light tasks | 5-10 minute activity chunks; movement/reset every 10-15 minutes; total direct instruction <=10 minutes at a time | Oral check + drawing/labeling + teacher observation checklist; optional 1-question exit prompt |
| **3-5** | Tier 1 + targeted Tier 2/3 terms (3-5 new terms) with student-friendly definitions | Two-step directions; guided practice before independent work; limited simultaneous demands | 10-15 minute chunks; direct instruction 10-15 minutes followed by application; closure 5 minutes | Short constructed response, graphic organizer, or quick quiz (3-5 items) with success criteria |
| **6-8** | Grade-appropriate discipline vocabulary (4-6 new terms) with morphology/context supports | Multi-step tasks with scaffolds; increasing abstraction; collaborative reasoning expected | 12-20 minute phases; direct instruction <=15 minutes before active processing | Exit ticket (2-4 items), claim-evidence reasoning response, or annotated product sample |
| **9-12** | Course-specific academic vocabulary (5-8 terms) and precise technical usage | Sustained analysis/synthesis; independent task management with periodic check-ins | 15-25 minute segments; seminar/lab/workshop blocks; independent work windows 20-35 minutes | Analytical response, lab/product artifact, or standards-aligned mini performance task with rubric |
| **Higher-ed** | Discipline/professional lexicon with threshold concepts explicitly framed | High intrinsic load acceptable; emphasize transfer, critique, and metacognition | 20-40 minute modules; blended lecture-application cycles; asynchronous prep assumed when relevant | Brief applied deliverable (memo/problem set/discussion post) plus criteria-based scoring guide |
| **Adult learning** | Job-embedded/plain-language-first; introduce technical terms only when task-relevant | Problem-centered, immediately applicable, autonomy-forward; leverage prior experience | 15-30 minute practical cycles; flexible pacing; chunk for work/life constraints | Performance demonstration, workplace artifact, reflection-to-action plan, or competency checklist |

**Delivery Mode Rules (from Context Profile):**
- **In-person:** Include room routines, transition cues, and live interaction protocols.
- **Hybrid:** Split tasks into face-to-face essentials vs. online follow-through; specify handoff points.
- **Online asynchronous:** Replace live talk moves with written/video prompts, explicit deadlines, and independent check-for-understanding checkpoints.

| 3 | SWBAT [objective] | [Level] | [How measured] |

**Bloom's Taxonomy Levels:**
- Remember: list, define, identify, recall, name
- Understand: explain, summarize, classify, compare, describe
- Apply: use, demonstrate, solve, implement, execute
- Analyze: differentiate, examine, compare, contrast, organize
- Evaluate: judge, justify, critique, defend, assess
- Create: design, develop, construct, produce, formulate

## Output Format

Structure the complete lesson plan as:
1. **Lesson Overview** (1-page summary)
2. **Standards & Objectives**
3. **Materials List**
4. **Detailed Procedure** (5E format with times)
5. **Differentiation Strategies**
6. **Assessment Plan**
7. **Student Handouts** (if applicable)
8. **Reflection Template**

## Quality Indicators

**Effective lesson plans include:**
- [ ] Measurable, student-centered objectives
- [ ] Standards alignment documented
- [ ] Realistic time allocations with buffers
- [ ] Active student engagement (not just lecture)
- [ ] Multiple check-for-understanding points
- [ ] Differentiation for diverse learners
- [ ] Assessment aligned to objectives
- [ ] Materials list complete and prepared

**Common Lesson Planning Pitfalls:**

| Pitfall | Sign | Prevention |
|---------|------|------------|
| **Time crunch** | Always rushing at end | Build in buffer, practice timing |
| **Over-lecture** | Students passive >15 min | Add interactive elements |
| **Assessment mismatch** | Testing what wasn't taught | Design assessment first |
| **No differentiation** | Same activity for all | Include scaffolds and extensions |
| **Unclear objectives** | "Students will understand..." | Use measurable verbs |
