**Purpose:** Design systematic vocabulary instruction plans that build academic language across content areas — including tiered word selection, student-friendly definitions, multi-modal learning activities, ELL scaffolding, and vocabulary assessment aligned to how words are actually used in the discipline.

**When to use:** Introducing vocabulary for a new unit; building academic language for content-area literacy; supporting ELL students with subject-specific language; creating vocabulary routines for daily use; designing vocabulary assessments that go beyond matching definitions.

**Input needed:**
- Subject area and topic/unit
- Grade level
- List of vocabulary words (or topic for word generation)
- ELL support needed

## Instructions

Design vocabulary instruction through these structured phases:

### Step 1: Select and Tier the Words

**Tiered Vocabulary Framework (Beck, McKeown, & Kucan):**

| Tier | Definition | Action | Examples |
|------|-----------|--------|----------|
| **Tier 1** | Basic, everyday words most students know | Don't teach formally (unless ELL students need them) | happy, run, house, eat |
| **Tier 2** | High-utility academic words used across subjects | **Priority for instruction** — highest ROI | analyze, compare, significant, evidence, justify, perspective |
| **Tier 3** | Domain-specific technical vocabulary | **Teach in context** of the subject unit | photosynthesis, denominator, imperialism, alliteration |

**Word Selection Matrix:**

| Word | Tier | Why Selected | Frequency of Use | Conceptual Difficulty | Priority |
|------|------|-------------|-----------------|----------------------|----------|
| [Word] | [2 or 3] | [Where/how students will encounter this word] | [How often it appears in upcoming texts/tasks] | [Low / Medium / High] | [High / Medium / Low] |

**Selection Criteria:**
- [ ] Words appear in upcoming texts, activities, or assessments
- [ ] Words are useful beyond this single unit (especially Tier 2)
- [ ] Words represent concepts, not just labels
- [ ] List includes a manageable number (5-10 per week)
- [ ] Cognates identified for ELL students (words similar in other languages)

### Step 2: Build Student-Friendly Definitions

**Definition Format:**

| Word | Student-Friendly Definition | Context Sentence | Visual/Gesture | Related Words |
|------|---------------------------|-----------------|----------------|---------------|
| [Word] | [In everyday language, starting with the function: "Something that..." / "When you..." / "A way to..."] | [Sentence from the unit using the word naturally] | [Image suggestion or physical gesture] | [Synonyms, antonyms, word family members] |

**Definition Quality Rules:**
- Use everyday language (define the word, don't use the word in its own definition)
- Start with the word's function or category ("A type of..." / "When someone..." / "The process of...")
- Keep to 1-2 sentences maximum
- Avoid circular definitions ("Photosynthesis is the synthesis of...")
- Include an example or non-example when helpful

**Example:**
| Word | Poor Definition | Strong Definition |
|------|----------------|-------------------|
| erosion | "The wearing away of the earth's surface by natural forces" | "When wind, water, or ice slowly wears away rock and soil over time — like how a river carves a canyon" |
| analyze | "To examine methodically" | "When you break something into parts and look at each part carefully to understand how it works" |

### Step 3: Design Multi-Modal Activities

**Daily Vocabulary Routine (choose 1-2 per day, rotate across the week):**

| Day | Activity | Time | Modality | Description |
|-----|----------|------|----------|-------------|
| Mon | **Word Introduction** | 10 min | Visual + Verbal | Teacher introduces words with definition, image, gesture, and context sentence. Students record in vocabulary journal. |
| Tue | **Word Sort** | 5-10 min | Kinesthetic + Cognitive | Students sort words by category, part of speech, concept, or relationship. |
| Wed | **Vocabulary Sketch** | 10 min | Visual + Written | Students draw a quick sketch representing each word (Frayer Model or Sketch & Label). |
| Thu | **Use It or Lose It** | 5-10 min | Verbal + Written | Students use words in sentences, partner conversations, or quick writes. |
| Fri | **Review Game** | 10-15 min | Social + Kinesthetic | Vocabulary Bingo, Jeopardy, Around the World, or Kahoot-style quiz. |

**Activity Bank by Engagement Type:**

**Visual Activities:**
| Activity | Description | Time | Grade Band |
|----------|-------------|------|-----------|
| Frayer Model | Four quadrants: Definition, Characteristics, Examples, Non-examples | 10 min | 2-12 |
| Vocabulary Sketch | Quick drawing + label for each word | 5-10 min | K-8 |
| Word Wall | Display words with images; interactive (students can add connections) | Ongoing | K-8 |
| Semantic Map | Web showing word relationships, synonyms, categories | 10-15 min | 3-12 |

**Kinesthetic Activities:**
| Activity | Description | Time | Grade Band |
|----------|-------------|------|-----------|
| Vocabulary Charades | Act out words; classmates guess | 10 min | K-8 |
| Word Sort (physical cards) | Sort words into teacher- or student-created categories | 5-10 min | 1-8 |
| Gallery Walk | Post definitions around room; students match words | 10 min | 2-12 |
| Total Physical Response | Assign a gesture to each word; teacher says word, students do gesture | 5 min | K-5 |

**Verbal/Written Activities:**
| Activity | Description | Time | Grade Band |
|----------|-------------|------|-----------|
| Think-Pair-Share | "Use [word] in a sentence. Share with your partner." | 5 min | K-12 |
| Sentence Stems | "The [word] is important because..." / "[Word] is like [analogy] because..." | 5-10 min | 1-8 |
| Quick Write | "Write 3 sentences using at least 2 vocabulary words." | 5-10 min | 3-12 |
| Vocabulary Conversation | Partners discuss a question that requires using target words | 5 min | 2-12 |

**Digital Activities:**
| Activity | Description | Time | Grade Band |
|----------|-------------|------|-----------|
| Quizlet/Blooket | Digital flashcards and games | 10-15 min | 3-12 |
| Vocabulary Video | Students create 30-second explanation video for a word | 15-20 min | 4-12 |
| Digital Word Wall | Collaborative online document (Padlet, Jamboard) | Ongoing | 3-12 |

### Step 4: ELL Vocabulary Scaffolding

**Scaffolds by WIDA Level:**

| WIDA Level | Vocabulary Scaffolds |
|-----------|---------------------|
| **Level 1 — Entering** | Visual vocabulary cards with L1 translation, gestures, real objects/realia. Label classroom items. Picture dictionaries. |
| **Level 2 — Emerging** | Visual + simple English definition. Word banks for all activities. Sentence frames: "[Word] means ___." |
| **Level 3 — Developing** | Student-friendly definitions. Context sentences. Graphic organizers. Encourage using words in speech with support. |
| **Level 4 — Expanding** | Focus on Tier 2 academic vocabulary. Multiple meaning words. Collocations (words that go together). Less scaffolding needed. |
| **Level 5 — Bridging** | Nuanced usage, register, connotation. Discipline-specific language patterns. Figurative language. |

**Cognate Identification:**
| English Word | Spanish Cognate | French Cognate | Other |
|-------------|----------------|----------------|-------|
| [Word] | [Cognate or "—"] | [Cognate or "—"] | [Other language cognates relevant to your students] |

**Note:** False cognates (words that look similar but mean different things) should be flagged. Example: "embarazada" in Spanish looks like "embarrassed" but means "pregnant."

### Step 5: Vocabulary Assessment

**Assessment Types (from least to most rigorous):**

| Type | Format | What It Measures | Best For |
|------|--------|------------------|----------|
| **Recognition** | Multiple choice / matching | Can student identify the definition? | Quick check, low stakes |
| **Production** | Fill-in-the-blank with word bank | Can student select the right word in context? | Formative, weekly quiz |
| **Application** | Use word correctly in original sentence | Can student use the word independently? | Deeper understanding |
| **Transfer** | Use word in new context or explain to someone else | Can student apply the word flexibly? | Summative, mastery check |

**Recommended: Multi-Format Assessment**

```
Vocabulary Assessment — [Unit Name]

Part A: Matching (Recognition — 5 points)
Match each word to its definition.

Part B: Fill in the Blank (Production — 5 points)
Choose the correct word to complete each sentence.
Word Bank: [words]

Part C: Use It (Application — 5 points)
Write an original sentence for each word that shows you understand its meaning.
(Bonus: Use 2+ vocabulary words in one sentence correctly.)

Part D: Explain It (Transfer — 5 points)
Choose 2 words. Explain each in your own words as if you were teaching
a younger student. Include an example.
```

## Output Format

Structure the vocabulary instruction plan as:
1. **Word List with Tiers** (selected and prioritized words)
2. **Student-Friendly Definitions** (with context, visuals, and related words)
3. **Weekly Activity Plan** (daily routine with specific activities)
4. **ELL Scaffolding Guide** (by proficiency level)
5. **Assessment** (formative and summative options)
6. **Student Materials** (vocabulary journal template, graphic organizers)

## Quality Indicators

**Effective vocabulary instruction includes:**
- [ ] Words are tiered and prioritized (not just "all the bold words in the chapter")
- [ ] Definitions are student-friendly (not dictionary copied)
- [ ] Multiple exposures across multiple modalities (not just "look up and write the definition")
- [ ] Words are used in authentic contexts (not just memorized in isolation)
- [ ] Assessment requires application, not just matching
- [ ] ELL scaffolds provided at appropriate proficiency levels
- [ ] Vocabulary connects to broader unit concepts
- [ ] Students encounter words at least 6-10 times before assessment

**Common Vocabulary Instruction Pitfalls:**

| Pitfall | Sign | Prevention |
|---------|------|------------|
| **Too many words** | 20+ words per week, students overwhelmed | Limit to 5-10 high-priority words |
| **Dictionary definition only** | "Look it up and write it down" | Provide student-friendly definitions with context |
| **One exposure** | Introduce Monday, test Friday | Build daily routines with multiple encounters |
| **Matching-only assessment** | Recognition without application | Include sentences, explanations, and transfer tasks |
| **No context** | Words taught in isolation from content | Embed words in unit readings and activities |
| **All Tier 3, no Tier 2** | Only teaching technical terms | Include high-utility academic words that transfer across subjects |
