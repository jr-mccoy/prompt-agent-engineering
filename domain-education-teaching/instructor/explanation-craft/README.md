# Explanation Craft

*9 prompts · part of [`domain-education-teaching`](../../README.md)*

## Use these when

You know what to teach and need it to actually land. These build the explanation itself — analogies, stories, spatial memory structures, age-pitched language, vocabulary scaffolds, and Socratic seminar design. Three prompts are pitched by age band (kids 5–10, teens 11–17, adults); pick by your learners, not your subject.
