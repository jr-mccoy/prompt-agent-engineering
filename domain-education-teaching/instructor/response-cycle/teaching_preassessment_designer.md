**Purpose:** Build high-leverage pre-assessments that align to unit objectives, reveal student readiness, classify misconceptions, and produce an immediate instructional response plan.

**When to use:** Before a new lesson or unit; when planning differentiation; before intervention cycles; when teams need baseline evidence for PLC discussions.

**Input needed:**
- Learning objective(s) and standards
- Grade/subject and class context
- Time available for pre-assessment
- Known prior misconceptions (if any)

---

## Your Input

**Grade Level / Course:** [e.g., Grade 6 Math, Biology I, AP U.S. History]  
**Unit / Lesson Focus:** [Topic]  
**Learning Objective(s):** [SWBAT statements]  
**Standards:** [Codes + short text]  
**Assessment Time Box:** [e.g., 12 minutes, 20 minutes]  
**Item Constraints:** [e.g., 8 items max, no calculator, mixed modality]  
**Known Misconceptions:** [Optional]  
**Student Population Notes:** [ELL, IEP, advanced learners, attendance patterns]

---

## Constraints

**Must:**
- Anchor every item to a stated learning objective and intended evidence type.
- Include at least one item that surfaces each major misconception category relevant to the objective.
- Keep total pre-assessment time within the stated Assessment Time Box.
- Provide misconception codes per item so scoring yields diagnostic, not just right/wrong, data.
- Define a measurable proficiency cut and reassessment window before finishing the plan.

**Must Not:**
- Do not test content outside the stated objectives or standards.
- Do not rely solely on selected-response items — include at least one short justification/explanation prompt.
- Do not produce an instructional response plan without explicit grouping, time blocks, and CFU mechanisms.
- Do not invent student data, demographics, or class history not provided in the inputs.
- Do not skip the alignment blueprint step, even when time pressure is high.

---

## Instructions

Design the pre-assessment using these phases:

### Step 1: Objective Alignment Blueprint

Create an alignment table before writing items:

| Objective | Standard | Evidence Needed | Cognitive Level | Success Threshold |
|-----------|----------|-----------------|-----------------|-------------------|
| [SWBAT...] | [Code] | [What students must demonstrate] | [Bloom/Webb DOK] | [e.g., 80% accurate] |

**Alignment rule:** Every item must map to one objective and intended evidence type.

### Step 2: Misconception Taxonomy

Build a misconception map for the target content:

| Taxonomy Category | Misconception Pattern | Likely Cause | Diagnostic Signal |
|------------------|-----------------------|--------------|-------------------|
| **Conceptual** | [Incorrect big idea] | [Faulty mental model] | [Error pattern in reasoning] |
| **Procedural** | [Incorrect process] | [Algorithm confusion] | [Step-level error] |
| **Language/Vocabulary** | [Term confusion] | [Academic language gap] | [Misread prompt/term swap] |
| **Transfer/Application** | [Fails in context] | [Cannot generalize] | [Correct in isolation, wrong in scenario] |

If known misconceptions are provided, include them explicitly.

### Step 3: Pre-Assessment Construction

Design a compact assessment that fits the time limit:

| Item # | Objective Match | Item Type | Prompt Summary | Misconception(s) Targeted | Est. Time |
|--------|-----------------|-----------|----------------|---------------------------|-----------|
| 1 | [Objective] | [MCQ/Short response/Sort] | [Prompt] | [Taxonomy tags] | [min] |

**Design constraints:**
- Include at least one item that diagnoses each major misconception category relevant to the objective.
- Include at least one item requiring brief explanation/justification (not only selected response).
- Keep total estimated time within the Assessment Time Box.

### Step 4: Scoring + Rapid Analysis Protocol

Provide a quick-score system:

| Item | Correct Criteria | Misconception Code if Incorrect | Instructional Meaning |
|------|------------------|---------------------------------|-----------------------|
| 1 | [Criteria] | [e.g., C1, P2, L1] | [What this error implies] |

Then provide a class-level interpretation matrix:

| Pattern | Threshold | Interpretation | Immediate Response |
|---------|-----------|----------------|--------------------|
| [e.g., >40% C1 errors] | [cut score] | [Conceptual gap] | [Reteach move] |

### Step 5: Time-Boxed Instructional Response Plan

Build the immediate follow-up plan:

| Time Block | Grouping | Activity | Objective Focus | Misconception Focus | Evidence Check |
|------------|----------|----------|-----------------|---------------------|----------------|
| 0-10 min | Whole class | [Clarify model] | [Objective] | [Codes] | [CFU] |
| 10-25 min | Small groups | [Targeted practice] | [Objective] | [Codes] | [Exit prompt] |

### Step 6: Success Criteria for Next Reassessment

Define measurable readiness criteria for the next check:

| Criterion | Measure | Proficiency Cut | Reassessment Format | Reassessment Window |
|----------|---------|-----------------|---------------------|---------------------|
| [Skill/understanding] | [Item/rubric score] | [e.g., 4/5 students in group score ≥3/4] | [Mini-quiz/exit ticket] | [e.g., within 3 class days] |

---

## Output Format

1. Objective Alignment Blueprint  
2. Misconception Taxonomy  
3. Pre-Assessment (items + answer key)  
4. Scoring & Misconception Coding Guide  
5. Time-Boxed Instructional Response Plan  
6. Success Criteria + Next Reassessment Plan

---

## Quality Indicators

- [ ] Every item maps clearly to an objective and evidence type
- [ ] Misconception taxonomy is explicit and actionable
- [ ] Assessment fits stated time box with realistic pacing
- [ ] Response plan includes concrete grouping and time blocks
- [ ] Next reassessment criteria are measurable and date-bounded

## Common Pitfalls

| Pitfall | Sign | Prevention |
|--------|------|------------|
| **Objective drift** | Items test tangential content | Build objective-to-item map first |
| **Binary scoring only** | Right/wrong data without diagnosis | Add misconception codes per item |
| **No instructional bridge** | Data collected but no action steps | Include immediate response schedule |
| **Untimed design** | Assessment cannot finish in class | Estimate per-item minutes and trim |
| **Vague reassessment target** | "Do better next time" | Define proficiency cut + reassessment date |
