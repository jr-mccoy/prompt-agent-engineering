**Purpose:** Convert diagnostic evidence into high-impact reteach interventions aligned to objectives, misconception categories, and measurable mastery checkpoints.

**When to use:** After common assessments; when a subset of students misses mastery; during MTSS/RTI planning; before reassessment windows.

**Input needed:**
- Priority objective(s) requiring reteach
- Misconception diagnosis data
- Available intervention time and staffing
- Reassessment constraints

---

## Your Input

**Course / Grade:** [Context]  
**Priority Objective(s):** [SWBAT statements]  
**Standards:** [Codes/text]  
**Diagnostic Summary:** [Misconception codes + prevalence]  
**Intervention Model:** [In-class reteach / pull-out / station support / tutoring]  
**Available Time:** [e.g., 3 sessions x 25 min]  
**Resources/Constraints:** [Staff, materials, attendance realities]  
**Reassessment Deadline:** [Date or week]

---

## Constraints

**Must:**
- Tie every intervention action to a stated priority objective and misconception code.
- Match instructional strategy to misconception type (conceptual / procedural / language / transfer / metacognitive).
- Design sessions that fit within the stated Available Time and staffing constraints.
- Include an embedded check-for-understanding in every session, not only at final reassessment.
- Define numeric or behavioral success criteria and explicit advancement / repeat / intensify rules.

**Must Not:**
- Do not apply a one-size-fits-all reteach across distinct misconception types.
- Do not plan sessions that exceed the bell schedule, staffing model, or attendance realities provided.
- Do not advance students past a reassessment gate without meeting the criterion across two independent opportunities.
- Do not invent diagnostic data, student names, or counts not present in the inputs.
- Do not omit progress-monitoring tools — diagnosis-to-reassessment with no checkpoints is not an intervention plan.

## Instructions

### Step 1: Objective-to-Misconception Prioritization

Create a prioritization matrix:

| Objective | Misconception Code | Severity | Student Count | Priority Tier |
|----------|---------------------|----------|---------------|---------------|
| [SWBAT...] | [C1/P1/L1/T1/M1] | [High/Med/Low] | [#] | [Tier 1/2/3] |

### Step 2: Intervention Strategy Selection

Match strategy to misconception type:

| Misconception Type | Strategy | Why It Fits | Materials/Supports |
|--------------------|----------|-------------|--------------------|
| Conceptual | [Concrete model + worked contrast] | Rebuilds mental model | [Manipulatives/anchor chart] |
| Procedural | [I do-We do-You do] | Repairs sequence fluency | [Practice set/checklist] |
| Language | [Vocabulary unpack + sentence frames] | Reduces language barrier | [Word bank/visuals] |
| Transfer | [Varied contexts + non-example sorting] | Strengthens application | [Scenario tasks] |

### Step 3: Time-Boxed Session Plan

Design session-by-session instruction:

| Session | Minutes | Focus Objective | Activity Flow | Misconception Target | Embedded Check |
|---------|---------|-----------------|---------------|----------------------|----------------|
| 1 | [25] | [Objective A] | [5-min retrieval, 12-min guided, 6-min independent, 2-min exit] | [Codes] | [Exit ticket item] |
| 2 | [25] | [Objective A/B] | [Flow] | [Codes] | [Quick quiz] |

### Step 4: Grouping, Roles, and Differentiation

| Group | Entry Criteria | Supports | Challenge Extension | Instructor Actions |
|------|----------------|----------|---------------------|--------------------|
| Group A | [C1 dominant] | [Scaffold set] | [Transfer challenge] | [Model + feedback cadence] |

### Step 5: Progress Monitoring + Reassessment Gate

Define success criteria for next reassessment:

| Objective | Monitoring Tool | Success Criterion | Reassessment Type | Advancement Rule |
|----------|------------------|-------------------|-------------------|------------------|
| [Objective] | [Daily check / exit task] | [e.g., 80% of students score ≥3/4 twice] | [Mini-assessment] | [Advance / repeat / intensify] |

Include a final readiness checkpoint:
- **Ready:** Meets criterion across two independent opportunities.
- **Almost Ready:** Meets criterion once; assign one booster session.
- **Not Ready:** Below criterion; escalate support and adjust strategy.

## Output Format

1. Prioritization Matrix (Objective × Misconception)  
2. Strategy-to-Misconception Match  
3. Time-Boxed Intervention Sessions  
4. Grouping + Differentiation Plan  
5. Monitoring Dashboard  
6. Reassessment Gate Criteria and Decision Rules

---

## Quality Indicators

- [ ] Intervention actions are directly tied to objective gaps
- [ ] Misconception taxonomy drives grouping and strategy choices
- [ ] Session timing is explicit and realistic
- [ ] Monitoring tools capture progress before reassessment
- [ ] Reassessment gate has clear advancement rules

## Common Pitfalls

| Pitfall | Sign | Prevention |
|--------|------|------------|
| **One-size reteach** | Same intervention for all errors | Match strategy to misconception type |
| **Overlong sessions** | Plans impossible within bell schedule | Use fixed minute allocations |
| **No monitoring loop** | Wait until final reassessment to check | Embed daily/each-session checks |
| **Weak success criteria** | "Students improved" with no threshold | Define numeric and behavioral criteria |
| **Intervention drift** | Activities not tied to priority objective | Keep objective listed per session |
