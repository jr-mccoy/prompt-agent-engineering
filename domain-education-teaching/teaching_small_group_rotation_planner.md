**Purpose:** Build structured small-group rotations that target specific misconceptions, maximize instructional minutes, and lead to clear reassessment readiness.

**When to use:** During workshop models, intervention blocks, station teaching, and mixed-readiness classrooms needing targeted support.

**Input needed:**
- Learning objective(s)
- Student grouping data by misconception
- Rotation duration and station constraints
- Reassessment target timeline

**Grade / Subject:** [Context]  
**Objective(s):** [SWBAT...]  
**Standards:** [Codes/text]

**Station Count:** [e.g., 3 stations]  
**Misconception Group Data:** [Group A: C1, Group B: P1, etc.]  
**Adult Support:** [Teacher only / co-teacher / para]  
**Reassessment Window:** [Date or class session]

## Constraints

**Must:**
- Align every station to a specific objective and intended evidence type.
- Group students by misconception code, not by general "ability."
- Schedule explicit minutes for launch, every transition, and closure — total must equal the Rotation Window.
- Assign a clear teacher-led protocol that prevents one group from monopolizing adult support.
- Define proficiency thresholds and follow-up rules (advance / recycle / escalate) before rotation begins.

**Must Not:**
- Do not run rotations whose stations could not be tied back to a stated objective.
- Do not exceed the Rotation Window once launch, transitions, and debrief minutes are added.
- Do not leave any station without observable completion criteria or evidence capture.
- Do not invent class composition, staffing, or schedule data not provided in the inputs.
- Do not omit the post-rotation decision rules — rotations without next-step rules are activity, not instruction.

## Instructions

### Step 1: Objective & Station Alignment

Map stations to objectives and evidence:

| Station | Objective Focus | Standard | Evidence to Collect | Success Signal |
|---------|-----------------|----------|---------------------|----------------|
| Teacher-Led | [Objective] | [Code] | [Observed performance] | [Meets criterion] |
| Collaborative | [Objective] | [Code] | [Task output] | [Accuracy/quality threshold] |
| Independent | [Objective] | [Code] | [Practice sample] | [Exit score] |

### Step 2: Misconception Taxonomy Grouping

Classify groups using shared taxonomy:

| Group | Primary Misconception Code | Secondary Code | Student Count | Instructional Need |
|------|-----------------------------|----------------|---------------|--------------------|
| A | C1 | L1 | [#] | Concept clarification + language scaffold |
| B | P1 | — | [#] | Guided procedural rehearsal |

### Step 3: Time-Boxed Rotation Schedule

Create minute-precise rotation flow:

| Segment | Minutes | Action |
|---------|---------|--------|
| Launch | [5] | Objective reminder + station norms |
| Rotation 1 | [12] | Group assignments |
| Transition | [2] | Reset + move |
| Rotation 2 | [12] | Group assignments |
| Transition | [2] | Reset + move |
| Rotation 3 | [12] | Group assignments |
| Debrief/Exit | [5] | Whole-class synthesis + exit check |

Then provide a rotation matrix:

| Group \ Round | Round 1 | Round 2 | Round 3 |
|----------------|---------|---------|---------|
| Group A | Teacher-Led | Collaborative | Independent |
| Group B | Independent | Teacher-Led | Collaborative |

### Step 4: Station Task Design

For each station define:

| Station | Task | Misconception Target | Differentiation | Completion Criteria |
|---------|------|----------------------|-----------------|---------------------|
| Teacher-Led | [Task] | [Codes] | [Scaffold/extension] | [What done well looks like] |

### Step 5: Success Criteria for Next Reassessment

Set objective-linked reassessment readiness:

| Objective | Station Evidence Requirement | Reassessment Tool | Proficiency Threshold | Follow-Up Rule |
|----------|-------------------------------|-------------------|-----------------------|----------------|
| [Objective] | [e.g., 2/3 station checks at mastery] | [Mini-quiz/performance item] | [cut score] | [Advance/recycle/escalate] |

## Output Format

1. Objective-to-Station Alignment Map  
2. Misconception-Based Grouping Table  
3. Time-Boxed Rotation Schedule + Matrix  
4. Station Task Cards (targets, scaffolds, criteria)  
5. Exit Evidence Plan  
6. Reassessment Success Criteria + Follow-Up Rules

---

## Quality Indicators

- [ ] Every station has explicit objective alignment
- [ ] Grouping uses a clear misconception taxonomy
- [ ] Timing includes launch, transitions, and closure
- [ ] Tasks include differentiation and observable completion criteria
- [ ] Reassessment rules define what happens next for each group

## Common Pitfalls

| Pitfall | Sign | Prevention |
|--------|------|------------|
| **Rotation without purpose** | Stations feel busy but not targeted | Align each station to objective + misconception |
| **Transition loss** | Students lose time moving | Schedule explicit transition minutes |
| **Teacher bottleneck** | One group monopolizes support | Set concise teacher-led protocols |
| **No evidence capture** | Hard to decide who is ready | Define station evidence per objective |
| **Unclear next step** | No action after rotation | Add reassessment decision rules |
