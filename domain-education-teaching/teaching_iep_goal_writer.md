**Purpose:** Generate SMART IEP and 504 plan goals across academic, behavioral, social-emotional, and functional domains with aligned accommodations, modifications, and progress monitoring plans.

**When to use:** Writing annual IEP goals; drafting 504 accommodation plans; preparing for IEP team meetings; creating progress monitoring frameworks; updating goals at review periods.

**Input needed:**
- Student's disability category or condition
- Present levels of performance (general description — no identifying information)
- Goal domain (academic, behavioral, social-emotional, functional)
- Grade level
- Target skills or areas of concern

## Your Input

**Disability Category:** [e.g., Specific Learning Disability, Autism, ADHD, Speech/Language, Other Health Impairment]
**Plan Type:** [IEP or 504]
**Grade Level:** [Grade or age range]
**Goal Domain:** [Academic / Behavioral / Social-Emotional / Functional / Communication / Motor]
**Subject Area (if academic):** [e.g., Reading fluency, Written expression, Math computation]
**Present Levels Summary:** [Describe current performance without student names — e.g., "Student reads at X level, struggles with Y, demonstrates strength in Z"]
**Baseline Data:** [Current measurable performance — e.g., "Reads 45 WPM with 85% accuracy on grade-level text"]
**Target Skills:** [Specific skills to develop]
**Evaluation Period:** [e.g., Annual, Trimester, Quarterly]

> **PRIVACY REMINDER:** Never include student names, school names, or other personally identifiable information in AI prompts. Use generic descriptors only. All AI-generated goals must be reviewed and individualized by the IEP team before inclusion in legal documents.

## Instructions

Generate IEP/504 goals and supporting documentation through these structured phases:

### Step 1: Present Levels of Academic Achievement and Functional Performance (PLAAFP)

Draft a PLAAFP summary using this structure:

**Current Performance:**
- **Strengths:** [What the student does well — build goals from strengths]
- **Needs:** [Specific skill deficits supported by data]
- **Baseline Data:** [Quantitative measures — scores, rates, percentages, frequency counts]

**Impact of Disability:**
- How the disability affects involvement in the general education curriculum
- Specific areas where performance differs from grade-level expectations

**Previous Goal Progress:**
- [Note progress on prior goals if available — Met / Progressing / Not Met]

### Step 2: Generate SMART Goals

Write goals using the SMART framework. Each goal must include ALL five components:

| Component | Requirement | Example |
|-----------|-------------|---------|
| **S**pecific | Names the exact skill or behavior | "decode multisyllabic words" |
| **M**easurable | Includes quantitative criteria | "with 80% accuracy" |
| **A**chievable | Realistic given present levels | Baseline 45% → Target 80% over 1 year |
| **R**elevant | Tied to identified needs | Addresses reading fluency deficit |
| **T**ime-bound | States the timeline | "By [annual review date]" |

**Goal Format:**
```
By [date], given [conditions/supports], [student] will [specific observable behavior]
[measurement criteria] as measured by [assessment tool/method] across [number]
consecutive data points/sessions.
```

**Generate 2-3 goals per domain requested:**

| Goal # | Domain | Goal Statement | Baseline | Target | Measurement Tool |
|--------|--------|---------------|----------|--------|------------------|
| 1 | [Domain] | [Complete SMART goal] | [Current level] | [Target level] | [How measured] |
| 2 | [Domain] | [Complete SMART goal] | [Current level] | [Target level] | [How measured] |

### Step 3: Short-Term Objectives / Benchmarks

Break each annual goal into 3-4 progressive benchmarks:

**Goal 1 Benchmarks:**

| Benchmark | Timeline | Target | Measurement |
|-----------|----------|--------|-------------|
| 1.1 | End of Q1 | [Incremental target] | [Method] |
| 1.2 | End of Q2 | [Incremental target] | [Method] |
| 1.3 | End of Q3 | [Incremental target] | [Method] |
| 1.4 | End of Q4 (Annual) | [Final target] | [Method] |

**Progression Logic:**
- Benchmarks should represent roughly equal increments from baseline to target
- Early benchmarks may focus on accuracy; later benchmarks add fluency or independence
- Each benchmark should be independently measurable

### Step 4: Accommodations and Modifications

**Accommodations** (change HOW student learns — does not alter content):

| Category | Accommodation | Setting |
|----------|--------------|---------|
| **Presentation** | [e.g., Text-to-speech, large print, visual aids] | [Classroom / Testing / Both] |
| **Response** | [e.g., Scribe, speech-to-text, extended response time] | [Classroom / Testing / Both] |
| **Setting** | [e.g., Preferential seating, small group, reduced distractions] | [Classroom / Testing / Both] |
| **Timing** | [e.g., Extended time (1.5x), frequent breaks, flexible scheduling] | [Classroom / Testing / Both] |

**Modifications** (change WHAT student learns — alters content/expectations):

| Area | Modification | Rationale |
|------|-------------|-----------|
| [Subject] | [e.g., Reduced problem set, alternative assignment] | [Why needed] |

**For 504 Plans specifically:**
- Focus on accommodations (504s typically do not include modifications)
- Link each accommodation to how the disability limits a major life activity
- Include accommodations for standardized testing

### Step 5: Progress Monitoring Plan

**Data Collection Schedule:**

| Goal | Monitoring Tool | Frequency | Person Responsible | Data Recording Method |
|------|----------------|-----------|--------------------|-----------------------|
| Goal 1 | [e.g., CBM probes, rubric, behavior log] | [Weekly / Biweekly / Monthly] | [Role] | [Where data is stored] |
| Goal 2 | [Tool] | [Frequency] | [Role] | [Method] |

**Progress Reporting:**
- Report progress to parents at same frequency as general education report cards
- Include: current performance level, rate of progress, likelihood of meeting annual goal

**Decision Rules:**
| Data Pattern | Interpretation | Action |
|-------------|----------------|--------|
| On track (at or above aimline) | Adequate progress | Continue current plan |
| Slightly below aimline | Slow progress | Adjust instruction intensity |
| Significantly below aimline | Inadequate progress | Convene team to revise goals/services |
| Goal met early | Exceeded expectations | Set new, more ambitious goal |

### Step 6: Service Delivery Summary

| Service | Provider | Frequency | Duration | Location | Start Date |
|---------|----------|-----------|----------|----------|------------|
| [e.g., Specialized instruction] | [Role] | [x times/week] | [minutes/session] | [Setting] | [Date] |
| [e.g., Speech-language therapy] | [Role] | [x times/week] | [minutes/session] | [Setting] | [Date] |

**Least Restrictive Environment (LRE) Statement:**
- Percentage of time in general education: [%]
- Justification for any removal from general education setting

## Output Format

Structure the complete IEP/504 goal package as:
1. **PLAAFP Summary**
2. **Annual Goals with SMART Components**
3. **Short-Term Objectives / Benchmarks**
4. **Accommodations and Modifications**
5. **Progress Monitoring Plan**
6. **Service Delivery Summary**
7. **Parent-Friendly Goal Summary** (plain language version)

---

## Quality Indicators

**Compliant IEP goals include:**
- [ ] All five SMART components present in every goal
- [ ] Goals are based on present levels data (not arbitrary targets)
- [ ] Benchmarks show logical progression from baseline to target
- [ ] Measurement tools are specific and accessible to the provider
- [ ] Accommodations match identified needs (not a generic checklist)
- [ ] Progress monitoring schedule is realistic and documented
- [ ] Goals address all areas of identified need
- [ ] Parent-friendly language version included

**Common IEP Goal Pitfalls:**

| Pitfall | Sign | Prevention |
|---------|------|------------|
| **Unmeasurable goals** | "Student will improve reading" | Include specific criteria and measurement tool |
| **Unrealistic targets** | Baseline 20% → Target 100% in one year | Set achievable increments based on growth norms |
| **Generic accommodations** | Same list copied for every student | Match accommodations to specific disability impact |
| **Missing baselines** | Goals with no starting data point | Always include current measurable performance |
| **Vague measurement** | "Teacher observation" only | Specify structured observation tool or rubric |
| **Compliance-only goals** | Goals that check boxes but don't drive instruction | Ensure goals reflect what will actually be taught |

---

## False-Positive Prevention

**Before finalizing, verify:**
- [ ] No student names or identifying information included in the prompt
- [ ] Goals reflect INDIVIDUAL student needs, not generic templates
- [ ] IEP team review is explicitly noted as required before implementation
- [ ] Goals are not so specific they would only apply to one assessment tool
- [ ] Accommodations do not inadvertently lower expectations
- [ ] Cultural and linguistic factors are considered in goal-setting
- [ ] Goals account for the student's strengths, not just deficits
