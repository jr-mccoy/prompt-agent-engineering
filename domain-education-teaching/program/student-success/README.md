# Student Success & Retention

*3 prompts · part of [`domain-education-teaching`](../../README.md)*

## Use these when

Keeping students enrolled and progressing, from the institution's side: the proactive advising calendar every first-year receives, the outreach and case plan for students already flagged this term, and the retention and persistence review that shows where and for whom students are being lost.

## Safety and data notes

- Every prompt here works with **aggregate counts** and placeholders; none asks for student-level records to be pasted into an AI tool. Follow FERPA or your local equivalent and your institutional-research suppression rules.
- The outreach plan routes any sign of self-harm, threat, or crisis to the counselling or behavioural intervention team **the same day**, outside the normal sequence.
- Retention definitions, reporting rules, and accreditor thresholds are supplied by the user, never stated from memory.
