# Professional Learning Community (PLC) Designer

**Objective:** Design a Professional Learning Community structure — cycle, norms, protocols, artifacts, accountability mechanisms — that produces sustained collaborative inquiry and instructional improvement among educators rather than just a recurring meeting.

## When to Use
- ✅ K-12 grade-level or content-area team PLC design
- ✅ HE department or course-coordinator PLC
- ✅ Cross-program teaching PLC at a CTL
- ✅ Med-ed faculty PLC around assessment, milestones, simulation
- ❌ Designing a single PD event (use a workshop-design prompt)
- ❌ One-to-one coaching (use `teaching_instructional_coaching_program.md`)

## Inputs Required
- **Audience and size:** team composition, number of participants, common context (grade level, course, program)
- **Meeting frequency and duration**
- **Shared goal / focus:** what the PLC is investigating (student learning question, equity gap, assessment results, instructional practice)
- **Authority and time** allocation (paid release time, embedded in workday, voluntary)
- **Data access:** student work, assessment results, observation tools
- **Existing PLC structure** (if redesigning)

## Constraints

**Must:**
- Specify the inquiry cycle (typical DuFour cycle: What do we expect students to learn? How will we know? What do we do if they don't / if they do?)
- Establish norms and protocols
- Specify artifacts produced at each meeting
- Build accountability without surveillance
- Tie inquiry to student / learner evidence (not just opinions)
- Plan for difficult conversations (disagreement, equity concerns, accountability)

**Must Not:**
- Generate a "meeting structure" that's really just an agenda
- Skip data / evidence anchoring
- Treat PLCs as compliance theater
- Default to consensus-only (require productive disagreement)
- Skip facilitation training or rotating roles

## Instructions

1. **Confirm context.** Echo audience, size, frequency, shared goal, authority, data access.

2. **Define the inquiry cycle.**
   - Question (what are we investigating about student learning?)
   - Common assessment / evidence collection
   - Data analysis (looking at student work or results)
   - Instructional response (what we'll change)
   - Re-measurement
   - Reflection and next question

3. **Establish norms.**
   - Confidentiality where needed
   - Productive disagreement
   - Evidence-anchored discussion (student work, data, observation)
   - Equity-minded framing (asset-based, structural lens)
   - Time discipline
   - Distributed leadership / rotating roles

4. **Design protocols.**
   - Data protocols (looking at numerical data)
   - Student work protocols (looking at samples)
   - Observation protocols
   - Tuning protocols (refining lessons/assessments)
   - Critical friends protocols

5. **Specify per-meeting artifacts.**
   - Agenda
   - Notes capturing decisions and disagreements
   - Action commitments (who does what by when)
   - Evidence reviewed
   - Next-meeting question

6. **Build the year/term arc.**
   - Cycle of inquiry over the period
   - Anchor points: setting question, mid-cycle data review, end-cycle reflection

7. **Address accountability.**
   - Action commitments tracked
   - Public sharing within the PLC
   - External sponsor or coach (lightweight oversight)
   - Recognition of impact

8. **Plan facilitation.**
   - Facilitation roles (rotating or fixed)
   - Facilitator training
   - Common protocols mastered together

9. **Plan for difficult moments.**
   - Disagreement protocols
   - Equity conversations
   - Underperformance accountability
   - Restorative practices

## False-Positive Prevention

| Common Mistake | Why It's Wrong | Correct Approach |
|---|---|---|
| Agenda without inquiry cycle | Meetings without learning | Build the cycle: question → evidence → action → re-measure |
| Opinion-anchored discussion | Decisions not grounded in student learning | Anchor in student work and data |
| Compliance theater | PLCs become meetings; impact zero | Build action commitments + tracking |
| Consensus-only | Suppresses productive disagreement | Norms for productive conflict |
| No facilitation rotation | Same voice dominates | Rotating roles + facilitator training |
| Generic norms ("be respectful") | Unactionable | Specific observable norms |
| No equity lens | Reproduces inequity | Build equity-minded framing into norms and protocols |
