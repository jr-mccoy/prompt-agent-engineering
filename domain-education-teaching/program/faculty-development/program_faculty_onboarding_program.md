# Faculty Onboarding Program Designer

**Objective:** Design a first-year (or first 2-3 year) faculty onboarding program — structured milestones, mentoring, required and recommended development, culture-building, and accountability — that produces faculty who are both productive and successfully integrated, with explicit equity-minded design.

## When to Use
- ✅ HE department / institution new-faculty onboarding
- ✅ K-12 new-teacher induction program
- ✅ Med-ed new-faculty (residency program / med school) onboarding
- ✅ Workforce trainer / CTE instructor onboarding
- ✅ Redesigning weak existing onboarding
- ❌ Single-day orientation (use an orientation prompt)
- ❌ Mentoring program structure alone (use a mentoring prompt)

## Inputs Required
- **Sector and institution**
- **New-faculty profile:** typical demographics, backgrounds, career stage at hire
- **Duration:** first year only / multi-year (typical: 1-3 years to tenure-track milestone)
- **Resources:** mentor capacity, FTE for onboarding lead
- **Critical first-year goals:** what must happen (typically: teaching effectiveness, scholarly start, navigating institution, building community)
- **Equity considerations:** institutional commitments to faculty diversity, climate, mentoring equity

## Constraints

**Must:**
- Specify milestones across the onboarding period
- Pair every new faculty with a mentor (1:1 or mentoring team)
- Required vs. recommended distinction
- Address teaching, scholarship/practice, service, well-being
- Build community and culture explicitly (not just events; relationships)
- Build feedback and check-in cadence
- Address equity (mentoring equity, climate, retention)

**Must Not:**
- Generate an event-based program (orientations + occasional workshops)
- Skip mentoring or treat it as optional
- Default to one-size-fits-all
- Skip well-being and integration support
- Generate program that overwhelms new faculty in Month 1

## Instructions

1. **Confirm inputs.** Echo sector, profile, duration, resources, goals.

2. **Build the onboarding timeline.**

   - **Pre-arrival:** information packet, mentor assignment, course assignments, technology access
   - **First weeks:** orientation, classroom/office setup, key relationships, first-week teaching/clinical
   - **First semester:** teaching support, scholarly/practice start, navigating policies, first review touchpoint
   - **First year-end:** comprehensive review, planning for Year 2
   - **Years 2-3:** sustaining mentoring, milestone preparation (tenure / promotion / boards)

3. **Specify required vs. recommended.**
   - Required: legal compliance, FERPA/HIPAA/Title IX training, mandatory teaching observations
   - Recommended: PLC participation, professional society, optional workshops

4. **Build the mentoring structure.**
   - Mentor selection criteria
   - Mentor training
   - Pairing process (with attention to equity — avoid burdening URiM faculty with disproportionate mentoring)
   - Cadence and protocols
   - Multi-mentor models (research mentor + teaching mentor + sponsor)

5. **Address each domain.**
   - **Teaching:** observation, coaching, course-design support, classroom community
   - **Scholarship / practice:** start-up support, writing groups, research community
   - **Service:** protect from over-service; clarify expectations
   - **Well-being and integration:** community-building events, affinity groups, EAP awareness
   - **Institution navigation:** policies, budgets, approvals, key offices

6. **Plan the feedback / check-in cadence.**
   - Mentor monthly
   - Chair / supervisor each semester
   - Onboarding lead at key milestones
   - Exit / Year-1 review

7. **Equity-minded design.**
   - Mentoring-load equity audit
   - Climate check-ins (anonymous if needed)
   - URiM affinity networks
   - Sponsorship (active advocacy beyond mentoring)

8. **Evaluation.**
   - Implementation: who got what
   - Faculty experience: surveys + qualitative
   - Outcomes: retention, productivity, satisfaction, milestone achievement

## False-Positive Prevention

| Common Mistake | Why It's Wrong | Correct Approach |
|---|---|---|
| Event-based program | Misses relational and ongoing nature | Build timeline with mentors and milestones |
| Optional mentoring | Falls apart fast | Mentoring required, with training |
| Overburdening URiM faculty with mentoring | Equity harm | Mentoring-load equity audit + sponsorship |
| All compliance, no community | Faculty leave for relational reasons | Build community-building explicitly |
| Over-loaded first month | New faculty drown | Sequence; right-size monthly load |
| No well-being support | Burnout risk | Build well-being touchpoints |
| Single mentor for all needs | Different needs require different mentors | Multi-mentor models |
| No evaluation | Can't improve program | Measure implementation + experience + outcomes |
