# Written Advocacy (Layperson-Facing Self-Advocacy Letters)

Thirty-five prompts for a **person acting for themselves** who needs something from a company, agency, insurer, school, or landlord — and wants it in writing, dated, and on the record. Each prompt produces **your own first-person letter**, built only from facts you supply, designed to still make sense months later to a regulator, an attorney, or a judge.

> ## Read This First — What These Prompts Are (and Are Not)
>
> **They draft and organize your own correspondence.** They do **not** give legal advice, legal strategy, or legal conclusions, and they are **not** a substitute for an attorney, a nonprofit counsellor, a clinician, or your jurisdiction's law. They will **not** tell you what anyone is legally obliged to do, cite a statute or article as authority, state a legal deadline, tell you whether a refusal is valid, predict whether you will get what you asked for, or assess how strong your position is. Those questions are **routed to an attorney, a regulator, or the relevant professional.**
>
> **This domain is deliberately separate from `domain-legal/`.** Most of what lives here is not a legal dispute — it is ordinary life admin that happens to work better in writing. `domain-legal/personal-self-advocacy/` handles the adversarial-harm matters (harassment, defamation, IP theft, identity theft, debt collection) and carries a mandatory crisis Safety Block on every prompt. That posture is right there and wrong for a gym-membership cancellation. Every prompt here routes to `domain-legal/` at the point a matter turns adversarial.

## Conventions (every prompt in this set)

1. **Written-record-first.** Every output is dated, names the recipient and the account, states one specific ask, sets a response window, and closes with a **Sending Log** capturing delivery method and proof of receipt. The letter is built to be read later by someone with no context.
2. **No fabrication of facts.** Only your own information. Gaps are flagged `[NEED ACCOUNT #:]`, `[NEED DATE:]`, `[NEED DOCUMENT:]`, `[NEED AMOUNT:]`, `[UNCERTAIN WORDING:]` — never filled with a plausible guess. No invented confirmation numbers, policy terms, representative names, or amounts.
3. **No invented law — the domain's highest fabrication risk.** Rights and protections are named only as plain-language literacy, flagged *verify for your jurisdiction*. **No statute, article, section number, regulation, deadline, or penalty is ever asserted from memory.** GDPR articles, CCPA sections, FCRA provisions, state deposit and lemon-law statutes, and agency response periods are the specific hazards, and a wrong one actively harms the person who sends it. Where a user has personally verified that a law applies to them, it appears as **their own stated basis in their own words** — never as a citation supplied by the prompt.
4. **No named bodies from memory.** Regulators, ombudsmen, credit bureaus, safety authorities, data protection authorities, hotlines, and support services are **never named** and their addresses, portals, and URLs are never supplied. Every one is flagged `[VERIFY: identify the correct body from an official source]`. Which body covers what changes constantly, and a wrong filing can consume a time limit.
5. **Proportionate tone ladder.** The default register is firm and neutral. Legal-threat and cease-and-desist language is **refused** and routed to an attorney — a layperson threatening litigation they have not researched usually worsens their position.
6. **Required jurisdiction input.** US-primary, with non-US users directed to verify their local equivalent. Every prompt requires the jurisdiction before drafting.
7. **Self-send label.** Every letter carries `MY OWN LETTER — NOT A LEGAL FILING` (or the equivalent for the request type). Any statutory attestation is presented as **your own** to read, verify, and sign.
8. **No outcome prediction.** Never "this will work," "they have to," or an assessment of how strong a position is.
9. **Channel neutrality.** The channel prompt presents documentation tradeoffs factually and never pathologizes or moralizes about phone versus written contact.

**File naming:** `advocacy_{function}.md` — lowercase, snake_case, no subdirectory prefix.

| If the matter is… | Go to |
|---|---|
| Adversarial harm — harassment, stalking, defamation, IP theft, identity theft, debt collection | `domain-legal/personal-self-advocacy/` |
| A refund or chargeback with your card issuer | `domain-legal/personal-self-advocacy/consumer-scams/legalprep_refund_chargeback_dispute_preparer.md` |
| Debt validation — you are not sure the debt is yours | `domain-legal/personal-self-advocacy/debt-collection/legalprep_debt_validation_dispute_letter_preparer.md` |
| A security deposit or a landlord notice | `domain-legal/personal-self-advocacy/housing-landlord-tenant/` |
| A workplace complaint about treatment | `domain-legal/personal-self-advocacy/workplace/legalprep_hr_complaint_narrative_preparer.md` |

| Divorce, custody, or family court | `domain-legal/family-self-advocacy/` |
| Small claims | `domain-legal/personal-self-advocacy/small-claims/` |
| An attorney-side demand letter | `domain-legal/client-intake-communications/legal_demand_letter_drafter.md` |

| Negotiation craft in written channels | `domain-negotiation/channels/negotiation_written_async_message.md` |

**Deliberately not duplicated.** Refunds and chargebacks, debt validation, security deposits, HR complaints, DMCA notices, and fraud-scoped credit disputes already exist in `domain-legal/personal-self-advocacy/`. Prompts here cross-link to them rather than re-covering them — `advocacy_recurring_charge_dispute.md` handles the cancellation-not-honoured pattern and hands off for the chargeback itself; `advocacy_credit_report_dispute.md` covers non-fraud inaccuracy only and routes fraud cases onward.
