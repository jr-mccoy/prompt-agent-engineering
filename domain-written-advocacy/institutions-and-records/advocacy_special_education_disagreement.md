# Special-Education Disagreement Letter

**Purpose:** Help you write **your own** dated letter when the school has made a
special-education decision you disagree with — so the disagreement, the explanation you
asked for, and any request for an independent evaluation exist on the record, in the words
of the school's own documents, rather than only in a meeting you left upset.

**When to use:**
- A meeting ended with a decision you did not agree to: your child found not eligible,
  services or minutes reduced or ended, a placement changed, or your concerns left out.
- The school's evaluation does not match what you, a teacher or an outside professional see,
  and you want to ask for an independent evaluation.
- You were told about a decision verbally and have nothing in writing explaining it.
- **Not this prompt if** you are making a **first** request for an assessment, support or
  records — `advocacy_school_written_request.md` already covers that (this prompt is
  re-angled to what happens *after* a decision). Preparing for a learning-concern meeting
  rather than writing after one: `domain-parenting/caregiver-facing/academics-skills/parenting_learning_concern_school_meeting.md`.
  Your earlier request went unanswered: `../cross-cutting/advocacy_followup_and_deadline_tracker.md`.
  Teacher-side goal writing: `domain-education-teaching/instructor/student-support/teaching_iep_goal_writer.md`.

## Boundary & Routing Block

Use a different pathway if:
- **A child is unsafe, has been restrained or secluded, harmed, or excluded from school** →
  not a disagreement letter. Contact the school's safeguarding lead and the appropriate
  authority now. `[VERIFY: the reporting route for your jurisdiction from an official source.]`
- **You are considering a formal dispute route** — mediation, a complaint to an education
  authority, a hearing or tribunal → these have their own forms, evidence rules and time limits.
  Contact an education advocacy service, legal aid or an attorney **before** sending anything
  that might be read as starting one. `[VERIFY: locate a parent advocacy or advice service in
  your jurisdiction from an official source.]`
- **A document from the school states a date or time limit** → find it in that document and act
  on it. This prompt states no deadlines.
- **You are being asked to sign, consent or refuse consent** → whether and what to sign is a
  decision to make with an advocacy service. This letter neither gives nor withholds consent.

This prompt prepares your own correspondence. It is not legal, educational-psychology or
clinical advice.

## Scope Boundary — Read First

This **drafts your own letter** recording a disagreement and making specific requests. It
will **not** state what the school is required to do; cite, quote or number any statute or
regulation; state any timeline or response period; say whether your child is eligible, what
services they need, or whether an evaluation was adequate; tell you whether to consent; or
predict the outcome. **Vocabulary note (literacy only):** in the US, federal special-education
law (commonly called IDEA) and the procedural-rights document districts give parents (often
titled a *procedural safeguards notice*) use terms such as *IEP*, *prior written notice* and
*independent educational evaluation*. Other countries use different plans and terms.
**Use a term in your letter only if it appears in a document your school or district gave
you — and quote it from that document.** *Verify locally.*

---

## Core Principles

1. **Quote the decision exactly** — a disagreement with a quoted sentence cannot be waved away.
2. **One disagreement per row** — "I disagree with everything" gives the school nothing to answer.
3. **Observations, not diagnoses** — what you saw, with dates; never what your child "has" or "needs".
4. **Ask for the reasons and the data** — the written explanation, options considered, information relied on.
5. **Anchor requests in the school's own documents** — page and heading, in its words, not a rule from memory.
6. **Firm, neutral, no threats** — legal threats are refused here; they belong to an attorney.
7. **Disagreement is not complaint** — frustration with how the meeting ran is a different letter.

## Constraints

**Must:** require jurisdiction; screen safety, formal-dispute and consent questions first; quote
the decision verbatim (`[NEED EXACT WORDING:]` if missing); list disagreements one per row with
observations; anchor each special-education term and the independent-evaluation request to a
quoted document the user holds (`[NEED DOCUMENT: procedural-rights notice, page/heading]`);
ask what the process is and who decides; include a Sending Log and the label
`MY OWN LETTER — NOT A LEGAL FILING`.

**Must Not:** cite or number any statute or regulation; state a deadline, response period or
entitlement; assert eligibility, needs, a diagnosis or the adequacy of an evaluation; give or
refuse consent; threaten legal action; predict the outcome; invent meeting content, data or
document wording; combine with a complaint about handling.

## Instructions

### Stage 1 — Screen and Frame (CM-01)
Run the Boundary & Routing Block. Restate jurisdiction, the decision and the boundary: this
records a disagreement and makes requests; entitlement questions go to an advocacy service.

### Stage 2 — Anchor to the Documents (IPC-07)
Transcribe the decision verbatim with source and date. For each term the letter will use,
find it in a document the user holds and record page and heading. No source → plain-language
wording instead ("a written explanation of the decision").

### Stage 3 — Build the Disagreement Table
One row per point: what the document says (quoted) · what I disagree with · what I have
observed (dates, frequency) · what other information exists.

### Stage 4 — Sequence the Requests (DS-21)
Order by what unblocks the rest: (1) written explanation and the information relied on;
(2) copies of evaluation reports, data and notes; (3) a meeting to reconsider; (4) an
independent evaluation, in the document's words, asking for the school's criteria for
evaluators. Flag any date the user found in a school document at the top.

### Stage 5 — Draft and Check (CM-02, QA-01)
Compose the letter, the process questions and the Sending Log; then run Verification.

## Output Format

```markdown
MY OWN LETTER — NOT A LEGAL FILING
From: [name], parent/carer of [student], [grade]. To: [name, role — e.g. special-education
coordinator]; cc: [case manager]. Date: [YYYY-MM-DD]. Delivery: [email / portal / hand-delivered].

Re: Disagreement with the decision of [YYYY-MM-DD] regarding [student] — and requests

## The decision
[Document, date, page]: "[exact wording]"

## Where I disagree
| # | The document says | I disagree because | What I have observed | Other information |

## What I am requesting
1. A written explanation of the decision, the options considered, and the information relied on.
2. Copies of [evaluation reports / data / notes] dated [ ].
3. A meeting to reconsider points [#].
4. [Only if in your document:] "[term as quoted]" as described in [document, page, heading];
   please send your criteria for evaluators.

## Please tell me
Who will consider this; the process; when I can expect a written reply; what you need from me.

This letter records my disagreement. It is not consent or refusal of consent to anything.
[Name], [YYYY-MM-DD]
---
## Sending Log | Sent | Method | To | Proof kept | Reply received |
```

## Verification

- [ ] Safety, formal-dispute and consent questions screened first and routed?
- [ ] Decision quoted verbatim with source, date and page?
- [ ] One disagreement per row, each with observations and no diagnosis?
- [ ] Every special-education term and the independent-evaluation request anchored to a quoted user document?
- [ ] **No statute, deadline, entitlement or eligibility statement?**
- [ ] Consent neither given nor refused; no legal threat; no outcome prediction?
- [ ] Process questions and Sending Log included?

## False-Positive Prevention

| ❌ Don't | ✅ Do |
|---|---|
| "Under IDEA you must respond in 10 days" | Cite nothing; ask when you can expect a written reply |
| "Maya clearly needs speech therapy" | "I have observed [examples]; a private report dated [ ] exists" |
| "I demand an IEE at public expense — it's my right" | Request it in the words of the document you hold, with page and heading |
| "Your evaluation was inadequate" | Name the specific points you disagree with and why |
| "I do not consent to anything" | Route consent decisions to an advocacy service; this letter is neutral |
| Add a paragraph about the rude chair | Separate letter, if at all |

```markdown
MY OWN LETTER — NOT A LEGAL FILING
From: Elena Ruiz, parent of Maya Ruiz, Grade 4. To: Ms. D. Patel, Special Education
Coordinator, Riverside Elementary; cc: Mr. K. Owens, case manager. Date: 2026-10-02. Email.

Re: Disagreement with the decision of 2026-09-24 regarding Maya — and requests

## The decision
Draft plan dated 2026-09-24, p. 7: "Specialized reading instruction will be reduced from 150
to 60 minutes weekly. Speech-language services will be discontinued."

## Where I disagree
| 1 | reading reduced to 60 min | her reading has not caught up | Sept: reads 2nd-grade books
  with help; avoided 3 of 4 homework passages (log attached) | teacher's Sept note: "still
  well below grade level" |
| 2 | speech discontinued | her speech is still hard for others to follow | relatives ask
  her to repeat most sentences; 3 notes from her coach | private evaluation dated 2026-08-15 exists |

## What I am requesting
1. A written explanation of both decisions, the options considered, and the data relied on.
2. Copies of the reading progress data (Jan–Sep 2026) and the speech evaluation of 2026-09-10.
3. A meeting to reconsider items 1 and 2.
4. "An independent educational evaluation at public expense," as described in the Parent
   Procedural Safeguards Notice (rev. 2025), p. 12, heading "Independent Educational
   Evaluation," for speech-language; please send your criteria for evaluators.

This letter records my disagreement. It is not consent or refusal of consent to anything.
```

## Adaptations

- **Not-eligible finding:** quote the eligibility summary; ask for the data and the criteria
  applied; independent-evaluation request only if your document describes it.
- **Services reduced or ended:** quote old and new minutes; ask what data supported the change.
- **No US-style plan where you live:** use the names your school's documents use; the method holds.

## Techniques Used

- **IPC-07 Verbatim Source Anchoring** — decision and every term quoted from the user's own documents.
- **CM-01 Explicit Context Framing** — jurisdiction, decision and boundary restated first.
- **DS-21 Proximity Assessment** — requests ordered by what unblocks the rest; school-stated dates surfaced first.
- **CM-02 Constraint Specification** — no statute, deadline, consent position or prediction.
- **QA-01 Self-Verification** — checklist before sending.
