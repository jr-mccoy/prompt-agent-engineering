# Ambition & Leverage

Prompts for leadership-level conversations about whether the organization's current plans reflect the leverage AI actually provides — and for the specific moves (expert-as-builder rollout, capital allocation between expansion and savings, workflow redesign to compress lead time) that turn aspiration into commitment.

**Audience:** CEO, leadership team, board, chief of staff running one of these exercises on the CEO's behalf. These are senior-leadership artifacts — they name uncomfortable gaps and are not intended for broad internal distribution.

**Core idea:** AI leverage is real but narrower than marketing suggests. These prompts force the question into specifics: in *this* plan, in *this* workflow, in *this* capital allocation — what would change, what would not, and what assumption blocks the more ambitious version.

---

## Suggested sequence

1. **`ambition_leadership_audit.md`** — done first, surfaces where the team's current plans sit vs available leverage.
2. **`ambition_expansion_vs_savings_brief.md`** — frames the capital-allocation question if the audit reveals latent ambition.
3. **`ambition_experts_to_builders_roadmap.md`** — operationalizes one kind of leverage (democratizing building) with the rigor the board will demand.
4. **`ambition_insight_to_action_workflow.md`** — redesign one workflow where leverage can be proven quickly, producing the pilot that the roadmap and brief need.

Skipping the audit and jumping to the roadmap is how these rollouts produce theater. The audit is the discipline step.
