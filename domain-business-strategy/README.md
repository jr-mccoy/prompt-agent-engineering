# Domain: Business Strategy

**Purpose:** Prompts for business analysis, strategic frameworks, startup operations, market research, and organizational productivity.

---

## What This Domain Covers

Strategic and operational business prompts:

1. **Analysis** - SWOT, BCG Matrix, competitive analysis, financial modeling
2. **Startup** - Naming, branding, copywriting, go-to-market
3. **Research** - Competitive landscape, company deep dives, market sizing
4. **Organization** - Meeting notes, brain dumps, weekly reviews
5. **Chief-of-Staff** - Personal operating cadence (briefings, reviews, delegation)
6. **AI Strategy** - Enterprise AI platform, vendor lock-in, capability compounding
7. **Ambition & Leverage** - Leadership audits, expansion vs savings, workflow redesign
8. **Browser Automation** - Automation audit, recording blueprint, safety check

## Key Frameworks

### Strategic Analysis
- **SWOT Analysis** - Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, Threats
- **BCG Matrix** - Portfolio analysis
- **Porter's Five Forces** - Competitive dynamics
- **OKR Analysis** - Objectives and Key Results

### Financial Analysis
- **Unit Economics** - CAC, LTV, margins
- **Financial Modeling** - Revenue projections, burn rate
- **Market Sizing** - TAM, SAM, SOM

### Startup Operations
- **Naming** - Brand name generation and validation
- **Positioning** - Value proposition development
- **Go-to-Market** - Launch strategy planning

## When to Use This Domain

Use these prompts when you need to:
- Conduct strategic analysis (SWOT, competitive, financial)
- Develop startup branding and positioning
- Research markets and competitors
- Organize team meetings and documentation

**Do NOT use for:**
- Coding tasks (use domain-software-engineering)
- Personal productivity (use domain-productivity)
- Presentations (use domain-presentations)
