## Usage Notes

- Complete all sections relevant to the assessment type
- Document direct quotes when clinically significant
- Risk assessment must include BOTH risk and protective factors
- Safety plan should be completed collaboratively with the patient when possible
- If patient lacks capacity, document the specific criteria not met
- Cultural considerations should inform the assessment approach
- This template complements `medicine_psychiatric_assessment_support.md`
