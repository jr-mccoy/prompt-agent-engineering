## False-Positive Prevention

When filling this template:

❌ **DON'T:**
- Fill "Prognosis Discussed: [Best case / most likely / worst case]" with estimates the clinician did not give in the meeting; record the words or numbers actually used, or "prognosis not discussed".
- Enter "Has capacity" under Decision-Maker Capacity without the decision-specific, time-specific assessment that supports it.
- Paraphrase what family members said into tidy values ("prioritizes comfort") that nobody voiced; quote the statement and attribute it to the patient, the surrogate or a named family member.
- Record a Legal Surrogate / HCPOA from a family member's say-so without the document; surrogate hierarchy where no HCPOA exists varies by jurisdiction.
- Leave "Code status mismatch with documented goals?" at "No" by default when the Code Status Decision differs from the active order.

✅ **DO:**
- Compare the Code Status Decision and Treatment Intensity Plan with the current orders; any difference gets an "Update orders/code status" row with an owner and deadline in Follow-up Accountability.
- Check that Participants Present includes the person named as decision-maker, and an interpreter whenever the documented language preference requires one.
- Date the Prognosis and Medical Update entries, so a later reader can tell whether the conversation reflected the current clinical picture.
