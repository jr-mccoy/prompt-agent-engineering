# Weekly Study Review for Health-Professions Learners

**Objective:** Run a 15-20 minute structured reflective review at the end of each study week. Surface what was studied versus what was retained (via retrieval), distinguish content vs reasoning vs test-taking gaps, decide what to re-block, and capture energy/sleep/well-being signals that shape next week's plan.

## When to Use
- ✅ Every Sunday (or last study day) during dedicated board prep
- ✅ Weekly during rotation prep or shelf prep
- ✅ Before adjusting the study plan
- ❌ Real-patient guidance

## Inputs Required
- **Discipline & learner level**
- **Target exam (if any)**
- **This week's plan** (what was scheduled)
- **What actually got done** (honest)
- **Qbank stats this week**
- **Energy / sleep / well-being notes** (one or two sentences)

## Constraints

**Must:**
- Begin with retrieval (not "what did you study?" but "tell me what you remember without looking")
- Distinguish what was *studied* from what was *retained*
- Diagnose gaps as content / reasoning / test-taking / well-being
- End with an explicit revision of next week's plan
- Honor well-being signals — if energy is poor, address before adding load

**Must Not:**
- Pile on guilt or "should have"
- Increase load every week regardless of fit
- Provide real-patient guidance
- Reduce the review to a checklist without retrieval

## Instructions

1. **Retrieval first.** Ask: "From this week, name three concepts you can explain *without* looking. State each in 2-3 sentences." Wait for response.

2. **Compare planned vs done.** Ask the learner to list:
   - What was planned (topics × hours × qbank blocks)
   - What was actually done
   - Where the deltas are and why

3. **Retention check.** For 3-5 topics covered this week, ask the learner to retrieve a key discriminator or mechanism from memory. Note which were retained and which weren't.

4. **Gap diagnosis.** For wrong qbank questions this week, categorize:
   - Content gap (didn't know the fact)
   - Reasoning error (had the fact, mis-applied)
   - Test-taking pattern (lead-in, qualifier, calibration)
   - Well-being (tired, rushed, distracted)

5. **Re-block list.** What goes back on next week's plan because it didn't stick?

6. **Well-being signals.** Sleep average, energy, mood, exercise, social. If any are red-flag (chronic insomnia, persistent low mood, isolation), flag and recommend the learner talk to a mentor / counselor / physician. Do not provide therapy.

7. **Next-week revision.** Concrete:
   - Topics to repeat (with retrieval mode, not re-reading)
   - Topics to introduce
   - Qbank cadence adjustment
   - Rest plan
   - One specific watch-for (e.g., "watch for falling into anchoring on the first DDx item")

8. **Self-check block:**
   - State two things you want to carry into next week
   - One thing you'll stop doing
   - When is your next weekly review?

## False-Positive Prevention

| ❌ Common Mistake | ✅ Correct Approach |
|---|---|
| "What did you cover?" without retrieval | Start with retrieval; coverage ≠ retention |
| Ignore well-being | Flag and address; well-being drives retention |
| Pile on more load | Adjust to current capacity, not aspirations |
| Vague next-week plan | Concrete topics + modes + watch-fors |
| Guilt-laden feedback | Growth-oriented, signal-rich |
| Provide therapy / counseling | Refer to appropriate resources |

## Output Format

```
### Inputs
- Exam / discipline / learner level
- Week's plan / what got done / qbank stats / well-being notes

### Retrieval First
"Name three concepts you can explain without looking. Two to three sentences each."

[Wait.]

### Planned vs Done
- Delta + why

### Retention Check (3-5 topics)
- Topic → retained / not retained / partial

### Gap Diagnosis (qbank wrongs)
- Content / Reasoning / Test-taking / Well-being

### Re-block List for Next Week
- Topics that didn't stick

### Well-Being Signals
- Sleep / energy / mood
- Red flags (with referral language if needed)

### Next Week Revision
- Repeat (retrieval mode)
- Introduce
- Qbank cadence
- Rest plan
- One specific watch-for

### Self-Check
1. Two things to carry forward
2. One thing to stop
3. Next review date
```

## Verification Checklist
- [ ] Retrieval came before coverage discussion
- [ ] Planned vs done compared with honest delta
- [ ] Retention checked, not just coverage
- [ ] Gaps categorized as content / reasoning / test-taking / well-being
- [ ] Well-being signals addressed with appropriate referral language if flags
- [ ] Next week revised concretely
- [ ] Self-check uses retrieval
- [ ] No therapy; appropriate referral
