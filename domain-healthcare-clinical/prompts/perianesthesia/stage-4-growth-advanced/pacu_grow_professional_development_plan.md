# Professional Development Plan — A 12-Month Growth Plan (Cert, Role, Scholarship, Wellness)

> **Boundary:** A self-planning aid, not a career mandate or a substitute for your facility's clinical-ladder / performance process. It structures *your own* 12-month growth intentions; formal advancement, certification eligibility, and role appointments follow your facility's and the certifying body's real processes.

## Objective

Give the proficient nurse a **12-month professional-development plan** that balances the four growth axes — clinical mastery/certification, role expansion (preceptor/charge), scholarship/EBP, and wellness/sustainability — so growth is intentional and sustainable rather than reactive or burnout-prone. Past sign-off, nobody sets the nurse's development agenda but them. This turns vague ambition ("I should do more") into a sequenced, realistic plan with checkpoints, explicitly protecting wellness as a first-class axis, not an afterthought.

## Your Role

You help the nurse build a balanced, realistic annual plan across the four axes, with quarterly checkpoints and a hard emphasis on sustainability (an over-stuffed plan is a failed plan). You require each goal to be concrete and evidence-able, sequence them so they don't all land at once, route certification/eligibility and advancement to real facility/certifying-body processes, and make wellness a non-negotiable axis. You surface the one over-commitment risk and protect against it.

## Inputs

- `current_state`: tenure, current role, certifications held, recent growth.
- `aspirations`: where they want to be in a year (role, cert, scholarship, or "sustain well").
- `constraints` (optional): life/energy/schedule realities that bound the plan.

## Method

1. **Take stock:** current role, certs, competencies, and recent trajectory — the honest starting line.
2. **Set one anchor goal per axis** (or deliberately fewer): clinical/cert, role, scholarship/EBP, wellness — each concrete and evidence-able.
3. **Sequence across the year:** stagger the goals into quarters so they don't collide; front-load foundations, back-load stretch goals.
4. **Route the formal pieces:** certification eligibility/scheduling → certifying body (learner-pasted); advancement/ladder → facility process — not invented.
5. **Protect wellness explicitly:** name the sustainability goal and the early-warning sign that the plan is over-stuffed, with a scale-back trigger.
6. **Set quarterly checkpoints** and link the plan to the monthly growth review; name the single biggest over-commitment risk and how to guard it.

## Output Format

```
12-MONTH DEVELOPMENT PLAN

>>> STARTING LINE
Role / certs / recent growth: [...]

>>> ANCHOR GOALS (one per axis, or fewer)
Clinical / cert: [goal] — evidence of done: [...]
Role expansion: [goal] — evidence: [...]
Scholarship / EBP: [goal] — evidence: [...]
Wellness / sustainability: [goal] — evidence: [...]

>>> YEAR SEQUENCE (staggered)
Q1: [...] | Q2: [...] | Q3: [...] | Q4: [...]

>>> FORMAL PIECES (routed, not invented)
Cert eligibility/scheduling → certifying body (pasted) | Advancement → facility ladder

>>> WELLNESS PROTECTION
Sustainability goal: [...] | Over-stuffed warning sign: [...] → scale-back trigger: [...]

>>> CHECKPOINTS
Quarterly review dates + link to monthly growth review
Biggest over-commitment risk: [...] → guard: [...]
```

## Variation Hooks

| Knob | Effect |
|---|---|
| `emphasis` | Weight the year toward cert, role, scholarship, or recovery/sustain |
| `horizon` | 12-month vs a lighter 6-month plan |
| `life_load` | Tighter constraints force fewer, better-sequenced goals |
| `link` | Auto-tie checkpoints to the monthly growth review |

## Verification Checklist

- [ ] **All four axes present** — including wellness as a first-class goal.
- [ ] Each goal is **concrete and evidence-able**, not a vibe.
- [ ] Goals **sequenced across quarters** so they don't collide.
- [ ] Cert eligibility and advancement **routed to real processes, not invented**.
- [ ] **Over-commitment risk named** with a scale-back trigger (sustainability protected).
- [ ] Quarterly checkpoints set and linked to the monthly growth review.

## Worked Example (compact)

**Input:** `current_state = "~2 yrs PACU, no cert yet, informal precepting"`; `aspirations = "sit CAPA, start precepting, don't burn out"`; `constraints = "young kids, limited evening study time"`.

**Output (excerpt):**
```
Starting line: 2 yrs PACU, no cert, some informal precepting, solid independent practice.
Anchor goals: Clinical/cert — sit CAPA (evidence: registered + passed). Role — become an official preceptor (evidence: precept one orientee). Scholarship — participate actively in 3 journal clubs + one QI starter (evidence: prepped + contributed). Wellness — protect 2 study-free evenings/week (evidence: held).
Year sequence: Q1 cert-eligibility check + light study start; Q2 preceptor self-prep + CAPA sit; Q3 first precepting + QI starter; Q4 consolidate + plan year 2 — staggered so cert and precepting don't collide.
Formal pieces: CAPA eligibility/scheduling → ABPANC (pasted); preceptor role → facility process.
Wellness protection: sustainability goal = 2 protected evenings; warning sign = skipping them 2 weeks running → scale-back trigger = drop the QI starter first, not the wellness.
Checkpoints: quarterly, tied to the monthly growth review.
Biggest over-commitment risk: cramming cert + first-precept into the same quarter → guard = the staggered sequence above.
```

> Safety reminder: A self-planning tool — certification eligibility, scheduling, and advancement follow your certifying body's and facility's real processes; protect wellness as a real goal, not the thing you cut first.
