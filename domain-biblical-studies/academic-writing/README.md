# Academic Writing

Prompts for **seminary and university writers (A)** and pastors writing for a degree or publication (P) — the discipline-specific writing workflows of biblical studies: an exegesis paper scaffold, a thesis/dissertation topic workshop, a history-of-research literature review plan, an annotated bibliography builder, and a referee-style self-check of your own paper. Each prompt carries the method that is particular to the field — working from the original languages, handling textual variants, separating the history of modern research from the history of interpretation, attributing readings to identifiable streams — and leaves general academic process to `domain-research-academic/`.

**Neutrality & anti-fabrication:** this is the domain's highest-risk place for **invented scholarship**, because citations are what academic writing is graded on. Every prompt here is **STRONG-GUARD**: none originates a scholar's name, a title, a page number, a manuscript siglum, an apparatus reading, a lexical gloss, a quotation, or a statement of what "most scholars" hold. Where a paper needs such material, the prompt names the *kind* of resource and leaves a **[VERIFY]** slot for the writer; anything the writer supplies is echoed as user-supplied and stays verify-required. The annotated-bibliography builder adds a verification gate and a **Quarantine** for sources that cannot be located, including any suggested by an AI tool. Contested readings are attributed to streams; no prompt ranks a method or tradition as correct, and none writes prose for submission.

## Prompts

| Prompt | Focus | Audience | Difficulty |
|---|---|---|---|
| `biblical_academic_exegesis_paper_scaffold.md` | Single-passage exegesis paper: provisional thesis, section questions, word budget, [VERIFY] resource slots (**STRONG-GUARD**) | A, P | advanced |
| `biblical_academic_thesis_dissertation_workshop.md` | Topic → 2–3 researchable questions; contribution claims as hypotheses; method, language, and source feasibility; anchored scores (**STRONG-GUARD**) | A | advanced |
| `biblical_academic_literature_review_plan.md` | History of research vs. history of interpretation; discipline indexes; multilingual search terms; inclusion rules; extraction record (**STRONG-GUARD**) | A, P | advanced |
| `biblical_academic_annotated_bibliography_builder.md` | Verified entries only; annotations from your notes; Quarantine for unlocatable or AI-suggested sources (**STRONG-GUARD**) | A, P | intermediate |
| `biblical_academic_peer_review_self_check.md` | Referee-style review of your own draft: anchored scores, located findings, blocking vs. minor, clean-pass valve (**STRONG-GUARD**) | A, P | advanced |

## Which prompt for which question

- **"Help me structure my exegesis paper on this passage"** → `biblical_academic_exegesis_paper_scaffold.md`
- **"I need a thesis topic / my supervisor says it's too broad / is this already done?"** → `biblical_academic_thesis_dissertation_workshop.md`
- **"How do I find what scholars and earlier readers have said about this text?"** → `biblical_academic_literature_review_plan.md`
- **"Make my annotated bibliography / are these sources even real?"** → `biblical_academic_annotated_bibliography_builder.md`
- **"Check my paper before I hand it in"** → `biblical_academic_peer_review_self_check.md`

A typical sequence: thesis workshop → literature review plan → annotated bibliography → exegesis paper scaffold (or thesis chapter plan) → self-check.

## Boundaries — lives elsewhere

- **General academic process** (research questions for any field, thesis chapter architecture and committee milestones, systematic-review protocols, search-strategy Boolean design, synthesis of sources, fact-check reconciliation) → `domain-research-academic/`, e.g. `research_thesis_dissertation_structure.md`, `research_literature_review_plan.md`, `research_manuscript_fact_check_reconciler.md`. This folder adds only what biblical studies requires on top.

- **Kinds of sources for a doctrinal question** → `theology-research/biblical_theological_research_bibliography.md`; **choosing commentary types** → `theology-research/biblical_commentary_evaluation.md`.
- **Socratic coaching for student annotations (no drafted text)** → `domain-education-teaching/learner/writing/learn_annotated_bibliography_helper.md`.
