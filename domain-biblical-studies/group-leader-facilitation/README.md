# Group Leader Facilitation

**Subdirectory of:** `domain-biblical-studies/`
**Audience:** Group leaders (G) and pastors who coach them (P) — people who lead Bible-study discussions and need help with the human dynamics, not just the content.
**Prompts:** 7
**Added:** 2026-06-25 (Phase 3A)

---

## Why This Exists

`study-methods-teaching/` helps a leader prepare *content* — discussion questions, lesson plans, study methods. This subdirectory helps with what happens *in the room*: silence, dominance, tangents, conflict, controversial questions, mixed maturity levels, online formats, and developing the next generation of leaders. The gap was identified by audit — no prompt in the domain addressed facilitation dynamics.

---

## Conventions

All prompts inherit the domain's two load-bearing conventions:

1. **Tradition-neutral** — handling hard questions, heretical claims, and theological disagreement prompts describe approaches across traditions; a user may declare a tradition, but alternatives stay visible.
2. **Anti-fabrication first** — no invented group-dynamics research, facilitator statistics, or pedagogical claims. The one STRONG-GUARD prompt (`biblical_groupleader_heretical_claim_response.md`) carries heavier language because it must define "orthodox" at ecumenical-creed level without adjudicating intra-Christian disputes.
