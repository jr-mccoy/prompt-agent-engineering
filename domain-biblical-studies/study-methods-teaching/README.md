# Study Methods & Teaching

Prompts that apply repeatable study methods and build teaching materials: inductive (OIA) study, SOAP, whole-book overview, small-group discussion guides, lesson plans, memorization and reading plans, and thematic/topical study.

**Neutrality & anti-fabrication:** discussion and teaching prompts generate open, text-grounded questions and present multiple readings rather than steering a group toward one tradition's conclusion. Thematic study guards against proof-texting and fabricated cross-references; memorization requires exact quotation from a user-named translation.
