# Pastoral Scripture

Prompts for **pastors, chaplains, and spiritual directors (P)** and ministry-context leaders (M), plus laypeople working with a director (L), who choose and present Scripture in pastoral settings: the Bible-study part of pre-marital preparation, Scripture alongside addiction recovery, formation practices fitted to a person's season, chaplaincy readings across institutional settings, and an honest response to passages that have been used against abuse victims. Each prompt does one thing: it selects passages, shows what each says in context and what it does **not** say or promise, and attributes contested readings to identifiable streams.

**Boundary contract (shared by every prompt here):** this layer is **Scripture selection and presentation only**.

- **Not counseling or therapy.** No prompt here does counseling, therapy, diagnosis, clinical assessment, or a treatment plan, and none replaces a licensed professional. Clinical work belongs in `domain-psychology/`. Lay one-to-one mentoring design belongs in `domain-discipleship/mentor-equipping/`.
- **Crisis routing.** Suicidality, self-harm, threats to others, or a medical emergency (including alcohol or benzodiazepine withdrawal and overdose): stop the Scripture work and route to emergency services, a crisis line, or a licensed clinician.
- **Abuse routing.** Domestic or intimate-partner abuse, coercive control, child abuse, elder or vulnerable-adult abuse, and clergy/spiritual abuse: safety first. Route to a DV advocate, the child-protection authority, adult protective services, or the appropriate professional. Mandated-reporting duties vary by jurisdiction and role `[VERIFY locally]`, and a consult never delays a required report. No joint sessions or reconciliation work where abuse is present.
- **No numbers or statutes from memory.** Every hotline, emergency number, statute, institutional policy, program claim, and denominational document is a `[VERIFY: …]` slot.
- **STRONG-GUARD against fabrication.** Verses are given by address only. The user supplies the translation text or checks it against a named translation. There are no invented citations, cross-references, original-language data, scholars, statistics, or program or instrument claims.
- **Tradition-neutral.** Readings are attributed (complementarian / egalitarian, Catholic, Orthodox, Reformed, Pentecostal, historical-critical, and so on) and never adjudicated. Canon differences, such as deuterocanonical books, are noted where they matter.
