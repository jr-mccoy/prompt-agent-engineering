# Ministry Contexts

Prompts for **ministry-context teachers (M)** — people teaching the Bible to specific groups: children, youth, new believers, seekers, families, and special programs (VBS/camp/retreat), plus a foundation for Scripture-rooted lay-care conversations. Each prompt calibrates a faithful handling of the text to its audience while staying useful for a real volunteer or leader.

**Neutrality & anti-fabrication:** these prompts present the text and the range of credible readings, attributing contested practices and doctrines to identifiable streams without endorsing one (a teacher may declare a tradition to foreground it, but alternatives stay visible). They reference Scripture **by address** and build from user-supplied translation text — no verses, cross-references, original-language data, statistics, stories, or citations are quoted or invented from memory (illustration needs route to `sermon-devotional/biblical_sermon_illustration_finder.md`). **Two load-bearing guardrails:** the kids/youth/family/program, story-retelling, and inclusive-teaching prompts carry a **child-safety note** (age-appropriate, concrete, active; no frightening, graphic, or inappropriate framing), and the care-conversation prompt carries a **STRONG boundary guardrail** — it is not therapy, counseling, diagnosis, or crisis intervention, and routes mental-health, abuse, self-harm, suicidality, and crises to licensed professionals and emergency services.

## Which prompt for which question

- **"Build a Sunday school / kids' church lesson on this passage"** → `biblical_ministry_kids_bible_lesson_builder.md`
- **"Design a youth-group study that has real depth and handles their hard questions"** → `biblical_ministry_youth_bible_study_designer.md`
- **"Where does a new believer start? Give me a staged path"** → `biblical_ministry_new_believer_discipleship_path.md`
- **"Introduce the Bible to a curious skeptic — honestly, no pressure"** → `biblical_ministry_seeker_intro_to_bible.md`
- **"Make a short family devotion that works for all our ages and we can actually keep"** → `biblical_ministry_family_devotions_designer.md`
- **"Plan a VBS / camp / retreat teaching arc"** → `biblical_ministry_special_program_session.md`
- **"Tell this Bible story to preschool, early-elementary, and older kids — without adding things"** → `biblical_ministry_age_graded_story_retelling.md`
- **"Adapt my lesson so a child with a disability or autism can fully take part"** → `biblical_ministry_special_needs_inclusive_teaching.md` (no diagnosis; home-side support → `domain-parenting/caregiver-facing/neurodivergence/`)
- **"How do I bring Scripture into an encouragement conversation with someone struggling?"** → `biblical_ministry_care_conversation_foundations.md` (routes mental-health/abuse/self-harm/suicidality/crisis to licensed professionals and emergency services)
