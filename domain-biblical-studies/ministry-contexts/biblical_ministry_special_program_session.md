# Special Program Session Designer

**Objective:** Design a teaching session — or a themed multi-session arc — for a VBS, camp, or retreat, with a unifying theme, energy and age scaling across the program, passages handled faithfully and referenced by address, and clear takeaways per session and overall.

> **Child-safety note.** Programs often run high-energy with minors away from parents. Keep all content age-appropriate: concrete, active, gentle with hard content; no frightening, graphic, or inappropriate framing. Flag any sensitive content for leaders to preview, and route care/crisis concerns out (see related care-conversation prompt).

**When to use:**
- Planning a multi-session VBS, camp, or retreat teaching track around a theme.
- You need an arc that builds across sessions with takeaways, not isolated lessons.
- You're scaling content for an age range or escalating energy across a week.

**When NOT to use:**
- You need a single classroom children's lesson — use `biblical_ministry_kids_bible_lesson_builder.md`.
- You're designing a regular weekly youth study — use `biblical_ministry_youth_bible_study_designer.md`.
- You want a preaching/teaching *series* plan for adults — use `sermon-devotional/biblical_sermon_series_planner.md`.

**Audience:** Ministry-context teachers (M) — VBS directors, camp/retreat speakers, program coordinators, volunteer teaching teams.

---

## Inputs / Context

1. **Program parameters.** Type (VBS/camp/retreat), number of sessions, session length, days, group size, setting.
2. **Audience.** Age band(s) present (single band or mixed), spiritual background.
3. **Theme & passages.** A theme (if chosen) plus the passages — reference and text in a named translation (pasted by the user). The model references by address and works from supplied text.
4. **Energy & format.** Available activity types, supplies, large-group vs. small-group structure, and the energy curve across the program.
5. **Declared tradition (optional).** If supplied, the model may foreground that emphasis but keeps contested specifics neutral and notes alternatives.

---

## Constraints

### Must
- Anchor the arc in a **unifying theme** drawn from the chosen passages; give each session a takeaway that builds toward an overall takeaway.
- Handle every passage **faithfully** — reference by address, build from supplied text; keep observation distinct from application.
- **Scale energy and age**: match activity intensity to time of day/program day and calibrate content to the age band(s) present.
- Provide a **session shape** the volunteer team can run consistently (large-group teach, small-group, activity, response).
- Keep content **age-safe**; flag sensitive content for leader preview; catalog supplies/resources generically (no fabricated specifics).

### Must Not
- Invent story details, dialogue, or events to make a passage more exciting.
- Invent citations, cross-references, original-language data, statistics, or stories. Route illustration needs to `sermon-devotional/biblical_sermon_illustration_finder.md`.
- Add frightening, graphic, or age-inappropriate content; do not use fear or guilt to drive program "decisions."
- Manufacture emotional altar-call pressure on minors; keep responses honest and non-coercive.

### Tradition-neutral stance (Must / Must Not)
- **Must:** keep teaching on the passages' plain sense; where program emphases differ by tradition (e.g., response/decision framing, sacraments), note the difference neutrally and let a declared tradition foreground its own.
- **Must Not:** present a contested doctrinal reading as the single obvious meaning; endorse one tradition as correct over others.

## Instructions

### Step 1 — Orient & set the theme
Restate program parameters, audience, and passages. State (or propose) a unifying theme drawn from the passages and confirmed from supplied text.

### Step 2 — Map the arc
Lay out the sessions in sequence: each session's passage (by address), its takeaway, and how it builds toward the overall takeaway. Show the throughline.

### Step 3 — Design the session shape
Define a consistent per-session structure (e.g., large-group teach → small-group → activity → response) that volunteers can run, with rough timings.

### Step 4 — Scale energy & age
Map the energy curve across days/sessions and calibrate activities and vocabulary to the age band(s). Note adaptations for younger vs. older participants in mixed groups.

### Step 5 — Build out each session
For each session: faithful teaching of the passage (by address), the activity, discussion/wonder questions, and the response — age-safe, with preview flags for sensitive content.

### Step 6 — Takeaways, supplies & team
State per-session and overall takeaways. Catalog needed supplies/resources by type (verify specifics), assign roles, and add a "if running short/long" flex note.

### Step 7 — Care & illustration routing
Add leader notes routing care/crisis concerns to qualified adults/professionals (`biblical_ministry_biblical_care_conversation_foundations.md`) and illustration needs to the illustration-finder.

## Verification

- [ ] Sessions share a unifying theme drawn from the passages and build toward an overall takeaway.
- [ ] Every passage handled faithfully and referenced by address from supplied text.
- [ ] Energy and content scaled to program day and age band(s).
- [ ] A consistent, runnable session shape is provided for the volunteer team.
- [ ] Content age-safe; sensitive content flagged; responses non-coercive (no pressure on minors).
- [ ] No invented citations, cross-references, language data, statistics, stories, or verified-sounding supplies/titles.

---

## False-Positive Prevention

❌ **DON'T:**
- String together unrelated lessons with no throughline or building takeaway.
- Embellish passages with invented drama to match high program energy.
- Engineer emotional pressure for "decisions" from minors.
- List specific curricula/props as if their details were verified.

✅ **DO:**
- Build a themed arc with per-session and overall takeaways.
- Teach faithfully from supplied text, by address; flag sensitive content.
- Scale energy and content to day and age; keep responses honest.
- Catalog supplies/resources by type with verify flags; route care and illustrations out.

---

## Techniques Used

- **ST-01 (Clear Objective Statement):** Fixes the unifying theme and per-session/overall takeaways so the program is a coherent arc, not a pile of disconnected sessions.
- **ST-02 (Structured Sequential Instructions):** The 7-step build (theme → arc → session shape → scaling → per-session build → takeaways/supplies → routing) gives a volunteer team a repeatable program design.
- **RT-02 (Multi-Dimensional Analysis Framework):** Designs across dimensions — theme/throughline, energy curve, age scaling, logistics, and textual faithfulness — rather than optimizing for energy alone.
- **OC-12 (External Reference Catalog):** Organizes supplies, resources, and team roles into a usable catalog while flagging specific titles/props as verify-required instead of asserting them.
- **QA-05 (Citation Requirements):** Every passage is referenced by address from supplied translation text; cross-references, language data, and illustrations are never invented and are routed to dedicated prompts.
