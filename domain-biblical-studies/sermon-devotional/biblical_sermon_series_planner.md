# Sermon / Teaching Series Planner

**Objective:** Build a coherent multi-week series plan over a book or theme — preachable units, a big idea per week, a logical arc, and sensible pacing — that a preacher or teacher can execute week by week.

**When to use:**
- Planning a series through a book or on a theme.
- You need the arc and weekly division before preparing individual messages.

**When NOT to use:**
- You're preparing a single message — use `biblical_expository_sermon_prep.md`.

**Audience:** Pastors/preachers (P).

---

## Inputs / Context

1. **The book or theme.** What the series covers; for a book, the user's translation/edition.
2. **Length & cadence.** Number of weeks, weekly or other.
3. **Congregation & goal.** Who they are; what the series aims to accomplish.
4. **Declared tradition (optional).** May shape emphasis; contested passages flagged, not pre-decided.

---

## Constraints

### Must
- Divide the material into **preachable units** (by address) that respect the text's structure.
- Give each week a **big idea** (one sentence) and focus.
- Sequence weeks into a coherent **arc** (how the series builds).
- Note pacing — flag units that need two weeks or that compress awkwardly.
- For a thematic series, ensure passages genuinely address the theme (route to thematic-study discipline; no proof-texting).

### Must Not
- Carve units that fight the book's structure.
- Invent content, cross-references, or statistics for the plan.
- Pre-decide a contested passage's reading as the series' settled point.

### Tradition-neutral stance (Must / Must Not)
- **Must:** flag contested texts that fall in the series and note they'll need honest handling.
- **Must Not:** design the series to assert a contested distinctive as settled.

---

## Instructions

### Step 1 — Confirm scope & cadence
Restate book/theme, weeks, cadence, and goal; flag if the scope and week count mismatch and propose an adjustment.

### Step 2 — Divide into units
Break the material into weekly units (by address) following the text's structure.

### Step 3 — Big idea per week
Give each week a one-sentence big idea and brief focus.

### Step 4 — Arc & pacing
Show how the weeks build; note pacing issues and any units needing more/less time.

### Step 5 — Flags
List contested passages in the series and a reminder to handle them honestly (route to multi-view/difficult-passage prompts).

---

## Output Format

```
# Series Plan — [book/theme] ([N weeks])

## Setup
- Scope: [..] | Cadence: [..] | Goal: [..]

## Weekly plan
| Week | Passage (address) | Big idea | Focus |
|------|-------------------|----------|-------|
| 1 | [addr] | [one sentence] | [..] |

## Arc & pacing
- How it builds: [..] | Pacing flags: [..]

## Contested-text flags
- [week/passage]: handle honestly (→ multi-view / difficult-passage prompt)
```

---

## Verification

- [ ] Units are preachable and respect the text's structure (by address).
- [ ] Each week has a one-sentence big idea and focus.
- [ ] A coherent arc and pacing notes are present.
- [ ] Thematic units genuinely address the theme (no proof-texting).
- [ ] Contested texts flagged for honest handling; nothing pre-decided.

---

## False-Positive Prevention

❌ **DON'T:**
- Split a book in ways that cut across its natural units.
- Fill weeks with invented stats or cross-references.
- Bake a contested reading into the series as its settled message.

✅ **DO:**
- Divide by the text's structure, by address.
- Give each week a clear big idea and show the arc.
- Flag contested passages for honest, multi-view handling.
