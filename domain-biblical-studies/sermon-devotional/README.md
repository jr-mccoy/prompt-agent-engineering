# Sermon & Devotional

Prompts for preaching and personal/communal devotion: expository sermon preparation, illustration development, daily devotionals, meditation, prayer/journaling, series planning, and text-to-application bridging.

**Neutrality & anti-fabrication:** sermon and devotional prompts stay anchored to what the text actually says and remain neutral on contested doctrine, surfacing rather than resolving interpretive disputes. The illustration prompt carries STRONG-GUARD language — it refuses to invent quotes, statistics, news events, or historical anecdotes, since fabricated illustration "facts" are a notorious failure mode.
