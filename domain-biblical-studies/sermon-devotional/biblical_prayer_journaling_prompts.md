# Prayer & Journaling Prompt Generator

**Objective:** Produce a set of prayer and journaling prompts tied to a passage and the reader's circumstances, so reflection and prayer flow from the text rather than from generic templates.

**When to use:**
- You want prompts to pray and journal through a passage.
- Pairing reading with reflective writing.

**When NOT to use:**
- You want a full devotional or meditation — use those prompts.

**Audience:** Laypeople (L), group leaders (G). Warm, accessible.

---

## Inputs / Context

1. **The passage.** Reference and wording (user-supplied, named translation).
2. **The reader's situation (optional).** What's going on, to ground prompts honestly.
3. **Declared tradition (optional).** May shape prayer language; stays neutral on contested doctrine.

---

## Constraints

### Must
- Tie prompts to the passage's actual themes and the reader's situation.
- Offer a mix: thanksgiving, confession, petition, listening/reflection, application.
- Keep prompts open and personal; the reader supplies the content.
- Reference the passage by address; use supplied wording if quoting.

### Must Not
- Quote the verse from memory; invent facts/quotes.
- Force a tradition's prayer formula or contested doctrine.
- Write prayers that put words/claims in the reader's mouth beyond the text.

### Tradition-neutral stance (Must / Must Not)
- **Must:** keep prayer/journaling prompts broadly usable.
- **Must Not:** require a tradition-specific practice or doctrinal stance.

---

## Instructions

### Step 1 — Draw the themes
Name the passage's themes that connect to prayer/reflection.

### Step 2 — Prayer prompts
Offer prompts across thanksgiving / confession / petition / listening, tied to the themes.

### Step 3 — Journaling prompts
Offer open journaling questions linking the text to the reader's situation.

### Step 4 — Optional structure
Suggest a simple rhythm (e.g., read → journal one prompt → pray one prompt).

---

## Output Format

```
# Prayer & Journaling — [reference]

## Themes
- [theme] / ...

## Prayer prompts
- Thanksgiving: [..]
- Confession: [..]
- Petition: [..]
- Listening: [..]

## Journaling prompts
- [open question linking text to life] / ...

## Optional rhythm
- [read → journal → pray]
```

---

## Verification

- [ ] Prompts tied to the passage's themes and the reader's situation.
- [ ] Mix of prayer types and open journaling questions.
- [ ] Passage referenced by address; supplied wording if quoted.
- [ ] No fabricated content; no tradition-specific formula imposed.

---

## False-Positive Prevention

❌ **DON'T:**
- Quote the verse from memory or invent supporting "facts."
- Impose a specific tradition's prayer practice as required.
- Write prompts disconnected from the actual passage.

✅ **DO:**
- Anchor prompts in the passage's themes and the reader's life.
- Keep them open and personal.
- Reference by address; stay neutral on contested doctrine.
