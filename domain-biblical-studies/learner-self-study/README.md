# Learner Self-Study

Personal study and formation tools for the **self-directed individual learner (S)** — someone studying the Bible on their own who wants structure, self-testing, honest exploration, and reflective engagement without a classroom, group, or teacher. These prompts help you plan, study, quiz yourself, explore doctrines and doubts, use study tools responsibly, check your own understanding, apply the text honestly, and journal through it.

**Boundary guardrail (every prompt):** these are personal study and formation tools — **NOT** pastoral counseling, crisis support, or a substitute for church community. Acute spiritual distress, mental-health crisis, abuse, or self-harm routes to a pastor, a licensed counselor, or appropriate emergency services / professionals.

**Neutrality & anti-fabrication:** like the rest of the domain, these prompts are tradition-neutral (they present competing views attributed to identifiable streams and never adjudicate) and anti-fabrication first — they reference verses **by address**, have you supply the verse text in a named translation, and **never quote Scripture from memory** or invent citations, cross-references, original-language data, or attributions. The self-quiz, comprehension, application, and journaling prompts build their content only from text **you** paste.

## Prompts

| Prompt | Focus | Audience | Difficulty |
|---|---|---|---|
| `biblical_learner_self_directed_study_plan.md` | Design your own paced multi-week study of a book or topic | S | beginner |
| `biblical_learner_character_study_guide.md` | Study a Bible figure by address and in context; separate text from tradition | S | intermediate |
| `biblical_learner_self_quiz_recall_drill.md` | Self-test recall/comprehension on YOUR supplied text (no quizzing from memory) | S | beginner |
| `biblical_learner_doctrine_self_exploration.md` | Explore a doctrine across traditions descriptively (not catechesis) | S | intermediate |
| `biblical_learner_honest_questions_doubt_explorer.md` | Work through honest questions/doubts charitably (routes crisis to pastor/counselor) | S | intermediate |
| `biblical_learner_study_tool_skill_builder.md` | Use concordance/lexicon/study Bible/commentary responsibly (anti-fabrication) | S | beginner |
| `biblical_learner_comprehension_self_check.md` | Feynman-style "explain it back" self-check against the text | S | beginner |
| `biblical_learner_personal_application_worksheet.md` | Honest application: text → original point → principle → today (anti-eisegesis) | S | beginner |
| `biblical_learner_reflection_journal_companion.md` | Guided reflective journaling prompts (engagement, not fabricated content) | S | beginner |

## Which prompt for which question

- **"How do I plan a multi-week study on my own?"** → `biblical_learner_self_directed_study_plan.md`
- **"I want to study a person in the Bible for myself"** → `biblical_learner_character_study_guide.md`
- **"Quiz me on this passage I've pasted"** → `biblical_learner_self_quiz_recall_drill.md`
- **"What do different traditions teach about this doctrine?"** → `biblical_learner_doctrine_self_exploration.md`
- **"I have an honest doubt / hard question I want to think through"** → `biblical_learner_honest_questions_doubt_explorer.md` (acute distress → pastor/counselor/emergency services)
- **"How do I use my concordance/lexicon/study Bible without being misled?"** → `biblical_learner_study_tool_skill_builder.md`
- **"Do I actually understand this passage? Let me explain it back"** → `biblical_learner_comprehension_self_check.md`
- **"How do I apply this passage to my life honestly?"** → `biblical_learner_personal_application_worksheet.md`
- **"Give me prompts to journal through this passage"** → `biblical_learner_reflection_journal_companion.md`
