# Doctrine Study (Multi-Tradition, Descriptive)

**Objective:** Lay out a single doctrine as the major traditions hold it — each position's core claim, biblical basis, internal logic, and the main objections to it — so a user understands the landscape fairly, without being told which is correct.

> **STRONG-GUARD prompt.** Fabricated theologians, councils, confessions, and quotations are a major risk. Attribute to identifiable traditions; flag any named source verify-required.

**When to use:**
- Studying a doctrine where traditions differ (e.g., predestination, the Lord's Supper, spiritual gifts, eschatology).
- You want to understand each side as its adherents do.

**When NOT to use:**
- You want a full topical/systematic synthesis — use `biblical_topical_theology_synthesis.md`.
- You want a structured debate format — use `biblical_interpretive_views_comparison.md`.

**Audience:** Group leaders (G), pastors (P), seminary/academic (A).

---

## Inputs / Context

1. **The doctrine.** Stated precisely, with the specific point of difference.
2. **Traditions of interest (optional).** Which streams to include.
3. **Declared tradition (optional).** May foreground that view; others still presented fully.

---

## Constraints

### Must
- For each major position: core claim, biblical basis (by address, verify-required), internal logic, and the strongest objection it faces.
- Present each as a **position held by a tradition**, attributed to an identifiable stream — never as the plain fact.
- Steelman each view; no straw men.
- Identify the underlying issue the doctrine turns on (a text, a principle, a prior commitment).

### Must Not
- Declare a winner or present one position as the settled truth (unless tradition declared — and then still present alternatives).
- Invent theologians, confessions, councils, dates, quotations, or cross-references.
- Misrepresent any tradition's view.

### Tradition-neutral stance (Must / Must Not)
- **Must:** describe each position fairly and attribute it; label confidence/support.
- **Must Not:** privilege/endorse one stream as correct; collapse disagreement.

---

## Instructions

### Step 1 — Frame the doctrine
State the doctrine and the precise point of difference.

### Step 2 — Positions
For each major position: core claim, biblical basis (by address), internal logic, strongest objection, and the stream(s) that hold it (named sources verify-required).

### Step 3 — The crux
Identify what the disagreement actually turns on.

### Step 4 — Landscape
Note which positions are widely vs. narrowly held (descriptive), and any common ground.

### Step 5 — Honest close
State what's genuinely contested and what would move the question, without ruling.

```
### [Position 2 / stream]
[same]

- Turns on: [..]

- Widely/narrowly held: [..] | Common ground: [..]

- Contested: [..] | What would move it: [..]
```

---

## Verification

- [ ] Each position: claim + basis (by address) + logic + strongest objection + stream.
- [ ] Every view steelmanned; none misrepresented.
- [ ] No winner declared (unless tradition declared, with alternatives kept).
- [ ] No invented theologians/confessions/councils/quotes/cross-references.
- [ ] The crux identified; contested status stated honestly.

---

## False-Positive Prevention

❌ **DON'T:**
- Slide toward the position you find most convincing.
- Quote a confession or theologian from memory as established.
- Straw-man a view so another wins.

✅ **DO:**
- Present each position as its adherents would recognize it.
- Attribute to streams; flag named sources verify-required.
- Name the crux and leave the contested question open.
