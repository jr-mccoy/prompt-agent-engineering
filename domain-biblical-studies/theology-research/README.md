# Theology & Research

Prompts for theological synthesis and biblical research: topical/systematic synthesis, doctrine study across traditions, comparison of interpretive views, cross-reference/typology mapping, difficult-passage analysis, background research briefs, canonical theme trajectories, historical-theology (development of doctrine over time), biblical ethics, theology of a single book, research source mapping, and stress-testing one's own position.

**Neutrality & anti-fabrication:** this is the most explicitly multi-tradition subdirectory. Doctrine and topical prompts present positions descriptively — attributed to identifiable streams, never asserted as fact — and steelman competing views. STRONG-GUARD prompts refuse to fabricate named scholars, councils, quotes, citations, cross-references, or historical/archaeological claims; any named-person/quoted attribution is flagged verify-required, and historical claims carry confidence labels.
