# Reading Prophecy & Apocalyptic Responsibly

> **STRONG-GUARD prompt.** Do not set or estimate dates for any future event. Do not identify any current event, nation, technology, leader, or person as the fulfillment of a prophecy. Do not assert any eschatological system (historicist, preterist, futurist, idealist; pre-/a-/post-millennial) as the correct one or as established fact. Present systems as positions held by identifiable streams, with their strongest textual basis, and leave the reader to weigh them.

**Objective:** Interpret a prophetic or apocalyptic passage by its genre conventions — separating forthtelling from foretelling, near/already from far/not-yet fulfillment, and symbolic from literal imagery — while attributing competing eschatological systems to streams and refusing to date-set or to map the text onto current events.

**When to use:**
- Studying or teaching a prophetic oracle or an apocalyptic vision and wanting genre discipline.
- You want eschatological systems laid out fairly without the model picking one.
- You need a guard against newspaper-exegesis and date-setting.

**When NOT to use:**
- You need to decide the passage's genre first — start with `biblical_genre_aware_reading.md`.
- The contested question is a full interpretive-options map across one disputed point — use `biblical_multiview_interpretation_map.md`.
- You want the general staged method for any passage — use `biblical_passage_exegesis_workflow.md`.
- The question is a single symbolic term's meaning — route the language part to `biblical_word_study_original_language.md`.

**Audience:** Pastors (P) and seminary/academic (A) primarily; equipped group leaders (G) with care.

---

## Inputs / Context

1. **The passage.** Reference plus the text in a named translation (pasted by the user). The model references by address and uses the supplied text rather than quoting from memory.
2. **The interpretive question (optional).** A specific focus (e.g., "Is this addressed to the original audience, to a future, or both?").
3. **Declared tradition / system (optional).** If supplied, the model may foreground that stream's reading but must keep alternatives visible and must still refuse date-setting and current-event identification. No declaration → neutral default.
4. **Surrounding context supplied (optional).** Framing verses, the historical situation named in the text, or any interpretive key the book itself gives.
5. **Depth / output length.** Quick read vs. full prophetic/apocalyptic brief.

---

## Constraints

### Must
- Distinguish **forthtelling** (addressing the prophet's own audience) from **foretelling** (announcing the future), and note where a passage does both.
- Distinguish **near/already** fulfillment from **far/not-yet** fulfillment, marking which the text signals and which is inferred.
- Treat apocalyptic **imagery as symbolic by genre convention** unless the text or its framing signals otherwise; distinguish symbolic from literal claims explicitly.
- Attribute eschatological systems (historicist, preterist, futurist, idealist; pre-/a-/post-millennial) to identifiable streams with their strongest basis, without ruling.
- State confidence on the central reading and what would change it.

### Must Not
- Set or estimate a date, season, or timetable for any future event.
- Identify any current event, nation, technology, leader, or person as a prophecy's fulfillment.
- Assert any eschatological system as the correct one or as fact (unless the user declared one — and even then, keep alternatives visible).
- Invent citations, cross-references, original-language data, or scholar/commentary attributions; route language questions to the word-study prompt.
- Collapse genuine disagreement into false consensus.

### Tradition-neutral stance (Must / Must Not)
- **Must:** present text + consensus; describe differing positions fairly, attributed to identifiable streams; treat doctrinal/interpretive claims as positions, not fact; label confidence on contested points.
- **Must Not:** privilege/endorse any single tradition as correct; present a contested reading as the plain meaning; smooth genuine disagreement into false consensus.

## Instructions

### Step 1 — Orient
Restate the passage reference, identify it as prophecy and/or apocalyptic, and note the historical situation and the prophet's audience as the text presents them. State (if given) the interpretive question.

### Step 2 — Observe & sort the oracle
List what the text actually says: the addressee, the announcement, conditions, repeated images, and structuring devices. Sort statements into forthtelling (to the original audience) and foretelling (announcing the future), marking which the text signals versus which is inferred.

### Step 3 — Fulfillment horizons
Identify the fulfillment horizon(s): near/already, far/not-yet, or a layered "both." Mark which horizon the text itself signals and which depends on a later reading; do not invent a horizon the text does not support.

### Step 4 — Imagery: symbolic vs. literal
Work through the key images. Treat apocalyptic imagery as symbolic by genre convention unless the text signals literalness, and state for each image whether you are reading it as symbol or as literal referent and why. Route any original-language image to `biblical_word_study_original_language.md`.

### Step 5 — Systems map (no ruling)
Lay out how the major eschatological systems (historicist, preterist, futurist, idealist; pre-/a-/post-millennial) would read the passage, each with its strongest textual basis, attributed to identifiable streams — without adjudicating, unless a tradition was declared. Reassert the guard: no date-setting, no current-event identification.

### Step 6 — Confidence & open questions
- Central reading + confidence (low/moderate/high) + what would change it.
- Remaining open questions and where to take them (the multi-view prompt, commentary, lexicon).

## Verification

- [ ] Forthtelling distinguished from foretelling, with text-signaled vs. inferred marked.
- [ ] Fulfillment horizons (near/already, far/not-yet, layered) identified and sourced to the text.
- [ ] Imagery read symbolically by genre convention unless the text signals otherwise; symbolic vs. literal stated per image.
- [ ] No date-setting and no current-event/person identification anywhere in the output.
- [ ] Eschatological systems attributed to streams with their basis, not adjudicated (unless tradition declared).
- [ ] No invented citations, cross-references, lexical data, or scholar attributions.
- [ ] Central reading carries confidence + a change condition.

## False-Positive Prevention

❌ **DON'T:**
- Predict timing or map the passage onto today's headlines, nations, or leaders.
- Present one eschatological system as the obvious or correct reading.
- Flatten apocalyptic symbols into wooden literalism (or dissolve every concrete claim into pure symbol).
- Invent a fulfillment horizon, cross-reference, or background detail the text does not support.

✅ **DO:**
- Keep forthtelling and foretelling, and near and far fulfillment, explicitly distinct.
- Read apocalyptic imagery by its genre conventions and label symbol vs. literal per image.
- Lay out the systems fairly, attribute each to a stream, and leave the ruling to the reader.
- Mark every horizon, image-reading, and cross-reference as text-signaled or verify-required.

## Techniques Used

- **ST-02 (Structured Sequential Instructions):** The 6-step sequence (Orient → Sort → Horizons → Imagery → Systems map → Confidence) forces genre discipline before any systemic reading and reasserts the date-setting guard at the systems stage.
- **RT-02 (Multi-Dimensional Analysis Framework):** Requires analysis across dimensions — forthtelling/foretelling, fulfillment horizons, imagery, and competing systems — so prophecy and apocalyptic are read on their full genre terms.
- **RT-05 (Evidence-Based Reasoning):** Every horizon, image-reading, and system must carry its strongest textual basis and be marked text-signaled or inferred; assertion without basis is prohibited.
- **QA-04 (Uncertainty Acknowledgment):** The central reading states confidence and a change condition, contested systems are kept open, and the guard prevents false certainty about timing or fulfillment.
- **QA-05 (Citation Requirements):** Cross-references and canonical links are given by address and marked verify-required; nothing recalled from memory is presented as authoritative.
