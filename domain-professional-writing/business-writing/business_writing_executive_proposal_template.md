## Customization Guidelines

**For Budget Requests:**
- Emphasize ROI and payback period
- Show sensitivity analysis
- Address "why now" vs. next fiscal year

**For Strategic Initiatives:**
- Connect to organizational priorities/OKRs
- Show competitive implications
- Address change management

**For Headcount Requests:**
- Quantify productivity impact
- Show capacity analysis
- Address alternatives (contractors, tools, reprioritization)

**For Technology Investments:**
- Include build vs. buy analysis
- Address integration complexity
- Show vendor evaluation criteria

---

## Quality Checklist

Before submitting:

- [ ] Ask is clear in first paragraph
- [ ] ROI is quantified with stated assumptions
- [ ] Alternatives were considered (including "do nothing")
- [ ] Risks are acknowledged with mitigations
- [ ] Timeline is realistic
- [ ] Stakeholder concerns are anticipated
- [ ] Executive summary stands alone (1 page max)
- [ ] Numbers have sources
- [ ] Tone is confident but not arrogant
