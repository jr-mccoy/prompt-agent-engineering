# Journalism — Desk Editing, Source Verification, and Investigative Planning

Three prompts for the newsroom work around a story rather than the writing of it:
the editor's pre-publication pass, the reporter's running record of how each fact is
known, and the plan for an investigation that will take weeks. Writing the story itself
from notes lives in [`../writing/writing_news_article_inverted_pyramid.md`](../writing/writing_news_article_inverted_pyramid.md).

## Contents

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| [`journalism_story_desk_edit.md`](journalism_story_desk_edit.md) | Editor's desk edit of a filed story: one-sentence story test, eight smell tests (lede, nut graf, attribution, anonymity, quotes, numbers, loaded language, headline), fairness table, legal-risk flags held for a media lawyer, must/should/could edits, reporter queries, publish/fix/hold verdict |
| [`journalism_source_verification_log.md`](journalism_source_verification_log.md) | Reporter's verification log: human sources (identity, position to know, independence, interest), document provenance and authentication, image/video verification, sourcing terms recorded when agreed, traffic-light claim status with verbatim anchors, carried-forward unknowns, corrections trail |
| [`journalism_investigative_project_plan.md`](journalism_investigative_project_plan.md) | Investigation plan: falsifiable hypothesis with minimum/maximum story, kill signals, phased gates, records and data plan with independent re-run, outside-in source map, legal and safety review, best-rebuttal stress test, no-surprises letter |

Order of use on a big story: investigative plan → verification log (kept throughout) →
draft (`writing_news_article_inverted_pyramid.md`) → desk edit.

## Guards (every prompt)

- **No invention.** No quote, source, number, or fact enters copy unless it is in the
  reporter's notes or log; gaps are queries, not insertions.
- **Legal risk is flagged, never cleared.** Defamation, privacy, protected identities,
  court reporting, and recording-consent questions go to a media lawyer; jurisdiction is
  a required input.
- **Fairness is specific.** Anyone criticised gets the specific allegation and a
  reasonable time to reply, recorded with dates.
- **Source protection.** Confidential identities appear only as codes.
