**Purpose:** Draft a position statement responding to a charge filed with the EEOC, a state Fair Employment Practices Agency (FEPA), or California's Civil Rights Department (CRD, formerly DFEH) — presenting the employer's factual chronology, articulating a specific legitimate non-discriminatory reason, demonstrating comparator-consistent treatment, rebutting pretext, and signaling the employer's posture on mediation, without conceding facts or expanding exposure.

**When to use:** Receipt of an EEOC Charge of Discrimination (Form 5), a state FEPA charge, or a CRD/DFEH complaint; agency request for position statement or RFI; preparation for fact-finding conference or mediation; preserving administrative record for any later federal-court action under Title VII / ADEA / ADA.

## Constraints

**Must:**
- Open with a **clear executive summary**: charge identification, denial of discrimination/retaliation, identification of the legitimate non-discriminatory reason in one sentence.
- Provide a **complete, dated, source-cited factual chronology** — pre-employment, employment history, performance, the events at issue, the adverse action, and post-employment communications. Tie each fact to a document or testimony source.
- For each issue alleged, articulate a **legitimate, non-discriminatory reason** with specificity — dated, documented, quantified where possible. Generic "performance issues" or "fit" reasons collapse on review.
- Address each **protected basis alleged**, not just one. For a charge alleging sex + age + retaliation, address each.
- Provide **comparator analysis**: identify similarly-situated employees outside the protected class with comparable conduct/performance and document consistent treatment. State the material-similarity basis (same supervisor, same standards, comparable severity, similar time frame).
- Rebut **pretext** affirmatively: address shifting reasons (there are none / the reason is consistent across documents), policy compliance (the action followed established policy), temporal proximity (if retaliation is alleged, explain the timing and decision-maker knowledge or lack thereof), and statistical patterns if raised.
- For **retaliation** claims, address each element: protected activity, employer knowledge, adverse action, causal connection. Document the legitimate reason that pre-dates or is independent of the protected activity where applicable.
- For **harassment / hostile work environment**, address the Faragher/Ellerth affirmative defense where supervisor harassment is alleged without tangible employment action: (1) employer exercised reasonable care to prevent and correct (anti-harassment policy, training, complaint procedure, prompt corrective response); (2) CP unreasonably failed to take advantage of preventive/corrective opportunities. Document each prong.
- For **ADA** claims, address: qualified individual analysis, interactive process documentation, reasonable accommodation considered and provided/why not, undue hardship if applicable, direct threat if applicable.
- For **pregnancy / accommodation** claims, address PDA, PWFA (Pregnant Workers Fairness Act, effective June 27, 2023 — requires reasonable accommodation for pregnancy/childbirth/related conditions absent undue hardship), and state pregnancy accommodation laws.
- Attach (or reference and produce on request) **supporting documentation**: anti-harassment / EEO policies; complaint and investigation records (with privilege considerations); performance documentation; comparator records (redacted as needed); written warnings; signed acknowledgments.
- Mark the document and exhibits **"Confidential — Submitted to {EEOC / agency}"**; recognize EEOC may share with CP per 2018 procedural updates and assume CP will see the statement.
- Address the **mediation posture** explicitly — open / not open / open under specified conditions — without conceding liability.
- Preserve all defenses (timeliness, jurisdictional, exhaustion, scope of charge) without waiving them by failing to raise.
- Address timeliness: 300 days in deferral states, 180 days in non-deferral states under Title VII / ADA / GINA; ADEA varies by state; state agencies may have different filing windows. Calculate from the date of the discrete act (or continuing-violation framework for hostile-work-environment per *Morgan*).
- Use `[CITE: ...]` placeholders for any case or statutory authority not verified.

**Must Not:**
- Concede protected-class status disputes unnecessarily (e.g., dispute coverage thresholds where in good faith disputable; do not concede ADA "qualified individual" status without analysis).
- Argue facts not in evidence or attribute statements to witnesses without verification.
- Use ad hominem framing of the CP — undermines credibility with the investigator.
- Treat the position statement as a litigation brief — agency tone, factual focus, not adversarial polemic.
- Disclose privileged investigation work product without considering waiver; if relying on the investigation report, consider scope and selective-waiver doctrine in the jurisdiction.
- Make factual concessions in a "background" or "summary" section that contradict the detailed chronology.
- Cite cases or statutes not verified — use `[CITE: ...]`.
- Use generic "consult counsel" disclaimers.
- Waive timeliness or jurisdictional defenses by failing to raise them.

## Instructions

1. **Confirm charge scope and timeliness.** Read the charge narrative. Identify each protected basis, each issue, and each date. Calculate filing deadlines and flag untimeliness as a defense.
2. **Pull the personnel file and documentary record.** Reviews, warnings, policies, training records, complaint history, RIF documentation, accommodation records.
3. **Identify the decision-maker(s) and their awareness** of any protected activity at the time of the decision.
4. **Build the factual chronology.** Dated, sourced, contextual.
5. **Articulate the legitimate non-discriminatory reason** with specificity. Pull from the pre-termination risk review if available.
6. **Run the comparator analysis** — identify similarly-situated employees and document consistent treatment.
7. **Address each protected basis and issue** the charge raises. Do not leave any unaddressed.
8. **Build the pretext rebuttal**: consistency of reasons, policy compliance, temporal proximity (or lack thereof), statistical patterns.
9. **Address claim-specific defenses** — Faragher/Ellerth (harassment), interactive process (ADA), PWFA accommodation (pregnancy), RFOA (ADEA RIF), §1981 / 42 U.S.C. § 1981a tier issues.
10. **Compile exhibits**: policies, complaint records, investigation summary (with privilege calibrated), performance docs, comparator records (redacted).
11. **State the mediation posture**.
12. **Final review**: factual accuracy, internal consistency, timeliness defenses preserved, no inadvertent admissions, tone appropriate for agency audience.

## Verification

- [ ] Every protected basis and every issue in the charge is addressed.
- [ ] Factual chronology is dated, sourced, and complete.
- [ ] Legitimate non-discriminatory reason is articulated with specificity for each adverse action.
- [ ] Comparator analysis identifies similarly-situated employees outside the protected class with material-similarity basis.
- [ ] Pretext rebutted on consistency, policy compliance, proximity, and statistical patterns.
- [ ] Retaliation analysis addresses each element with decision-maker awareness.
- [ ] Faragher/Ellerth, ADA interactive process, PWFA, or RFOA defenses raised as applicable.
- [ ] Timeliness and exhaustion defenses preserved.
- [ ] Exhibits compiled; privileged materials calibrated.

- [ ] Mediation posture stated.
- [ ] `[CITE: ...]` placeholders used for unverified authority.

## False-Positive Prevention

| Failure mode | Correction |
|---|---|
| Generic "performance" as the legitimate reason | Articulate dated, documented, quantified failures |
| Failing to address all protected bases alleged | Address each basis and each issue separately |
| Comparator analysis without material-similarity basis | Specify same supervisor, same standards, comparable conduct, similar time frame |
| Treating position statement as litigation brief | Agency tone, factual focus, no polemic |
| Ad hominem framing of CP | Strike; investigator credibility loss |
| Conceding ADA "qualified individual" without analysis | Address each prong if disputable |
| Faragher/Ellerth defense asserted without policy + prompt-response documentation | Build the record before asserting |
| Disclosing investigation work product without privilege analysis | Calibrate disclosure; consider selective-waiver doctrine |
| Failing to preserve timeliness defense | Reserve it expressly even when responding on the merits |
| Inadvertent admission in executive summary or background | Audit against detailed chronology |
| Assuming CP will not see the statement | EEOC may share per 2018 procedural updates; write accordingly |
| Citing temporal proximity without addressing decision-maker knowledge | Knowledge is a necessary element of causation |
| Fabricated cites | Use `[CITE: ...]` placeholders |
| Generic "consult counsel" closing | Replace with mediation posture and substantive conclusion |
