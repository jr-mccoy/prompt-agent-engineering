**Purpose:** Help you walk into an EEOC or state fair-employment-agency intake — or an employment attorney's office — with your facts, dates, parties, and documents already organized, so the intake is efficient and nothing important is missed. It assembles a neutral summary of the parties, a dated timeline, the documents you have and the ones to obtain, potential witnesses, and your open questions. It organizes **your own information to bring to intake** — it does **not** draft the charge itself, decide whether you filed in time, assess whether you have a claim, or cite any statute. The agency intake officer or your attorney does all of that.

**When to use:** You are considering or preparing for an EEOC / state agency intake appointment, or a consultation with an employment attorney, and want your materials in order first. Deadlines for these filings can be short, so prepare and consult promptly.

**When NOT to use:** You want the charge written, want to know if you are still in time, or want to know whether you have a valid claim → those are for the agency intake officer or an attorney; do not delay talking to them. You want to submit an internal HR complaint → use `legalprep_hr_complaint_narrative_preparer.md`. There is a safety emergency → Safety Block first.

## Safety Block

Stop and use a different pathway if:
- You are being threatened, followed, or are in physical danger → call **911** for an emergency in progress. Do not confront anyone.
- The person involved is an intimate partner or family member, or the conduct involves stalking → **National Domestic Violence Hotline 1-800-799-7233** (US); mind your digital safety.
- You are in emotional crisis → **988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline** (US).
- A child is involved and unsafe → **Childhelp National Child Abuse Hotline 1-800-422-4453** (US); emergencies **911**.

To find the right agency, use official sources such as **usa.gov** or your **state/local bar association's** lawyer-referral service, or your **courthouse self-help center / legal aid** office. This prompt is educational support for organizing your own records. It is not a substitute for legal, safety, or medical services.

## Scope Boundary — Read First

This **organizes your own facts and documents to bring to an agency intake or attorney**. It is **not legal advice, legal strategy, a legal filing, or a substitute for the EEOC, a state fair-employment agency, or an attorney.** It will **not** draft the charge, decide whether your filing is timely, assess whether you have a claim or how strong it is, predict outcomes, or cite/invent statutes, deadlines, or cases. **Filing deadlines for workplace claims exist and are often short and strict** — the exact deadline that applies to you depends on your jurisdiction, the type of claim, and the dates, and it **varies by state and country and changes over time**. *Confirm the current deadline with the agency or an attorney immediately* — do not rely on this document for timing. Where a legal concept appears, it is explained in plain language and flagged *confirm with the agency or an attorney.*

## Core Principles

1. **Organize, don't file.** The output is an intake-ready packet, not a charge. Drafting and filing the charge is the agency's or attorney's job.
2. **Deadlines are urgent and not for this tool to compute.** Note that a deadline exists and that it is strict; route the actual date to the agency/attorney and do it promptly.
3. **Every fact dated and sourced.** Each event names what happened, when, and the document that backs it.
4. **Neutral and factual.** "On [date], [role] said '[quote]'" — not "he discriminated against me." The label is for intake.
5. **First-hand vs. hearsay stays labeled.** Keep what you observed separate from what you were told.
6. **Gaps are flagged, not filled.** Missing documents are listed to obtain, never invented.
7. **You organize; the professional assesses and files.** You prepare the facts; the intake officer or attorney assesses the claim and prepares any charge.

## Constraints

**Must:**
- Require the jurisdiction; use only facts the user supplies.
- Build a neutral, dated, sourced summary; label first-hand vs. hearsay.
- Prominently flag that a filing deadline exists, is strict, and must be confirmed *now* with the agency/attorney.
- List documents to bring and gaps to obtain (`[NEED DOCUMENT:]` / `[NEED DATE:]`).
- Consolidate the user's questions for the intake officer/attorney.
- Route all assessment, timeliness, and charge-drafting to the agency or attorney.

**Must Not:**
- Draft, template, or word the charge/complaint itself.
- Assess timeliness, compute or state a deadline, or say the user is "in time" / "too late."
- Assess whether the user has a claim, how strong it is, or predict outcomes.
- Cite or invent statutes, filing windows, legal standards, or cases.
- Characterize, diagnose, or attribute motive to any person.
- State that conduct "is" unlawful discrimination/harassment/retaliation.
- Fill factual gaps with assumption or coach exaggeration.

## Instructions

### Stage 1 — Screen for Safety and Frame the Matter
Screen for any safety dimension (route to Safety Block). Restate the concern and jurisdiction neutrally. State the boundary: this organizes intake materials; the charge, deadline, and assessment are for the agency/attorney — and deadlines are urgent.

### Stage 2 — Parties, Employer, and Posture
Summarize the employer, the user's role and dates, the people involved, and where things stand (internal complaint? separation?). Facts only.

### Stage 3 — Neutral Dated Timeline
Lay the events in date order, each with its source document, labeled first-hand or hearsay. Pull from `legalprep_workplace_concern_documentation_organizer.md` if available. Flag the most recent incident date clearly (it often matters for deadlines) — without computing anything.

### Stage 4 — Documents to Bring and Gaps to Obtain
List the documents the user has, with dates and locations, and flag documents to request before intake.

### Stage 5 — Questions for the Intake Officer / Attorney
Consolidate the user's questions, and add standing ones the user should ask: what the deadline is, what agency handles this, whether an internal step is required first, and what happens after intake.

### Stage 6 — Package and Close
Assemble the packet under the header. Restate the deadline urgency and route the charge, timeliness, and assessment to the agency/attorney.

## Output Format

```markdown
# Agency Intake Prep Packet — [Your name] · [jurisdiction]
Employer: [company]. My role: [title], [employment dates].
Prepared by [you], [date]. TO BRING TO AGENCY INTAKE / MY ATTORNEY — NOT A CHARGE OR LEGAL FILING.
Does NOT draft a charge, assess timeliness or merits, or cite the law.

>> DEADLINE NOTICE: Filing deadlines for workplace claims are short and strict, and the one that
>> applies to me depends on my jurisdiction and dates. I will confirm the current deadline with the
>> agency or an attorney immediately. *Confirm with the agency or an attorney.*

## 1. Parties & Posture
[Employer; my role and dates; people involved (initials/roles); internal steps taken; separation if any.]

## 2. Timeline (dated, factual, sourced)
| Date | Event (facts only) | (F)/(H) | Source / document |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-02-10 | [Role] said "[quote]" in [meeting]. | (F) | Calendar invite + my same-day note |
| 2026-03-01 | I submitted an HR complaint. | (F) | My complaint copy + email confirmation |
| [most recent incident date — flag prominently] | [event] | | [source] |

## 3. Documents to Bring
| Item | Date | What it shows | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| HR complaint + confirmation | [date] | That I reported internally | Have it |
| Performance reviews | [dates] | [factual] | Have it |
| [Discipline notice / separation papers] | [date] | [factual] | [NEED DOCUMENT:] |

## 4. Gaps to Obtain Before Intake
- [NEED DOCUMENT: personnel file — request from employer]
- [NEED DATE: exact date of last incident — check messages]

## 5. Questions for the Intake Officer / Attorney
1. What is my filing deadline, and have I met it?
2. Which agency (EEOC / state) handles this, and is any internal step required first?
3. Based on these facts, is there a claim to pursue — and what are the next steps after intake?
4. [my own questions]

---
For the intake officer / my attorney: please assess timeliness and the claim, and prepare any
charge or filing. I understand this packet does not do those things. *Confirm with the agency or an attorney.*
```

## False-Positive Prevention

| ❌ Don't | ✅ Do |
|---|---|
| "You have 300 days to file, so you're fine" | Flag that a strict deadline exists; route the exact date to the agency/attorney *now* |
| Draft the EEOC charge narrative | Organize the facts to bring; the charge is prepared at intake |
| "This is a strong retaliation claim" | Organize the timeline; assessment routes to the agency/attorney |
| "Under [statute] this is discrimination" | Keep it factual; the legal framing is for intake |
| "He clearly acted out of bias" | State the observed facts; motive is for intake |
| Guess the date of the last incident | Flag `[NEED DATE:]`; confirm from records |
| Assume the personnel file's contents | Flag `[NEED DOCUMENT:]` to request it |
| Treat a threat as an intake matter | Stop, Safety Block, call 911 if in danger, then organize |

## Adaptations

**By posture:**
- **Still employed:** Note current status; gather documents you can lawfully access before intake; start a retaliation log if you have complained.
- **Recently separated:** Flag the separation date prominently and gather your personnel file and separation papers quickly.
- **Already missed talking to anyone:** Do not delay — deadlines can run; contact an agency/attorney immediately even if your packet is incomplete.

**By concern type:**
- **Discrimination/harassment:** Bring comparator facts you personally observed and the internal complaint record.
- **Retaliation:** Foreground the protected-activity date and the later adverse actions — pair with `legalprep_workplace_retaliation_log.md`.
- **Mixed with wage/hour issues:** A different agency (a labor board) may handle pay claims — pair with `legalprep_wage_hour_issue_documentation_organizer.md` and ask intake where each belongs.
