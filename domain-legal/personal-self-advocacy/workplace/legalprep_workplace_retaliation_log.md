**Purpose:** Help you keep a running, dated log that records **a protected activity** — a complaint or report you made — and then the adverse actions you observe at work afterward, each written factually with its source and date. The point is a clean chronological record connecting *what you did* to *what happened next*, so an attorney or agency can assess it. It records **your own observations** — it does **not** conclude that any action "is" retaliation, decide whether one thing caused another, weigh how strong the connection is, cite statutes, or draft a filing.

**When to use:** You have made an internal complaint, filed with an agency, reported a safety issue, requested an accommodation, participated in an investigation, or otherwise raised a concern — and you want to track what happens afterward while it is fresh and accurate. Keep adding to one running log over time.

**When NOT to use:** You want to know whether what happened is legally retaliation, or whether the timing "proves" causation → that is legal analysis; take the log to an attorney or agency. You want to organize the underlying concern itself → use `legalprep_workplace_concern_documentation_organizer.md`. There is a safety emergency → Safety Block first.

## Safety Block

Stop and use a different pathway if:
- You are being threatened, followed, or are in physical danger → call **911** for an emergency in progress. Do not confront anyone.
- The person involved is an intimate partner or family member, or the conduct involves stalking → **National Domestic Violence Hotline 1-800-799-7233** (US); mind your digital safety.
- You are in emotional crisis → **988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline** (US).
- A child is involved and unsafe → **Childhelp National Child Abuse Hotline 1-800-422-4453** (US); emergencies **911**.

This prompt is educational support for organizing your own records. It is not a substitute for legal, safety, or medical services.

## Scope Boundary — Read First

This **maintains a dated factual log from your own observations**. It is **not legal advice, legal strategy, a legal filing, or a substitute for an attorney or your jurisdiction's employment law.** It will **not** conclude that any action "is" retaliation, decide whether your protected activity *caused* a later action, assess how strong the connection is, predict outcomes, or cite/invent statutes, standards, or cases. Whether an adverse action is legally retaliatory — and whether the timing supports a claim — depends on facts and law that **vary by state and country and change over time** and are for an attorney or agency. A close time gap between a complaint and an adverse action is a fact worth recording; whether it *means* anything legally is **not** for this log to decide. Where a legal concept appears it is explained in plain language and flagged *confirm with an attorney or agency.*

---

## Core Principles

1. **Record the link; don't conclude it.** The log places the protected activity and the later action side by side. Whether one caused the other is for an attorney/agency, never the log.
2. **Anchor the protected activity precisely.** Its exact date, channel, and recipient matter — capture them first.
3. **Each adverse action is dated, factual, and sourced.** "On [date], my schedule was cut from [X] to [Y] hours" with the schedule as source — not "they punished me."
4. **First-hand vs. hearsay stays labeled.** What you observed is separate from what a coworker told you.
5. **Describe the action; don't attribute motive.** "Assigned to [task] on [date]" — not "assigned it to force me out."
6. **Note the time gap as a neutral fact.** You may record "N days after my complaint" as arithmetic on dates; do not call it proof of anything.
7. **You log; the professional assesses.** You keep the factual record; an attorney/agency assesses causation, significance, and any claim.

## Constraints

**Must:**
- Require the jurisdiction; use only facts the user supplies.
- Anchor the protected activity with its date, channel, and recipient.
- Log each adverse action factually, dated, sourced, and labeled (F)/(H).
- Record the day-gap between the activity and each action as neutral arithmetic only.
- Flag missing dates/documents as `[NEED DATE:]` / `[NEED DOCUMENT:]`.
- Route all causation, significance, and claim questions to an attorney or agency.

**Must Not:**
- Conclude or state that an action "is" retaliation.
- Decide or assert that the protected activity *caused* an adverse action.
- Assess strength, weigh the timing as "proof," or predict outcomes.
- Cite or invent statutes, standards, burdens, or cases.
- Characterize, diagnose, or attribute motive to any person.
- Merge first-hand observation with hearsay.
- Fill gaps with assumption or coach exaggeration.

## Instructions

### Stage 1 — Screen for Safety and Frame the Task
Screen for any safety dimension (route to Safety Block). Restate the jurisdiction and the boundary: this logs the facts; whether they show retaliation is for an attorney/agency.

### Stage 2 — Anchor the Protected Activity
Capture what the user did, its exact date, the channel, the recipient, and — factually — who knew about it and how the user knows. Flag any gap.

### Stage 3 — Log Each Adverse Action
For each action, record the date, a factual description, who did/decided it (if known), and the source document. Label (F) first-hand or (H) hearsay. Strip motive and legal labels.

### Stage 4 — Record the Time Relationship Neutrally
For each action, note the number of days after the protected activity as plain arithmetic. Do not interpret it.

### Stage 5 — Documents and Gaps
List the source document for each entry; flag documents to obtain.

### Stage 6 — Maintain and Route
Note that this is a living log to keep updating. Route causation and claim questions to an attorney/agency; feed the log into `legalprep_eeoc_agency_charge_preparation_organizer.md` when preparing for intake.

## Output Format

```markdown
# Workplace Retaliation Log — [Your name] · [jurisdiction]
Employer: [company]. My role: [title], [employment dates].
Started [date]; last updated [date]. FOR MY RECORDS / MY ATTORNEY OR AGENCY — NOT A LEGAL FILING.
Does NOT conclude anything is retaliation, decide causation, or assess strength.
Key: (F) = First-hand. (H) = Heard from others / hearsay.

## Protected Activity (the anchor)
- What I did: [complaint / report / accommodation request / investigation participation].
- Date: [YYYY-MM-DD]  · Channel: [email/portal/in person] · Recipient: [role/name].
- Who was aware (and how I know, factually): [role] — [how I know]. [NEED: confirm awareness fact]
- Supporting document: [my complaint copy + confirmation] / [NEED DOCUMENT:]

## Adverse Actions Observed Since
| # | Date | Days after activity | What happened (facts only) | Who did/decided it | (F)/(H) | Source document |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 2026-03-08 | 7 | Schedule cut from 38 to 22 hrs/week. | [Manager role] | (F) | Schedule (screenshot) |
| 2 | 2026-03-20 | 19 | Removed from [project] I had led. | [role] | (F) | Reassignment email |
| 3 | 2026-04-02 | 32 | Written warning for [stated reason]. | [role] | (F) | Warning notice (copy) |
| 4 | [date] | [n] | [Coworker told me [X] was said about me] | [unknown] | (H) | [my note of what I was told] |

## Documents & Gaps
- Have: complaint copy, schedules, reassignment email, warning notice.
- [NEED DOCUMENT: prior schedules to show the change]
- [NEED DATE: exact date of Action 4 — confirm with coworker]

## Notes (facts only, no conclusions)
- [e.g., "No performance concerns were raised to me before [date]" — if true and first-hand.]

---
For my attorney or the agency: please assess whether these facts support a claim, including
causation and timing. I understand this log does not decide that. *Confirm with an attorney or agency.*
```

## False-Positive Prevention

| ❌ Don't | ✅ Do |
|---|---|
| "The warning was retaliation for your complaint" | Log both events with dates; causation is for an attorney/agency |
| "The 7-day gap proves they retaliated" | Record "7 days after my complaint" as a fact; do not call it proof |
| "They cut your hours to punish you" | "Schedule cut from 38 to 22 hrs on [date]"; motive is for counsel |
| "This is a slam-dunk retaliation case" | Maintain the log; assessment routes to a professional |
| Merge "a coworker told me" with what you saw | Label (F) vs. (H) for every entry |
| Guess who decided the action | Record "[unknown]" or flag `[NEED: confirm decision-maker]` |
| Assume everyone knew about your complaint | Record only awareness you can factually support; flag the rest |
| Treat a threat as a log entry | Stop, Safety Block, call 911 if in danger, then log |

## Adaptations

**By protected activity:**
- **Internal HR complaint:** Anchor to your submission date and confirmation; keep your complaint copy as the source.
- **Agency charge:** Anchor to the filing/receipt date; note who at the employer was notified and when, if known.
- **Accommodation or safety report:** Anchor to the request/report date and its recipient.
- **Investigation participation (as witness):** Anchor to your interview date; keep the description factual.

**By action type:**
- **Schedule/pay/assignment changes:** Keep the before-and-after documents so the change is visible in the record.
- **Discipline:** Keep the notice and any prior reviews; do not argue whether the stated reason is pretext — that is for counsel.
- **Termination:** Record the date, the stated reason, and who delivered it; gather your personnel file promptly.
