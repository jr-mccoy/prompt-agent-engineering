# Personal Legal Self-Advocacy (Layperson-Facing Preparation)

Prompts for an **individual handling their own side** of a personal legal matter that is **not**
family law — a workplace problem, harassment or stalking, defamation, intellectual-property
theft, a consumer dispute or scam, a landlord–tenant issue, identity theft, debt collection, or
a small-claims matter. They help you **organize, document, and prepare**: build factual records
and timelines, preserve evidence, and — where a channel is designed for non-lawyers — draft
**your own** factual account or letter (an HR complaint, a police-report account, a platform/DMCA
report, an FTC/agency report, a correction request, a debt-validation letter). Then they route
the legal questions to an attorney or the right authority.

> ## Read This First — What These Prompts Are (and Are Not)
>
> **They organize, document, and prepare your own information.** They do **not** give legal
> advice, legal strategy, or legal conclusions, and they are **not** a substitute for a licensed
> attorney or your jurisdiction's law. They will **not** tell you that something "is" harassment,
> defamation, retaliation, or infringement "under the law," predict how a court or agency will
> rule, assess how strong your matter is, cite or invent statutes or case law, or draft a court
> filing. Those questions are **routed to your attorney or the relevant authority.**
>
> **This is the second subsection of `domain-legal/` that inverts the domain's attorney-only,
> disclaimer-free convention** (the first is `family-self-advocacy/`, which covers divorce and
> custody). The rest of `domain-legal/` is written for practitioners and deliberately omits
> disclaimers. This subsection is written for a **layperson**, in plain language, with an
> explicit and load-bearing not-legal-advice boundary.

## Conventions (every prompt in this set)

- **Audience:** a layperson preparing their own side. Plain language, second person.
- **Required jurisdiction input.** Standards, deadlines, forms, and procedure **vary by
  state/country and change over time.** Any legal concept is rendered as plain-language literacy
  flagged *confirm with counsel for your jurisdiction.*
- **No fabrication of facts.** These prompts organize **only your own information**. They never
  invent facts, dates, documents, witnesses, dollar amounts, or events; never characterize,
  diagnose, or attribute motive to the other party; never coach you to exaggerate, manufacture,
  or provoke. Missing items are flagged as gaps to obtain (`[NEED DOCUMENT:]`, `[NEED DATE:]`),
  never filled with assumptions.
- **No legal conclusions.** Whether conduct is legally actionable is for your attorney. These
  tools describe what happened, factually and sourced — they do not label it.
- **Every fact dated and sourced; neutral tone throughout.** Neutral, sourced records are more
  credible and more useful to counsel and to an agency than argument.
- **Lawful evidence only.** These prompts never advise surveillance, non-consensual recording,
  or accessing someone else's accounts — methods vary in legality by jurisdiction
  (*confirm with counsel what is lawful where you live*).
- **Self-submit documents are yours.** Where a prompt helps you draft something you submit
  yourself (HR complaint, police account, DMCA notice, agency report, dispute letter), the output
  is **your own** first-person factual document, labeled "NOT A LEGAL FILING." Any statutory
  certification (e.g. a DMCA good-faith / penalty-of-perjury statement) is presented as **your
  own attestation to read, verify, and sign** — never asserted for you.
- **Mandatory Safety Block.** Abuse, domestic violence, stalking, threats, or an emergency →
  stop and use the safety pathway: **National Domestic Violence Hotline 1-800-799-7233 ·
  Childhelp 1-800-422-4453 · 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline · 911** (US). For fraud/identity
  theft: **IdentityTheft.gov · ReportFraud.ftc.gov · ic3.gov**. Documentation supports but never
  replaces protective action.
- **Verification + false-positive block** end every prompt (jurisdiction flagged; facts dated and
  sourced; neutral; no characterization; no legal conclusion; no outcome prediction; no invented
  standards; legal questions routed; safety screened).
- **File naming:** `legalprep_{function}.md`.

## How These Compose — the "Organize / Prepare → Route" Pipeline

```
cross-cutting anchors (router · incident record · evidence preservation · chronology)
                              ↓
      matter-specific documentation organizers + logs
                              ↓
   self-submit preparers  ──►  the right channel yourself
   (HR · police · platform/DMCA · FTC/agency · landlord · collector)
                              ↓
   legalprep_professional_handoff_brief.md  ──►  your attorney OR the authority
                              ↓
        legal advice · strategy · court filings  ──►  attorney
```

## Where These Route (boundaries)

- **Legal advice, strategy, legal conclusions, court filings** → your attorney. The
  attorney-side counterparts live elsewhere in `domain-legal/` (e.g.
  `../employment-labor/`, `../ip/`, `../litigation/`).
- **Family law** (divorce, custody, protective orders in a family context) → `../family-self-advocacy/`
  and the attorney-side `../divorce/` and `../custody/`.
- **An active safety emergency** → the Safety Block pathway (911, DV hotline), an advocate, and
  your attorney — not a document.
- **Fraud / identity theft / scams** → also report promptly via **IdentityTheft.gov**,
  **ReportFraud.ftc.gov**, **ic3.gov**, and your bank — reporting is separate from any legal action.
- **Ordinary, non-adversarial written requests** — cancelling a subscription, an account or data
  deletion request, a warranty claim, a hardship or fee-waiver request, an insurance or benefits
  appeal, a records request, a regulator complaint → [`domain-written-advocacy/`](../../domain-written-advocacy/README.md).
  That is a **separate top-level domain**, not a subsection of `domain-legal/`. It covers matters
  that are not legal disputes but benefit from a dated paper trail, and it uses a lighter
  convention set (no mandatory crisis Safety Block; a proportionate Boundary & Routing Block
  instead). Its prompts route **here** the moment a matter turns adversarial, and they deliberately
  **do not duplicate** what this set already covers — refunds and chargebacks, debt validation,
  security deposits, HR complaints, DMCA notices, and fraud-scoped credit disputes are cross-linked
  from there rather than re-covered.
