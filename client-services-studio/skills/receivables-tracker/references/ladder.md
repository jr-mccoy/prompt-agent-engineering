# The collections escalation ladder

Seven rungs. One at a time, never repeated, never threatened unless it will be
executed.

| # | Rung | Trigger (days overdue) | Action | Relationship cost |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | AP confirmation | 3 | Contact accounts payable directly. Confirm receipt, approval, PO validity, payment run | none |
| 2 | Written follow-up with evidence | 14 | Email AP, sponsor copied, re-attaching invoice and evidence. One direct question: is anything preventing payment? | negligible |
| 3 | Sponsor escalation | 30 | To the person who wanted the work. Frame as unblocking an internal process | low |
| 4 | Notice of suspension | 45 | Written notice that work pauses on a stated date. Requires a contractual right and ongoing work | material |
| 5 | Suspension, executed | 52 | Stop. Confirm in writing with what is required to resume | high |
| 6 | Formal notice before action | 60 | Debt, contractual basis, interest, final deadline, intended next step. Take legal advice first | relationship ends |
| 7 | Handoff | 75 | Demand letter, collections agency, or recorded write-off | relationship ended |

## Rules enforced in code

1. **A dispute is never escalated.** Resolve the substance first. Escalating a
   disputed invoice hardens the position and stalls the undisputed portion.
2. **An undiagnosed invoice is sent back to triage.** Roughly half of overdue
   services invoices are administrative and resolve at rung 1; escalating one of
   those costs a relationship over a missing PO number.
3. **A rung fires only when its trigger is met.**
4. **A rung is never repeated.** A second copy of the same email resets the clock
   and teaches the client that nothing follows. Staying on rung 2 indefinitely is
   the single most common reason invoices reach 120 days.
5. **Suspension is skipped where it is unavailable** — no contractual right, or the
   work has already finished. The ladder then waits for rung 6 rather than
   proposing a notice that could not be executed.
6. **Distress compresses every trigger by half.** The creditor queue forms quickly
   and position matters.

## Branches, not rungs

- **Payment plan offered** → document it in writing with dates and amounts, then
  restart at rung 2 from the first missed instalment.
- **Dispute emerges mid-ladder** → stop, return to triage. The cause has changed.
- **Write-off** → rung 7's legitimate branch. A £2,000 debt pursued through a
  process costing £1,500 and three weeks of attention is a worse outcome than a
  recorded write-off and three weeks of business development.

## Tone

Neutral, specific, factual, on every rung. State the amount, the date, the ask. Do
not apologise for requesting payment for work delivered, and do not express anger.
The practitioners who collect best are consistently unembarrassed and consistently
procedural.

## Not legal advice

Pre-action protocols, statutory interest entitlement, suspension rights and
limitation periods vary by jurisdiction and contract. Take advice before rung 6,
and certainly before rung 7. See
`domain-legal/client-intake-communications/legal_demand_letter_drafter.md`.
