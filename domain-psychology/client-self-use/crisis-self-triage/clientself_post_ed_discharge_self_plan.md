# First 72 Hours After ED Discharge — Personal Plan

> **IF THINGS GET WORSE OR THOUGHTS OF SUICIDE RETURN WITH A PLAN OR URGE TO ACT: call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, US), call 911, or go back to the nearest emergency department (ED) now.** The hours and days right after an ED discharge are a high-risk window — going back in is not a failure, it's the plan working. This is a self-support aid to organize your first 72 hours; it is **not** an emergency service or a substitute for your discharge instructions or clinician.

## Objective

Turn the most fragile window — the first 72 hours after leaving the ED for a mental-health crisis — into a concrete, written plan covering means-safety, support, follow-up lock-in, medication continuity, and clear go-back signs. The output is meant to be brought to your outpatient clinician.

## When to Use

- You've just been discharged from an ED (or are about to be) after a mental-health crisis.
- A loved one wants to help you set up the next few days.
- Your discharge paperwork is vague and you want a plan you can actually follow.

## Inputs / Context

- The date/time you left (or will leave) the ED.
- Your discharge instructions and any follow-up already scheduled.
- Medications you were told to take, restart, or stop.
- What's at home that could be a means of self-harm.
- Who can be with you or check in over the next 3 days.
- Your own early warning signs that things are sliding.

## Constraints

### Must

- Open the output with the 988/911/ED escalation block, visible before anything else.
- Cover six sections: **Means-Safety at Home**, **Who's With Me (next 72h)**, **Follow-Up Lock-In**, **Medication Continuity**, **Warning Signs = Go Back**, **Crisis Routing (988/911/ED)**.
- Make follow-up concrete: an actual appointment time, or the exact call to make tomorrow morning and the backup if it can't be reached.
- Means-safety must name removing/securing specific items and who holds them.
- End with a one-line handoff: "Bring this to my outpatient clinician."
- Validate (NE-07): going back to the ED is the plan working, not relapse.

### Must Not

- Do not contradict the person's discharge instructions; defer medical/medication specifics to clinician and paperwork.
- Do not give medication dosing advice.
- Do not minimize the risk window or imply they should "tough it out" instead of returning.
- Do not promise confidentiality about safety from the people supporting them.

## Instructions

1. Lead with the escalation block.
2. Fill means-safety first (highest-leverage step in this window).
3. Pin support people to specific time blocks across 72 hours.
4. Lock the follow-up appointment or the exact call + backup.
5. Capture medication continuity per discharge paperwork (no dosing advice).
6. List personalized go-back warning signs.
7. Close with crisis routing and the clinician handoff line.

## Output Format

```
=== MY FIRST 72 HOURS — POST-ED PLAN ===

⚠️ IF THINGS GET WORSE: call/text 988 · call 911 · go back to the ED now.
Going back in is the plan working, not failing. This is a support aid, not an emergency service.
Discharged: [date/time] ____________

1) MEANS-SAFETY AT HOME (do first):
- Items to remove/lock: [medications, weapon, other] → held by [person] at [location]
- Done by: [time]   Who confirmed: ____________

2) WHO'S WITH ME (next 72 hours):
- Tonight (0–12h): [person / how reached]
- Day 1: [person / check-in times]
- Day 2–3: [person / check-in times]
- If no one is available: call/text 988 to talk it through

3) FOLLOW-UP LOCK-IN:
- Appointment already set: [provider, date/time] — OR —
- Call to make tomorrow AM: [clinic + number] at [time]
- Backup if I can't reach them: [crisis line 988 / warm line / clinic triage]

4) MEDICATION CONTINUITY (per my discharge paperwork — no changes on my own):
- Take/restart: [as written on paperwork]
- Stop/hold: [as written]
- Refill needed by: [date] → how I'll get it: ____________
- Questions for my clinician about meds: ____________

5) WARNING SIGNS = GO BACK:
- [My early sign #1, e.g., "thoughts come back with a plan"]
- [Sign #2, e.g., "can't keep myself safe alone tonight"]
- [Sign #3]
→ If any appear: 988 / 911 / return to ED.

6) CRISIS ROUTING:
- 988 (call or text) for rising distress or returning thoughts
- 911 / nearest ED for immediate danger

HANDOFF: Bring this page to my outpatient clinician at the first appointment. ☐
```

## Verification

- [ ] 988/911/ED escalation block at top and inside output.
- [ ] All six sections present.
- [ ] Means-safety names specific items and who holds them.
- [ ] Follow-up is a concrete appointment or exact call + backup.
- [ ] Medication section defers to discharge paperwork; no dosing advice.
- [ ] Personalized go-back warning signs listed.
- [ ] "Bring this to my outpatient clinician" handoff included.
- [ ] Returning to the ED framed as the plan working, not relapse.
