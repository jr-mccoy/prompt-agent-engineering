# Retirement Identity Reframe

## Objective

Help someone work through the identity shift of retirement — beyond the financial picture. Output should:

1. Name what work actually provided beyond income: structure, status, purpose, social connection, identity.
2. Validate that losing those can feel like grief, even when retirement was wanted.
3. Reframe and build a plan to rebuild each function in new forms.
4. Flag warning signs of depression that retirement can mask or trigger.
5. Offer a therapist handoff for stuck grief, depression, or relationship strain.

## When to Use

- Approaching, newly in, or a while into retirement and feeling unmoored, restless, invisible, or "useless."
- Surprised to feel low after a transition that was supposed to be a reward.
- Struggling with "Who am I now?" after a long career or a defining role.
- Noticing days losing shape, or social contact shrinking.

## Inputs / Context

- The career or role left, and how central it was to their identity.
- Whether retirement was chosen, forced, or phased.
- What they miss most (and least).
- Current daily structure, social contact, and sense of purpose.
- Mood, energy, sleep, and whether low mood is creeping in.

## Constraints

### Must

- Output sections in order: **What Work Gave Me (the four functions)**, **What I Actually Miss**, **The Grief I Didn't Expect**, **Rebuilding Each Function**, **Watching for Depression**, **A First Small Step**, **Bring to a Therapist**.
- Break down what work provided into at least: **structure**, **status/identity**, **purpose/contribution**, and **social connection** — then rebuild each separately.
- Validate that grief and disorientation are normal even when retirement was desired; don't treat low mood as ingratitude.
- Use a concrete, non-prescriptive reframe (work was one container for these needs; the needs remain and can be met other ways).
- Include warning signs of depression and an escalation path.

### Must Not

- Don't give financial or retirement-planning advice.
- Don't prescribe a specific "purpose" (volunteer! get a hobby!) — help the person find their own.
- Don't minimize the loss or imply they should just enjoy the free time.
- Don't pathologize normal adjustment, but don't ignore real depression either.

## Instructions

1. Map the four functions work provided for this person.
2. Distinguish what they miss from what they're glad to be rid of.
3. Name the unexpected grief and normalize it.
4. Rebuild each function in new, person-fit forms.
5. List depression warning signs and the escalation path.
6. Choose one small step and offer the therapist handoff.

## Output Format

```
=== RETIREMENT IDENTITY REFRAME ===

What Work Gave Me (beyond the paycheck):
- Structure: how my days were shaped: [...]
- Status / identity: who I was to others and myself: [...]
- Purpose / contribution: what I was building or helping with: [...]
- Social connection: the people and daily contact: [...]

What I Actually Miss (vs. what I'm glad to leave):
- Miss most: [...]
- Glad to be rid of: [...]

The Grief I Didn't Expect:
- What's surprising me: [...]
> It's normal to grieve this even though I wanted to retire. Low mood here isn't ingratitude.

Rebuilding Each Function (my own way, not a prescription):
- Structure → one anchor to my week: [...]
- Status / identity → how I want to introduce myself now: "[...]"
- Purpose → something that would feel like contributing: [...]
- Social → one relationship or group to invest in: [...]

Watching for Depression (this transition can mask it):
- Warning signs: persistent low mood 2+ weeks, loss of interest, sleeping too much/little,
  withdrawing, hopelessness, feeling like a burden.
- If these show up → contact a clinician this week.
- If I'm thinking about ending my life → call or text 988 (US) today, or go to the ER.

A First Small Step (this week):
- [One concrete, low-stakes action toward any function above.]

Bring to a Therapist:
- What I'd want help with: [grief / purpose / identity / relationship shift at home]
```

## Verification

- [ ] Four functions of work named and rebuilt separately.
- [ ] Miss vs. glad-to-leave distinguished.
- [ ] Grief normalized even when retirement was chosen.
- [ ] Reframe concrete and non-prescriptive; purpose not dictated.
- [ ] Depression warning signs and escalation (clinician this week; 988 today for SI).
- [ ] One small concrete first step.
- [ ] No financial advice; therapist handoff included.
