# Conflict Postmortem

## Objective

Help someone look back at a specific conflict and see the *cycle* rather than the content — what each person was protecting underneath the surface argument, the trigger that lit it, and one concrete experiment to break the loop next time. The point is the pattern, not winning the replay.

## When to Use

- A fight that ended badly or keeps repeating in the same shape.
- The "we always end up here" feeling with a partner, family member, or friend.
- Cooling off after a conflict and wanting to learn from it before re-engaging.

## Inputs / Context

- The conflict, in plain sequence (what was said, roughly).
- What the user felt in their body / mind at the peak.
- Their best honest guess at what the other person was feeling.
- What the fight was "officially" about vs. what it felt like underneath.
- Whether this is a recurring pattern.

## Constraints

### Must

- Output sections in order: **The Cycle (the loop we got into)**, **What I Was Protecting**, **What They May Have Been Protecting**, **The Trigger (where it lit)**, **One Experiment for Next Time**.
- Frame each person around an underlying need/fear (e.g., "to matter," "to not be controlled," "to be safe"), not around who's right.
- Keep the read of the other person tentative — a hypothesis, not a verdict on their inner life.
- Make the experiment one specific, small, repeatable move.

### Must Not

- Don't adjudicate who was right.
- Don't put words in the other person's mouth as fact; mark guesses as guesses.
- Don't collapse into self-blame or other-blame; the cycle is the unit.
- Don't replace a needed repair conversation — point to the repair drafter if one is owed.

## Instructions

1. Reconstruct the loop: trigger → each person's move → escalation → outcome.
2. Name what the user was protecting underneath their reaction.
3. Offer a tentative read of what the other person was protecting.
4. Locate the specific trigger moment where it tipped.
5. Design one small experiment for the next time the cycle starts.

## Output Format

```
=== CONFLICT POSTMORTEM ===

The Cycle (the loop we got into):
- It usually goes: [trigger] -> I [my move] -> they [their move] -> it escalates to [outcome].
- The shape repeats even when the topic changes.

What I Was Protecting (underneath my reaction):
- Surface: I was arguing about [topic].
- Underneath: I was protecting [need/fear — e.g., "feeling like I don't matter to them"].

What They May Have Been Protecting (my best guess — a hypothesis, not a fact):
- They seemed to be protecting [need/fear — e.g., "not being controlled" / "not failing me"].
- I'd want to check this with them rather than assume it.

The Trigger (where it lit):
- The moment it tipped was: "[the specific line / look / silence]."
- What it meant to me in that second: [...]

One Experiment for Next Time:
- When I notice [early sign the cycle is starting], I'll try: [one small move — e.g., "say 'I'm getting flooded, can we pause 20 minutes?'"].
- I'll judge it not by whether we agree, but by whether the loop changed shape.

If I owe a repair from this one, I'll draft it (see repair conversation drafter) before re-litigating the content.
```

## Verification

- [ ] Cycle reconstructed as a loop, not a transcript.
- [ ] User's protected need/fear named.
- [ ] Other person's read marked as a hypothesis.
- [ ] Specific trigger moment located.
- [ ] One small, repeatable experiment provided.
- [ ] No right/wrong verdict; repair handoff noted where owed.
