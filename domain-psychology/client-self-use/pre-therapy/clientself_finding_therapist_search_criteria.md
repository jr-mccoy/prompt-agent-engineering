# Finding a Therapist — Search Criteria & Consult Questions

## Objective

Help me convert what I need into a usable therapist search: concrete filters to type into a directory, a set of questions for the free consult call, and a short list of green and red flags so I know what I'm hearing. The output should be something I can act on this week.

## When to Use

- I'm staring at a directory (Psychology Today, my insurer's list, an app) and don't know how to narrow it.
- I've had a bad fit before and want to screen better this time.
- I have a consult call booked and want to know what to ask.

## Inputs / Context

- What I want help with, in my own words.
- Any approach I'm leaning toward (or "no idea").
- Identity factors that matter to me in a therapist (gender, language, faith, LGBTQ+ affirming, cultural background) — optional.
- Logistics: telehealth vs in-person, days/times, location.
- Money: insurance, budget per session, whether I'll pay out of pocket.

## Constraints

### Must

- Output sections in order: **Search Filters (copy into a directory)**, **Consult-Call Questions**, **Green Flags**, **Red Flags**, **My Shortlist Tracker**.
- Make filters concrete and directory-ready (e.g., "Specialty: anxiety; Modality: CBT or ACT; Accepts: [my insurance]; Format: telehealth; Within budget: $[X]/session or sliding scale").
- Treat identity factors as my preference to state, never a requirement I must justify; include them only if I raised them.
- Consult questions must cover: experience with my concern, approach, logistics/availability, cost and sliding scale, and a "how we'll know it's working" question.
- Green/red flags must be behavioral and observable on a call, not character judgments.
- Where money is a barrier, point me toward the affordability navigator (see related prompts) rather than just listing cost filters.

### Must Not

- Don't recommend specific named therapists, practices, or directories as endorsements.
- Don't diagnose me or tell me which specialty I "must" pick.
- Don't frame credentials or license type as the only thing that matters — fit and experience count too.
- Don't promise the first good-fit therapist will be easy to find.

## Instructions

1. Reflect back my needs and turn them into directory-ready filters.
2. Build the consult-call question set covering the required areas.
3. List behavioral green and red flags to listen for.
4. Give me a simple tracker to compare candidates.
5. Route me to the affordability navigator if cost is a barrier.

## Output Format

```
=== THERAPIST SEARCH KIT ===

Search Filters (copy into a directory):
- Specialty: [...]
- Approach/modality: [... or "open"]
- Accepts: [my insurance / out-of-pocket / sliding scale]
- Budget: [$__/session or "sliding scale needed"]
- Format: [telehealth / in-person / either]
- Availability: [days/times I can actually do]
- Identity preferences (optional): [only what I named]

Consult-Call Questions (the free 15-min call):
- "Have you worked with people dealing with [my concern]? About how often?"
- "How would you describe your approach?"
- "What's your availability, and is it a regular weekly slot?"
- "What's the cost per session? Do you offer a sliding scale or a reduced rate?"
- "How will we know together whether this is working for me?"

Green Flags (good signs on the call):
- Answers my questions directly and warmly
- Comfortable saying when something isn't their specialty and offering a referral
- Asks about my goals, not just symptoms
- Clear about fees, cancellation, and logistics

Red Flags (slow down / keep looking):
- Vague or defensive about approach, experience, or cost
- Guarantees a cure or a timeline
- Pushes me to commit before a consult
- Dismisses an identity factor I said matters to me

My Shortlist Tracker:
| Name | Takes my $ | Experience w/ concern | Vibe on call | Next step |
|------|-----------|------------------------|--------------|-----------|
| ...  |           |                        |              |           |

If cost is the blocker: see the insurance / EAP / sliding-scale navigator.
```

## Verification

- [ ] Filters are concrete and directory-ready.
- [ ] Identity preferences included only if I raised them, framed as preference.
- [ ] Consult questions cover experience, approach, logistics, cost, and "is it working."
- [ ] Green/red flags are behavioral and observable on a call.
- [ ] Affordability navigator referenced if money is a barrier.
- [ ] No named-therapist endorsements, no diagnosis, no false promises.
