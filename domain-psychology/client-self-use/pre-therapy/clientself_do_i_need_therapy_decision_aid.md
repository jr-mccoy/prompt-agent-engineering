# Do I Need Therapy? — Self-Decision Aid

## Objective

Help me weigh the honest signals about whether to start therapy and land on a clear lean — "worth starting now," "watch and reassess," or "this is urgent, don't wait." Sort my situation across functioning, duration, distress, and whether I've hit the ceiling of self-help, and surface safety escalation if it shows up.

## When to Use

- I keep wondering "is this bad enough for therapy?" and never decide.
- Something happened (loss, breakup, burnout, a scare) and I can't tell if it'll pass.
- Friends or family have suggested it and I'm not sure they're right.
- I've been white-knuckling on self-help (apps, books, podcasts) and want a gut-check.

## Inputs / Context

- What's going on, in my own words, and roughly when it started.
- How it's showing up in daily life (work/school, sleep, eating, relationships, things I've stopped doing).
- How much distress it causes, 0–10, on an average day.
- What I've already tried and whether it's still helping.
- Any safety signals: thoughts of suicide or self-harm, not feeling safe, using substances to cope in a way that scares me.
- Practical context: cost worries, time, past therapy experiences (good or bad).

## Constraints

### Must

- Run a **safety triage first.** If I mention suicidal thoughts, self-harm, harming someone, or not feeling safe, surface the escalation block immediately and route there before doing the weigh-up.
- Output sections in order: **Safety Check**, **What I'm Weighing (the four signals)**, **The Lean**, **What Would Change the Lean**, **If I Decide Yes — First Step**, **If I Decide Not Yet — Reassess Trigger**.
- Score each of the four signals (functioning, duration, distress, self-help ceiling) plainly with a one-line rationale grounded in what I said.
- State the lean as one of three: *worth starting now*, *watch and reassess*, *urgent — don't wait*, and say plainly why.
- Acknowledge uncertainty where the inputs are thin instead of forcing false confidence.

### Must Not

- Don't diagnose me or name a disorder.
- Don't set a "threshold of suffering" I have to clear to deserve help — therapy is allowed for ordinary distress.
- Don't pressure me toward or away from therapy; give the lean and what would move it.
- Don't promise outcomes ("therapy will fix this").

## Instructions

1. Triage safety first; if any safety signal appears, route to the escalation block before anything else.
2. Reflect back what I'm dealing with in plain language.
3. Score the four signals and explain each in one line.
4. Give the lean, with the reasoning visible.
5. Name what would change the lean in either direction.
6. Give a concrete first step for "yes" and a concrete reassess trigger for "not yet."

## Output Format

```
=== DO I NEED THERAPY? ===

Safety Check:
[ No safety signals noted — proceeding /
  Safety signal present — see escalation below, handle this first ]

ESCALATION (if any safety signal):
- This week: tell my doctor or a therapist, or use a same-week intake line.
- Today: if I'm thinking about suicide or self-harm, call or text 988 (US Suicide & Crisis Lifeline).
- Now: if I might act, or I don't feel safe, go to the nearest ER or call 911.

What I'm Weighing (the four signals):
- Functioning: [Low / Some / High impact] — [one line from what I said]
- Duration: [Days / Weeks / Months+] — [one line]
- Distress (0–10): [__] — [one line]
- Self-help ceiling: [Still helping / Plateaued / Not enough] — [one line]

The Lean:
[ Worth starting now / Watch and reassess / Urgent — don't wait ]
Why: [grounded in the signals above, 2–3 sentences]

What Would Change the Lean:
- Toward starting: [...]
- Toward waiting: [...]

If I Decide Yes — First Step:
- [One concrete next action, e.g., "look up 3 therapists who take my insurance this week"]
- Bring to a clinician this week if: [specific marker]

If I Decide Not Yet — Reassess Trigger:
- Re-run this check if: [specific marker, e.g., "still 6/10 in two weeks" or "sleep doesn't recover"]
```

## Verification

- [ ] Safety triaged before the weigh-up; escalation block present.
- [ ] All four signals scored with a one-line rationale each.
- [ ] Lean stated as one of the three options, with visible reasoning.
- [ ] What-would-change-it named in both directions.
- [ ] Concrete first step and concrete reassess trigger given.
- [ ] No diagnosis, no suffering-threshold gatekeeping, no pressure.
