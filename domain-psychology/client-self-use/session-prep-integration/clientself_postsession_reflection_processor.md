# Post-Session Reflection Processor (Client-Side)

## Objective

Walk a client through a 10-minute reflection within 24 hours of a session so the work has a chance to consolidate. Output:

1. What was actually discussed (the client's recall, not the therapist's chart).
2. What landed and what didn't.
3. What shifted (a thought, a feeling, a question, a willingness).
4. What to do between now and next session.

## When to Use

- Within 24 hours of a session (sooner is better).
- After an unusually intense or unusually flat session.
- When you walked out unsure what just happened.
- Before bed if the session is sitting heavy.

## Inputs / Context

- When the session was; how long ago.
- The first 1–2 things the client recalls discussing.
- Anything the therapist said that stuck (positively or negatively).
- Any homework / between-session focus assigned.
- The client's affective state walking out vs now.

## Constraints

### Must

- Output sections in order: **What We Talked About (recall)**, **What Landed**, **What Didn't Land or Felt Off**, **What Shifted (even slightly)**, **Homework / Between-Session Focus**, **Anything I Want to Bring Back Next Time**.
- Make space for ambivalence — sessions can leave a person more confused, not less, and that is allowed.
- "What didn't land" is normalized: it's information about the work, not failure.
- Translate any homework into a concrete first step ("Tuesday morning, while my coffee brews, I'll [...]") rather than a vague intention.

### Must Not

- Don't replay the session for the client; ask what they remember.
- Don't reinterpret the therapist's comments through a different framework.
- Don't push for emotional processing the client isn't doing.
- Don't generate a self-therapy intervention.

## Instructions

1. Ask: "When was the session, and what's the first thing that comes to mind when you think about it?"
2. Walk through each section with one open-ended question.
3. For homework, anchor it to a specific time and trigger.
4. End with a single carry-forward question for next session.

## Output Format

```
=== POST-SESSION REFLECTION ===

Session date / time: [...]    Reflection time: [...]

What We Talked About (my recall, not the chart):
- [Topic / thread #1]
- [Topic / thread #2]
- [...]

What Landed:
- [Thing said / question asked / observation made]
- Why it landed: [...]

What Didn't Land or Felt Off:
- [Thing said / interpretation offered / pacing of the session]
- What I notice about that: [...]

What Shifted (even slightly):
- [A thought, a feeling, a question, a willingness]

Homework / Between-Session Focus:
- Concrete first step: "[On day, at time, when trigger, I will action]"
- What might get in the way: [...]
- Backup if it does: [...]

For Next Session:
- One thing I want to bring back: [...]
```

## Verification

- [ ] Recall section uses client's words, not paraphrase.
- [ ] What-landed and what-didn't-land both present.
- [ ] Shift section honors small movements; "nothing shifted" is also valid.
- [ ] Homework anchored to specific time and trigger.
- [ ] Single carry-forward item for next session.
- [ ] Ambivalence and confusion are allowed.
- [ ] No re-interpretation of the therapist's work.
