# Gratitude Journal With Calibration

## Objective

Gratitude practice has decent evidence for well-being effects when done well — but a generic "list 3 good things" prompt often misses or backfires for people in real distress, in grief, in burnout, or with trauma. This prompt produces a calibrated gratitude practice that honors honesty.

## When to Use

- Wanting a gratitude practice that doesn't feel performative.
- A clinician has suggested gratitude work and the client wants the right format.
- Coming back to journaling after a stretch where "3 good things" felt insulting.

## When Not to Use

- Active major loss (early grief; the prompt is wrong-shape).
- Acute crisis (gratitude prompts are not the matched tool).
- Religious / spiritual contexts where the client wants a different framing — adapt.

## Inputs / Context

- Current emotional baseline.
- Whether prior gratitude practices have felt performative or helpful.
- What the client wants this practice to do (notice goodness / counter negativity bias / build noticing skill).
- Time available daily.
- Specific aversions (e.g., "I hate the word 'blessed,'" "I don't want to thank a higher power").

## Constraints

### Must

- Output a calibrated daily practice with: 1 specific gratitude + the honest texture around it; 1 noticing of something small; 1 acknowledgment of something hard.
- Specificity rule: not "my family" — "the way my daughter hummed while drawing this morning."
- Texture rule: each gratitude is allowed an honest "and": "and I'm exhausted" / "and I can also see how hard it is."
- No forced positivity. If today doesn't have a gratitude that lands honestly, the prompt accommodates.
- Adapt to the client's language preferences (no "blessed" / no higher-power framing if not wanted).

### Must Not

- Don't push gratitude in active distress.
- Don't shame "complaining" or absence of gratitude.
- Don't require positive reframing of hard things.
- Don't make this a religious practice if the client doesn't want that.

## Instructions

1. Capture preferences and aversions.
2. Set the daily structure (1 specific gratitude with texture + 1 small noticing + 1 acknowledgment of what's hard).
3. Set time and trigger.
4. Add a "no-gratitude day" backup so the practice doesn't collapse on hard days.

## Output Format

```
=== GRATITUDE JOURNAL — CALIBRATED ===

My preferences:
- Time of day: [...]
- Length: [3 lines / 5 lines / 1 paragraph]
- Language I want to use / avoid: [...]
- Spiritual / secular framing: [...]

Daily Format (3 parts):

1. ONE SPECIFIC GRATITUDE — with texture
   Specificity: not "my partner" — "the way [partner] noticed I was tired and made coffee without asking."
   Texture (the honest "and"): "and I'm exhausted lately and can't always meet that the way I want to."
   
   Example today: 
   "I'm grateful for [specific moment]. And [honest texture]."

2. ONE SMALL NOTICING
   Something I caught today that I might have missed. Doesn't have to be good or bad — just noticed.
   "I noticed [...]"

3. ONE ACKNOWLEDGMENT OF SOMETHING HARD
   Naming what's hard right now without trying to flip it.
   "What's hard right now is [...]"

The three together hold: appreciation, attention, honesty.

NO-GRATITUDE-DAY BACKUP:
On days when no gratitude lands honestly:
- Skip the gratitude.
- Do the small noticing only.
- Acknowledge what's hard.
- That counts as the practice today. The honesty is the practice; gratitude is one form of it.

WEEKLY REVIEW (5 min, end of week):
- Patterns in what landed.
- Patterns in the honest "ands."
- Anything I want to bring to therapy.

What this practice IS:
- Building noticing skill.
- Resisting negativity bias without falsifying.
- Cultivating both/and capacity.

What this practice is NOT:
- A reframe machine.
- A reason to skip working on what's actually hard.
- A test of whether I'm sufficiently grateful.
- A substitute for therapy.
```

## Verification

- [ ] Specificity rule applied.
- [ ] Honest "and" allowed on every gratitude.
- [ ] Hard-acknowledgment included.
- [ ] No-gratitude-day backup present.
- [ ] Language preferences honored.
- [ ] No forced reframing.
- [ ] No shaming about hard days.
- [ ] Distinction from reframe machine explicit.
