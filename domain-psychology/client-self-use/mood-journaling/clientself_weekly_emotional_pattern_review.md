# Weekly Emotional Pattern Review

## Objective

A structured 15-minute weekly review (separate from a productivity weekly review) that surfaces emotional pattern: highs, lows, recurring themes, what helped, what didn't, what to carry forward.

## When to Use

- End of week, beginning of week, or any consistent anchor.
- During treatment to compound between-session learning.
- When weeks blur together and nothing seems to land.

## Inputs / Context

- Mood / energy data if tracked.
- Calendar of the week (events, meetings, social, alone-time).
- One-line journal entries from the week if any.
- What was assigned in therapy this week.

## Constraints

### Must

- Output sections: **The Week in One Line**, **Three Highs**, **Three Lows**, **Recurring Theme**, **What Helped**, **What Didn't Help**, **One Thing I'm Carrying Forward**, **One Thing I'm Letting Go**.
- Each section is 1–3 sentences max; the review is bounded.
- "What helped / didn't help" is observation, not should-statements.
- "Carrying forward" is one thing, not a list.

### Must Not

- Don't turn this into goal-setting; that's a different review.
- Don't critique the week ("you should have...").
- Don't push insight where none arrived.

## Instructions

1. Set 15-min timer.
2. Write the week in one line.
3. Three highs, three lows.
4. Notice recurring theme (sometimes there isn't one; that's allowed).
5. Helped / didn't help (observational).
6. Carry forward / let go.

## Output Format

```
=== WEEKLY EMOTIONAL PATTERN REVIEW ===

Week of: [...]
Time: 15 min

The Week in One Line:
"[A single sentence capturing the week's emotional arc.]"

Three Highs:
1. [Specific moment — what made it a high]
2. [...]
3. [...]

Three Lows:
1. [Specific moment — what made it a low]
2. [...]
3. [...]

Recurring Theme:
- [Theme that showed up across multiple days, OR "no clear theme this week"]

What Helped:
- [Behavior, person, place, practice — observed, not prescribed]

What Didn't Help:
- [Behavior, person, place, practice — observed, not blamed]

One Thing I'm Carrying Forward:
- [Single thing — a learning, a practice, a question, a decision]

One Thing I'm Letting Go:
- [Single thing — a story, a should, a tension I want to set down]

If anything from this week needs more space than this:
- Bring it to therapy.
- Or give it its own 30-min reflection.
```

## Verification

- [ ] All 8 sections present.
- [ ] Each section bounded.
- [ ] Highs and lows are specific moments.
- [ ] Theme can be "none" — not invented.
- [ ] Helped / didn't help observational.
- [ ] Single carry-forward, single letting-go.
- [ ] No should-statements.
