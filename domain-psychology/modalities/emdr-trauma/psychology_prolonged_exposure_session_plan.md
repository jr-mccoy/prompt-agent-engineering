# Prolonged Exposure (PE) for PTSD — Session Plan

## Objective

Generate a single 90-minute Prolonged Exposure (PE; Foa, Hembree, Rothbaum) session plan placed within the 8–15 session protocol. The plan includes: homework review, imaginal exposure to the trauma memory (with hot-spot work in later sessions), processing of imaginal material, in-vivo hierarchy step assignment, and between-session homework (listen to imaginal recording, in-vivo exposures, monitoring).

## When to Use

- DSM-5-TR PTSD confirmed, ideally with PCL-5 / CAPS-5 baseline.
- Single-incident or chronic trauma, with PE adherent protocol.
- Adults; adolescent PE has an adapted protocol.
- Telehealth: feasible with adaptations (recording handled within the platform; in-vivo done in client's environment).
- Mid-protocol (sessions 3–10) where imaginal has been introduced.
- Not appropriate for clients with active psychosis, untreated severe substance use, imminent suicide risk, or severe dissociation requiring stabilization-first.

## Inputs / Context

- Baseline PCL-5 / CAPS-5 / PHQ-9 / GAD-7 and trajectory.
- Index trauma memory and any other memories targeted later in protocol.
- Trauma cognition map (negative cognitions, avoidance themes, "stuck points" in CPT lineage if blended).
- In-vivo hierarchy: SUDS-ranked situations the client avoids.
- Homework status from prior week (imaginal listening, in-vivos, monitoring).
- Substance use status (PE protocol generally requires no AOD use before or during exposure homework).
- Risk plan and safety plan.
- Modality and recording method.
- Cultural and developmental considerations.

## Constraints

### Must

- Confirm PCL-5 / SUDS data at session start.
- Conduct **imaginal exposure** for 30–45 minutes (sessions 3–6 typically full memory; sessions 7+ hot-spot focus).
- Capture **SUDS every 5 minutes** during imaginal and at peak / end.
- Use the trauma memory in **first person, present tense, eyes closed**, narrated by the client.
- **Hot-spot work** (mid-protocol): identify the moments of peak SUDS within the memory; repeat those segments multiple times.
- **Processing** after imaginal (15–20 min): what did the client notice; emotions; meaning-making (without arguing the cognition).
- **In-vivo** hierarchy step assigned for between-session.
- **Recording**: imaginal is recorded; client listens daily between sessions.
- **Homework**: imaginal recording listened to daily; in-vivo exposures specified; monitoring forms.
- **No safety behaviors** during imaginal: no eyes-open, no avoiding hot spots, no substances, no distraction.
- **Risk re-screen** at start of each session (PCL-5 cluster screen; suicidality; substance use).
- For dissociation during imaginal: ground first, then resume; if persistent, pause PE and reformulate.

### Must Not

- Do not abort imaginal because SUDS is high; high SUDS is expected. Abort only on dissociation / safety.
- Do not allow eyes-open / distraction / substance use during imaginal.
- Do not skip the recording.
- Do not skip processing time; uncoded high-arousal exposure is not the goal.
- Do not perform imaginal without an established safety plan and risk monitoring cadence.
- Do not engage CBT-style cognitive disputation during processing; PE processing is reflective, not argumentative (CPT is the lineage for cognitive disputation).
- Do not skip in-vivo; PE works through both imaginal and in-vivo.

## Instructions

1. PCL-5 / SUDS check-in; homework review (imaginal listening, in-vivos, monitoring).
2. Brief psychoeducation reminder if needed (rationale: emotional processing through approach not avoidance).
3. Imaginal: client sits, eyes closed, recounts memory first-person present-tense; recorded. SUDS every 5 min.
4. After 30–45 min: end imaginal; gently bring client out.
5. Process: what came up; emotions; what surprised; meaning-making (no disputation).
6. Assign in-vivo step for the week (SUDS-ranked).
7. Homework: daily imaginal listening + in-vivos + monitoring.
8. Schedule next session; safety plan re-check; risk-stratification update.
9. Document: imaginal length, SUDS trajectory, in-vivo step, homework, risk.

## Verification

- [ ] PCL-5 trajectory tracked.
- [ ] Imaginal in first-person, present tense, eyes closed, recorded.
- [ ] SUDS captured every 5 minutes plus peak and end.
- [ ] Hot-spot work begun in mid-protocol sessions.
- [ ] No safety behaviors / distraction allowed.
- [ ] Processing time present; no cognitive disputation.
- [ ] In-vivo assigned with SUDS-ranked step.
- [ ] Imaginal recording assigned for daily listening.
- [ ] Risk and AOD re-screen at session start.
- [ ] No imaginal performed without active safety plan.
- [ ] Reformulation flag if no movement after several sessions.
- [ ] No fabricated SUDS or client content.
