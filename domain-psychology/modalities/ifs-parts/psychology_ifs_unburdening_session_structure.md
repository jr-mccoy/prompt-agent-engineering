# IFS Unburdening Session Structure (for an Exile)

## Objective

Structure a phased IFS unburdening session: gain protector permission, find the exile, hear its story / witness, retrieve it from the past (if needed), unburden the extreme belief or feeling it carries, invite needed qualities, and integrate. The session ends with protector check-in, system update, and integration planning.

## When to Use

- Mid-IFS protocol after parts mapping and reliable Self-access.
- A specific exile has been identified, and protectors guarding it have been engaged.
- Protectors have given permission for the work.
- Client has stabilization adequate to traverse the work.
- Telehealth or in-office.
- Not appropriate when: protectors have not given permission; risk is acute; dissociation is uncontained; or IFS-for-dissociation training is needed and unavailable.

## Inputs / Context

- Target exile identified (from parts mapping).
- Protectors guarding it; their fears and permissions.
- Self-access status across the 8 Cs.
- Origin marker(s) for the burden (events or developmental periods).
- Resources installed (calm place / safe figures / body resources).
- Risk plan and dissociation plan.
- Modality, session length (typically 75–90 min for unburdening).
- Between-session support and check-in plan.

## Constraints

### Must

- Confirm protector permission before approaching the exile. If protectors are reluctant, slow down and dialogue with them; do not push.
- Maintain Self-energy throughout; if Self blends out (e.g., the client becomes the part), unblend before continuing.
- Phases in order:
  1. **Find** — locate the exile (body, image, age).
  2. **Focus** — Self attends to the exile.
  3. **Flesh out** — get to know the exile.
  4. **Feel toward** — check the client's feelings toward the exile; if not 8-C, unblend the part interfering.
  5. **Befriend** — connect, listen.
  6. **Fear** — what does the exile fear / want Self to know?
  7. **Witness** — Self witnesses the exile's experience.
  8. **Retrieval** — if exile is stuck in the past, retrieve to a present-day safe internal place.
  9. **Unburdening** — release the burden (extreme belief / feeling) to a chosen element (fire, water, wind, earth, light, or client's chosen process).
  10. **Invitation** — invite needed qualities (love, courage, lightness, joy) to fill the space.
  11. **Integration** — exile finds its new role; protectors update; system check-in.
- Confirm the exile's age, body location, and image so the work is concrete, not abstract.
- Use **client-directed unburdening element**; do not impose.
- For protectors: at the end, ask "Now that this exile is unburdened, what would you like to do? You don't have to keep this old job."
- Plan **between-session integration**: 1–2 minutes daily checking in with the unburdened part and the updated protector roles.
- Schedule a follow-up check-in or session.
- If at any phase the work cannot proceed safely, **end the work, restore Self-energy, resource, and integrate**; do not push.

### Must Not

- Do not bypass protector permission.
- Do not coach the client to "let go" before they are willing.
- Do not impose an unburdening element (clinician's preference).
- Do not push retrieval if the exile or protector resists.
- Do not allow the session to end with the client blended into the exile.
- Do not deliver this protocol without IFS training (Level 1 or equivalent supervision).
- Do not run unburdening in shortened sessions; the integration phase is critical.
- Do not promise the burden won't return; reconsolidation is iterative.

## Instructions

1. Pre-flight: confirm protector permission, Self-access, resources, time.
2. Walk through the 11 phases above with the client.
3. At each phase, check Self-energy; if blended, unblend before continuing.
4. After unburdening, **invite qualities**; pause for sensation.
5. Update protectors; ask their new role.
6. Integration: image, body sensation, words from the system.
7. Plan between-session integration practice.
8. Document; schedule check-in.

## Verification

- [ ] Protector permission confirmed.
- [ ] Self-access checked at each phase; unblending applied when needed.
- [ ] All 11 phases conducted or paused with reason.
- [ ] Exile witnessed before unburdening attempted.
- [ ] Burden named verbatim.
- [ ] Unburdening element is client-chosen.
- [ ] Qualities invited after release.
- [ ] Protectors updated and new roles named.
- [ ] Between-session integration assigned.
- [ ] Risk / dissociation status documented at end.
- [ ] Clinician training in IFS adequate for this work.
- [ ] No bypass of consent, no pushing through resistance.
