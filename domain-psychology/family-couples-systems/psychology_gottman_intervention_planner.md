# Gottman Method Intervention Planner

## Objective

Produce a sequenced Gottman Method Couples Therapy (GMCT) intervention plan that derives directly from a Gottman assessment. The plan locates the couple on the Sound Relationship House, identifies which of the Four Horsemen are present, names the friendship-system deficits (Love Maps, Fondness & Admiration, Turning Toward) and conflict-management failures (flooding/Diffuse Physiological Arousal, harsh start-up, failed repair, gridlock on perpetual problems), and then sequences interventions in the Gottman order: assess and de-escalate destructive conflict, rebuild friendship, then build shared meaning. Output is an intervention map keyed to specific Gottman interventions, not a generic communication plan.

## When to Use

- After a Gottman-style assessment (Sound Relationship House questionnaires, Oral History Interview, conflict observation) is complete.
- When a couple presents with high negativity, contempt, or stonewalling and the clinician needs a structured antidote sequence.
- When distinguishing perpetual problems (to be dialogued) from solvable problems (to be problem-solved) is the planning bottleneck.
- For supervision/consultation requiring the plan to be tied to specific Gottman constructs.

## Inputs / Context Required

- **Sound Relationship House status**: which levels are weak (Love Maps; Fondness & Admiration; Turn Toward Instead of Away; The Positive Perspective; Manage Conflict; Make Life Dreams Come True; Create Shared Meaning; plus the weight-bearing walls Trust and Commitment).
- **Four Horsemen present**: criticism, contempt, defensiveness, stonewalling — with examples.
- **Bids and turning ratio**: estimate of turning-toward vs away/against.
- **Flooding / DPA**: does either partner flood (heart rate proxy, "can't think straight," shut down)?
- **Conflict typology per issue**: which recurring issues are perpetual (rooted in personality/values, ~69% of conflict) vs solvable.
- **Gridlock markers**: dreams-within-conflict not yet surfaced.
- **Repair attempts**: are they made? received?
- **Safety screen**: IPV / situational vs characterological violence screen. `[clinician input required: result of separate IPV screen; characterological/coercive violence is a contraindication to conjoint conflict work]`
- `[clinician input required: affairs/betrayals or active addiction requiring Atone–Attune–Attach or separate track]`

## Constraints

### Must

- Tie every intervention to a specific Sound Relationship House level or Gottman construct.
- Prescribe the antidote for each Horseman present (see table).
- Sequence in Gottman order: (1) down-regulate destructive conflict / teach self-soothing for flooding; (2) rebuild the friendship system (Love Maps → Fondness & Admiration → Turning Toward / Positive Perspective); (3) manage conflict (softened start-up, accept influence, repair, compromise, dreams-within-conflict for gridlock); (4) build shared meaning.
- Distinguish perpetual from solvable problems and route each correctly: perpetual problems get the Dreams-Within-Conflict / Gottman-Rapoport dialogue (understanding before problem-solving), not a solution.
- Include a physiological self-soothing protocol when flooding/DPA is present (the 20-minute break with genuine self-soothing, not rumination).
- Include the Aftermath of a Fight/Regrettable Incident processing tool for past ruptures.
- Screen for and route characterological/coercive IPV out of conjoint conflict work; include the safety re-assessment hook.
- Use `[clinician input required: ...]` for clinician-supplied content.

### Must Not

- Do not attempt to "solve" perpetual problems; gridlock is worked through dialogue and surfacing dreams, not resolution.
- Do not begin conflict-management interventions while contempt is unaddressed or while a partner is chronically flooded — de-escalate and build friendship first.
- Do not proceed with conjoint conflict work where characterological/coercive violence is present.
- Do not skip the antidote for contempt (building fondness & admiration); contempt is the single strongest predictor of dissolution and must be prioritized.
- Do not fabricate assessment data; flag missing levels.

## Instructions

1. **Score the Sound Relationship House.** For each level and the two walls, mark strong / mixed / weak, with one supporting observation each.

2. **Identify the Four Horsemen and prescribe antidotes.**

   | Horseman | Description | Antidote |
   |----------|-------------|----------|
   | Criticism | Attacking character ("you always…") | Gentle/softened start-up — "I feel ___ about ___, I need ___" |
   | Contempt | Disrespect, sarcasm, mockery, eye-rolling | Build a culture of fondness & admiration; describe own needs |
   | Defensiveness | Counter-attack / victim stance | Accept responsibility for even a part of the problem |
   | Stonewalling | Shutting down, withdrawing (often flooded) | Physiological self-soothing; take a 20-min break, then return |

3. **Assess flooding / DPA.** If either partner floods, install the self-soothing/break protocol before conflict skill-building: signal a break, genuinely self-soothe for ≥20 minutes (no rehearsing resentment), then return to the conversation.

4. **Sort recurring issues into perpetual vs solvable.**
   - Perpetual (~69%): rooted in enduring differences. Route to Dreams-Within-Conflict and the Gottman-Rapoport intervention (each partner first understands and can summarize the other's position and the dream beneath it; no persuasion until both feel understood).
   - Solvable: route to softened start-up → accept influence → compromise (the two-circle / core-needs vs flexible-areas exercise).

5. **Sequence friendship-system rebuilding.**
   - Love Maps: build knowledge of partner's inner world (Love Map card-deck style questions).
   - Fondness & Admiration: the antidote to contempt; "I appreciate" exercises, recalling positive history.
   - Turning Toward: notice and respond to bids; the Emotional Bank Account; stress-reducing conversation (turning toward outside stress).
   - Positive Perspective follows naturally once friendship and de-escalation are in place (it is an outcome, not a direct intervention).

6. **Process past ruptures.** Use the Aftermath of a Fight / Regrettable Incident tool: each shares subjective reality and feelings, validates the other, identifies triggers and what each can do differently — without re-litigating who was right.

7. **Build shared meaning (later stage).** Rituals of connection, supporting each other's roles, shared goals and symbols.

8. **Re-screen safety** and specify routing if characterological violence or an undisclosed affair surfaces.

9. **Run verification.**

## Verification

- [ ] Sound Relationship House scored level-by-level with the two walls.
- [ ] Each Horseman present is paired with its correct antidote; contempt prioritized.
- [ ] Flooding/DPA assessed; self-soothing/break protocol installed where present.
- [ ] Issues sorted into perpetual (dialogue) vs solvable (problem-solve) and routed correctly.
- [ ] Perpetual problems routed to Dreams-Within-Conflict / Gottman-Rapoport, not "solved."
- [ ] Interventions sequenced: de-escalate → friendship → conflict management → shared meaning.
- [ ] Aftermath-of-a-Fight tool assigned for a past rupture.
- [ ] IPV screen documented; characterological violence routed out of conjoint conflict work.
- [ ] Safety re-assessment hook present.
- [ ] Missing assessment data flagged with `[clinician input required]`.
