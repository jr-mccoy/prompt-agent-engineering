# OCD Exposure & Response Prevention (ERP) — Provider Protocol

## Objective
Generate a single 50–60 minute Exposure and Response Prevention (EX/RP) session plan within a manualized OCD protocol (Foa & Kozak's emotional-processing model and the Foa, Yadin & Lichner therapist guide; Abramowitz's evidence-based treatment), updated with Craske's inhibitory-learning framework. The plan locates the session in a typical 12–20 session course (often intensive/twice-weekly early on), builds from a Yale-Brown Obsessive Compulsive Scale (Y-BOCS) baseline and a SUDS-anchored exposure hierarchy, and specifies in-vivo and/or imaginal exposures paired with **full response prevention** of overt and covert rituals. It targets the maintaining cycle (obsession → distress → compulsion → negative reinforcement) and is built to violate the patient's catastrophic expectancy and generalize learning across contexts rather than to "habituate" distress away. It accommodates the major presentations — contamination, harm/checking, symmetry/ordering, taboo intrusions ("Pure-O"), and scrupulosity — and includes reassurance-seeking and family-accommodation reduction.

## When to Use
- DSM-5-TR Obsessive-Compulsive Disorder confirmed, with an obsession–compulsion functional link mapped.
- Mid-protocol session designing graded in-vivo or imaginal exposure with response prevention.
- Presentations dominated by covert/mental compulsions, reassurance-seeking, or scrupulosity where rituals are easily missed.
- Reducing family accommodation that maintains the cycle.
- Not appropriate as a freestanding session when the patient has had no ERP psychoeducation/rationale, or when active suicidal intent (not ego-dystonic harm intrusions) requires a risk session first.

## Inputs / Context
- Current Y-BOCS severity and symptom checklist categories; primary subtype(s).
- The functional map: obsession, feared outcome, distress level, and the compulsion/neutralizing it triggers (overt and covert).
- SUDS-anchored hierarchy (items rated 0–100) and where the patient is on it.
- Covert compulsions present: mental reviewing, neutralizing phrases, counting, praying-to-undo, mental checking, reassurance-seeking.
- Family/partner accommodation behaviors (answering reassurance, doing checks, modifying routines).
- `[clinician input required: distinction between ego-dystonic harm/taboo intrusions and any genuine intent or risk — and the risk-screen result]`
- `[clinician input required: medical/safety carve-outs for any exposure that involves real contaminants, heights, or sharps]`
- `[clinician input required: the specific catastrophic expectancy each planned exposure is designed to test]`

## Constraints

### Must
- Target the **ritual**, not the obsession — and explicitly include covert/mental compulsions, neutralizing, and reassurance-seeking in the response-prevention plan.
- Build each exposure to **violate a stated expectancy** (inhibitory learning): name the patient's prediction, the maximally informative test, and the disconfirmation.
- Implement **full response prevention** (eliminate, not merely reduce, the compulsion); if shaping is needed, document the explicit step-down to full prevention.
- Vary exposures across contexts, stimuli, and cues to promote generalization; build in occasional "deepened" or combined exposures rather than repeating one safe rep.
- For scrupulosity, frame exposures around tolerating uncertainty about moral/religious "rightness," not around resolving the religious question.
- Address family accommodation with a concrete reduction step and a script that withholds reassurance compassionately.
- State the CPT code and a risk-reassessment hook; for taboo/harm or scrupulosity content include a supervisor co-sign line.

### Must Not
- Must not provide reassurance, certainty, or "proof" that the feared outcome won't occur (that is the patient's compulsion).
- Must not treat ego-dystonic harm or taboo intrusions as evidence of intent — distinguish unwanted intrusions from risk; do not escalate them as danger.
- Must not allow distraction, suppression, mini-rituals, or mental neutralizing during exposure.
- Must not reduce exposure intensity because SUDS is "too high"; reduce only for genuine medical/safety reasons or documented shaping.
- Must not fabricate Y-BOCS scores, SUDS ratings, or symptom content — these come from the patient and the instrument.

## Instructions
1. Confirm Y-BOCS baseline, subtype, and the functional map; run/confirm the risk screen and explicitly distinguish ego-dystonic intrusions from intent.
2. Review homework: completed exposures, response-prevention adherence, any rituals or reassurance that slipped in.
3. Select the hierarchy item(s) for this session and state the patient's catastrophic expectancy verbatim.
4. Design the exposure: in-vivo and/or imaginal script; name duration, repetitions, and the contexts to vary across.
5. Specify full response prevention for every overt and covert ritual tied to this exposure.
6. Conduct the inhibitory-learning debrief: did the prediction hold? what was learned about tolerating uncertainty?
7. Plan family-accommodation reduction with a specific step and a no-reassurance script.
8. Assign homework (named exposures, durations, reps, response-prevention rules) and a monitoring log.
9. Complete the outcome/risk block, billing, and co-sign line for taboo/scrupulosity content.

--- HOMEWORK REVIEW ---
Exposures completed: [list]    Response prevention adherence: [overt + covert]

--- TODAY'S TARGET ---

## Verification
- [ ] Both overt and covert/mental compulsions are named and blocked in the response-prevention plan.
- [ ] Each exposure states a catastrophic expectancy and how it will be violated (inhibitory learning), not just "habituation."
- [ ] Full response prevention is specified; any shaping has an explicit step-down.
- [ ] Exposures vary across contexts to promote generalization.
- [ ] No reassurance, certainty, or proof is provided to the patient.
- [ ] Ego-dystonic harm/taboo intrusions are distinguished from intent; risk screen documented.
- [ ] Family-accommodation reduction step and no-reassurance script included.
- [ ] CPT, risk hook, and co-sign line for taboo/scrupulosity present.
- [ ] No fabricated Y-BOCS scores, SUDS ratings, or symptom content — all come from the patient and the instrument.
