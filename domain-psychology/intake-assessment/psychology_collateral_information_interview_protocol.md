# Collateral Information Interview Protocol

## Objective

Guide and document a structured collateral informant interview at psychiatric or behavioral health intake that:

1. Verifies ROI scope before any clinical information is shared or elicited.
2. Gathers collateral data across clinically relevant domains not fully accessible through client self-report (symptom history, functional changes, safety concerns, longitudinal course).
3. Manages the inherent tension between gathering useful clinical information and maintaining the primary client's privacy and alliance.
4. Produces a structured Collateral Contact Note suitable for the intake record and cross-referenced in the biopsychosocial assessment.

## When to Use

- At intake when a family member, partner, caregiver, or other informant is available and the client has consented via ROI.
- When the client's self-report may be limited by cognitive impairment, psychosis, anosognosia, or acute distress.
- When developmental or childhood history requires a parental informant for adult-onset neurodevelopmental assessment (ADHD, autism).
- When safety-relevant history (prior suicidal behavior, violence, substance use) benefits from corroboration.
- For pediatric or adolescent intakes where caregiver collateral is standard of care (see also: `psychology_pediatric_intake_with_caregiver.md`).

## Inputs / Context Required

- **ROI documentation:** Client-signed ROI form: informant name, relationship, specific domains authorized for disclosure and/or receipt of information, expiration date, any exclusions.
- **Informant identity:** Name, relationship to client, how long they have known the client in this capacity, frequency of contact, living arrangement (same household or not).
- **Reason for collateral contact:** What specific questions does the clinician need the collateral to address that the client's report cannot fully answer?
- **Client's consent framing:** What did the client tell the informant about the purpose of the contact? What does the client want or not want shared with the informant?
- **Clinician's prior knowledge from client interview:** What the clinician already knows, to avoid confirmatory bias (note where collateral data corroborates vs. diverges from client report).

## Constraints

### Must

- Verify ROI validity (signed, dated, not expired, informant matches the named person on the ROI) at the start of the contact — do not proceed without confirmed ROI.
- Adhere strictly to the authorized scope; if the informant asks questions or volunteers information outside the authorized domains, use the protocol redirect script.
- Document specifically which domains the ROI authorizes for: (a) receiving information from the informant, (b) sharing information with the informant. These are two distinct authorizations; do not conflate.
- Note wherever collateral data corroborates vs. diverges from client self-report; do not silently defer to one source.
- Inform the informant at the outset that you cannot confirm whether the client is or is not in treatment (unless that disclosure is authorized), and that general statements you share are limited to what the ROI permits.
- Maintain the primary therapeutic alliance: collateral contact should not compromise the client's sense of safety or privacy beyond what they explicitly authorized.
- Document the collateral contact in a separate Collateral Contact Note referencing the intake; do not incorporate collateral data into the intake note without flagging it as collateral-sourced.

### Must Not

- Do not share clinical information outside the authorized scope under any circumstances, including when an informant is distressed or insistent.
- Do not use the collateral contact as a proxy therapy session for the informant — offer a referral if indicated, but do not engage in clinical work beyond the authorized scope.
- Do not present collateral data as factual without qualifying it as informant-reported; collateral is one data source, not ground truth.
- Do not share the client's specific diagnoses, medications, or session content unless the ROI explicitly authorizes this.
- Do not schedule a collateral contact without confirmed client ROI.
- Do not fabricate; flag gaps.

## Instructions

1. **Pre-contact ROI review.** Confirm ROI is signed, dated, valid, and identifies this informant. Extract the authorized domains: what you may receive from the informant, what you may share with the informant. Note any exclusions.

2. **Open the contact with identity confirmation and scope-setting.** Confirm the informant's identity matches the ROI. State the purpose: "I'm calling as part of [client's first name]'s initial evaluation, with their permission. I'd like to ask you a few questions about what you've observed — specifically [authorized domains]. I'll also be limited in what I can share with you today."

3. **Conduct the domain-by-domain inquiry.** For each authorized domain, use open-ended questions followed by behavioral anchors. Do not lead with clinical hypotheses.

4. **Apply the redirect script for out-of-scope inquiries.** When the informant asks about something outside the ROI scope, use: "I appreciate you sharing that. I'm limited today to discussing [authorized domains]. What I can tell you is [authorized disclosure, if any]. If you have other concerns, I'd encourage [appropriate resource]."

5. **Document all data as collateral-sourced.** In the note, prefix with "Per [relationship] (collateral informant, ROI on file, [date]):..."

6. **Identify corroboration and divergence points.** Where collateral data align with or contradict client self-report, flag explicitly.

7. **Write the Collateral Contact Note** in the format below.

8. **Run verification.**

## Output Format

```
=== COLLATERAL CONTACT NOTE ===

Client: [Initials/MRN]    Date of Service: [YYYY-MM-DD]    Time: [HH:MM–HH:MM]
Clinician: [Name, credentials]

Contact method: [In-person / Telephone / Video]    Duration: [N minutes]

─────────────────────────────────────────
ROI VERIFICATION
─────────────────────────────────────────
ROI on file: [Yes / No — do not proceed without ROI]
ROI signed by client: [Yes]    Date signed: [YYYY-MM-DD]    Expiration: [YYYY-MM-DD or "1 year"]
Informant identity confirmed: [Yes — matches ROI]
Authorized domains — RECEIVE information from informant: [List: e.g., symptom history, functioning, safety]
Authorized domains — SHARE information with informant: [List: e.g., appointment confirmation, safety planning;
OR "No disclosures authorized beyond acknowledgment of treatment status"]
Exclusions noted on ROI: [e.g., "Client requested no disclosure of SUD history"]

─────────────────────────────────────────
INFORMANT BACKGROUND
─────────────────────────────────────────
Relationship duration: [X years]
Frequency of contact with client: [Daily / Weekly / Other]
Living arrangement: [Same household / Separate]
Informant's stated reason for participating: [clinician input required]

─────────────────────────────────────────
COLLATERAL DATA BY DOMAIN
─────────────────────────────────────────
[Only document domains authorized for receipt of information:]

SYMPTOM PRESENTATION (if authorized):
  Per informant: [What the informant observes regarding the client's symptoms — mood, behavior,
  functioning changes; use informant's language]
  Onset and timeline per informant: [When changes were first noticed]
  Behavioral anchors observed: [Specific behaviors, e.g., "stopped attending work 3 months ago,"
  "crying daily for past 6 weeks," "not eating — lost approximately 10 lbs by informant estimate"]

SAFETY-RELEVANT OBSERVATIONS (if authorized):
  Prior suicidal behavior witnessed or reported to informant: [Yes — describe / No]
  Current safety concerns expressed by informant: [Yes — describe / No]
  Access to means in shared household: [Firearms / Medications — informant report]
  Recent behavior changes raising concern: [...]

PSYCHIATRIC AND TREATMENT HISTORY CORROBORATION (if authorized):
  Prior diagnoses informant is aware of: [...]
  Prior hospitalizations informant recalls: [Dates, reasons — may add to client-reported history]
  Medication adherence observed: [Consistent / Inconsistent — informant perspective]

FUNCTIONAL HISTORY (if authorized):
  Occupational / school functioning changes: [...]
  Social withdrawal or relationship changes: [...]
  Self-care and daily functioning: [...]
  Substance use observations: [None / Describe — informant-reported only]

DEVELOPMENTAL HISTORY (if authorized — primarily for adult neurodevelopmental assessments):
  ADHD symptoms in childhood (inattention, hyperactivity, impulsivity): [...]
  ASD indicators in childhood: [...]
  Academic history as recalled by informant: [...]
  Milestone history: [...]

FAMILY HISTORY (if authorized):
  Informant's knowledge of family psychiatric history: [...]

─────────────────────────────────────────
OUT-OF-SCOPE REQUESTS DURING CONTACT
─────────────────────────────────────────
[Document any requests the informant made that fell outside the authorized ROI scope,
the redirect script used, and disposition:]
  [Informant asked: "..."]    Redirect used: [Script applied]    Outcome: [Informant accepted redirect / continued pressing — escalation response: ...]

─────────────────────────────────────────
CORROBORATION AND DIVERGENCE SUMMARY
─────────────────────────────────────────
Data corroborating client self-report:
  [List specific clinical data points where informant and client reports align]

Data diverging from client self-report:
  [List specific points of disagreement — e.g., "Client reported no suicidal ideation;
  informant reports witnessing client researching methods online 2 weeks prior."]
  Clinical weight given to divergence: [Note which source will be weighted more heavily for
  which clinical decision, and rationale]

─────────────────────────────────────────
DISCLOSURES MADE TO INFORMANT
─────────────────────────────────────────
[Document exactly what was disclosed to the informant within authorized scope:]
  [e.g., "Confirmed client is engaged in treatment as of today's date." (Authorized on ROI)]
  [e.g., "Shared safety plan elements per authorized safety disclosure." ]
  [e.g., "No clinical content disclosed beyond confirmation of treatment — per ROI scope."]

─────────────────────────────────────────
CLINICAL INTEGRATION NOTE
─────────────────────────────────────────
[Brief paragraph: how collateral data will be integrated into the intake formulation.
Note any significant additions to or modifications of the clinical picture derived from
this contact. Identify any follow-up collateral contact planned.]

─────────────────────────────────────────
BILLING NOTE
─────────────────────────────────────────
Collateral contact conducted in conjunction with CPT [90791 | 90792] intake evaluation.
Duration: [N minutes]. If separate collateral contact note is billed: CPT 99212/99213 with

with billing team before billing separately from the intake CPT.
```

## Verification

- [ ] ROI verification section confirms signed, dated, not expired, and informant identity matched.
- [ ] Authorized domains — receive and share — documented separately; they are not the same.
- [ ] All collateral data explicitly labeled as "Per [relationship] (collateral informant, ROI on file)."
- [ ] Safety-relevant observations documented (present or absent) if within authorized scope.
- [ ] Out-of-scope requests and redirect responses documented if any occurred.
- [ ] Corroboration and divergence summary present; divergences noted with clinical weighting.
- [ ] Disclosures made to informant documented with specificity.
- [ ] No diagnoses, medications, or session content disclosed beyond ROI authorization.
- [ ] Clinical integration note states how collateral data modifies or confirms the intake formulation.
- [ ] Gaps flagged with `[clinician input required: ...]`.
