# Group Therapy Member Screening (Fit and Suitability)

## Objective

Produce a pre-group screening record that yields a defensible placement decision (Include / Conditional / Exclude / Redirect) for a prospective group member by:

1. Distinguishing the **group type** (interpersonal-process vs. psychoeducational/skills) and screening against the criteria appropriate to that type.
2. Applying **Yalom-informed inclusion and exclusion criteria** and identifying contraindications (acute crisis, active psychosis, certain personality features that would derail the group).
3. Matching the member's needs to the group's **therapeutic factors** and the existing group composition.
4. Conducting a **risk screen** (SI/HI/self-harm, acute instability) that must be current.
5. Reviewing and documenting **group-specific informed consent** (confidentiality of co-members, attendance/commitment expectations, limits).
6. Producing a placement recommendation suitable for a group seen under CPT 90853 (group psychotherapy) or 90849 (multiple-family group).

## When to Use

- Before admitting any new member to an existing or forming therapy group.
- When deciding whether a referred client is better suited to a process group, a psychoeducational/skills group, or individual therapy first.
- When a member's acuity or interpersonal style raises a fit question that needs documented reasoning.
- When forming a new group and composing a roster for balance.

## When NOT to Use

- For individual intake (use `domain-psychology/documentation/psychology_intake_assessment_note.md`).
- For couples or family screening (use the couples or family intake prompts in this directory).
- For psychoeducational classes that are not clinical groups and carry no therapeutic-relationship expectations.

## Inputs / Context Required

- **Group description:** Type (process vs. psychoeducational/skills), focus/population, open vs. closed, size, stage of development, current composition.
- **Prospective member:** Presenting concerns, diagnoses, current individual treatment, motivation for group, prior group experience.
- **Interpersonal profile:** Capacity for self-disclosure, tolerance of feedback, interpersonal style (withdrawn, dominating, hostile, help-rejecting), reflective capacity.
- **Risk and stability:** Current SI/HI/self-harm, substance use, recent crises/hospitalizations, current symptom severity.
- **Practical factors:** Schedule fit, ability to commit to attendance, transportation, language.
- `[clinician input required: current risk status and any acute instability within the last 30 days that would defer group entry]`

## Constraints

### Must

- Identify the **group type** first and apply the matching suitability standard (process groups demand greater interpersonal tolerance and reflective capacity; psychoeducational/skills groups tolerate a wider range).
- Apply Yalom-informed **inclusion criteria** (motivation, ability to engage in the group task, problems amenable to interpersonal learning) and **exclusion criteria/contraindications**.
- Treat the following as contraindications/deferrals for a typical outpatient process group and document explicitly: acute suicidal/homicidal crisis, active psychosis, acute manic state, severe current substance intoxication/withdrawal, and interpersonal features (e.g., severe hostility, inability to tolerate any feedback, monopolizing that cannot be managed) that would predictably derail the group or harm the member.
- Conduct and document a **current risk screen** (SI/HI/self-harm); if positive at a level requiring stabilization, defer group entry and document the plan.
- Match the member to the group's **therapeutic factors** (e.g., universality, interpersonal learning, cohesion, instillation of hope) and assess composition balance.
- Review and document **group informed consent**: confidentiality of co-members (and that the clinician cannot guarantee it), attendance/commitment, fees, and limits.
- Produce a clear placement recommendation with rationale.
- Flag all `[clinician input required: ...]` gaps; do not fabricate history or screening results.

### Must Not

- Do not apply process-group exclusion criteria to a psychoeducational/skills group without noting the difference; many members excluded from process groups are appropriate for skills groups.
- Do not admit a member in an acute crisis to a group as a substitute for needed stabilization or a higher level of care.
- Do not treat diagnosis alone as inclusion/exclusion; screen the interpersonal and risk profile.
- Do not omit the group informed-consent review (especially the limits of co-member confidentiality).
- Do not fabricate the member's prior group history or current risk status.

## Group-Type Suitability Overlay

| Dimension | Interpersonal-Process Group | Psychoeducational / Skills Group |
|-----------|-----------------------------|----------------------------------|
| Core task | Here-and-now interpersonal learning | Acquire content/skills (e.g., DBT skills, psychoed) |
| Required capacity | Self-disclosure, feedback tolerance, reflective capacity | Ability to attend and participate; lower interpersonal demand |
| Typical exclusions | Severe hostility, inability to tolerate feedback, paranoia, acute crisis, monopolizing | Acute crisis, active psychosis precluding participation |
| Key therapeutic factors | Interpersonal learning, cohesion, catharsis, self-understanding | Imparting information, universality, instillation of hope |
| Composition concern | Balance to avoid a lone "deviant"; range of styles | Homogeneity of need often acceptable |

## Instructions

1. **Characterize the group.** Document type, focus, format (open/closed), size, stage, and current composition.

2. **Take the member profile.** Presenting concerns, diagnoses, current treatment, motivation, prior group experience, and goals.

3. **Assess interpersonal suitability** against the group type: capacity for disclosure, feedback tolerance, reflective capacity, and any style (withdrawn/dominating/hostile/help-rejecting) that bears on fit.

4. **Run the current risk screen** (SI/HI/self-harm, substance use, recent crises/hospitalizations). If positive at a stabilization-requiring level, defer and document the plan.

5. **Apply inclusion/exclusion criteria** and identify contraindications, calibrated to the group type.

6. **Match to therapeutic factors and composition.** Which factors will most help this member; does the roster stay balanced; would the member become an isolate or a destabilizer?

7. **Review group informed consent.** Confidentiality of co-members (and that it cannot be guaranteed), attendance/commitment, fees, between-session contact norms, limits.

8. **Render a placement recommendation** (Include / Conditional / Exclude / Redirect) with rationale and any pre-group conditions.

9. **Run verification.**

## Verification

- [ ] Group type identified and suitability criteria calibrated to process vs. psychoeducational/skills.
- [ ] Yalom-informed inclusion criteria and exclusion/contraindication screen completed.
- [ ] Contraindications (acute crisis, active psychosis, mania, intoxication/withdrawal, derailing interpersonal features) explicitly screened.
- [ ] Current risk screen (SI/HI/self-harm, recent instability) documented; stabilization plan if positive.
- [ ] Therapeutic-factor match and composition impact assessed.
- [ ] Group informed consent reviewed, including the limit on co-member confidentiality.
- [ ] Clear placement recommendation (Include/Conditional/Exclude/Redirect) with rationale and alternatives.
- [ ] Diagnosis not used alone as inclusion/exclusion; interpersonal and risk profile screened.
- [ ] Billing note distinguishes the screening encounter from group CPT codes (90853/90849).
- [ ] Gaps flagged with `[clinician input required: ...]`; no fabricated history or risk status.
