# Post-Attempt Re-Engagement Plan (After ED / Inpatient)

## Objective

Generate a structured re-engagement plan for the first 90 days after a suicide attempt and ED or inpatient stay — the highest-risk window for re-attempt. The plan must:

1. Establish **rapid reconnection** with outpatient care (within 24–48 h of discharge ideally).
2. Conduct a **stigma-aware** first session that does not re-traumatize, shame, or skip the event.
3. Support **narrative reconstruction**: what happened, what the client now sees, what the client wants now.
4. **Update the safety plan and lethal-means status** based on what actually worked / didn't during the crisis.
5. Loop in **family / partner / support** with consent.
6. Establish a **dense follow-up schedule** for the 90-day window.
7. Coordinate with prescriber, PCP, and the discharging facility.

## When to Use

- First outpatient contact after ED visit for SI / SA / NSSI requiring medical attention.
- First outpatient contact after inpatient discharge.
- Returning to a current clinician after a period of disengagement that included an attempt.
- Establishing care with a new clinician post-discharge.

## Inputs / Context

- Discharge summary from ED / inpatient with: dates, attempt method and lethality, treatments received, medications at discharge, working diagnoses, recommended outpatient plan.
- Client's current self-described state: physical, emotional, cognitive.
- Pre-attempt warning signs the client now recognizes in retrospect.
- What helped during the hospital stay and what didn't.
- Family / partner involvement: who knew, who knows now, who's helping.
- Means access status at discharge: firearm location, medication quantities, other.
- Current medications, side effects, adherence intent.
- Practical re-entry: time off work, school, return-to-driving, follow-up appointments scheduled, transportation.
- Insurance / financial considerations (psychiatric care continuity).
- Cultural / faith context relevant to the meaning-making work.

## Constraints

### Must

- Output the following labeled sections in order: **Re-Entry Metadata**, **Stigma-Aware Reconnection Frame**, **Narrative Reconstruction**, **What Helped / What Didn't**, **Updated Safety Plan**, **Means Restriction Follow-Through**, **Medication and Prescriber Coordination**, **Family / Partner Loop-In**, **Practical Re-Entry Plan**, **90-Day Follow-Up Schedule**, **Risk Re-Stratification**, **Coordination with Discharging Facility**, **Documentation**.
- Reconnection frame must avoid both extremes: (a) ignoring the attempt, (b) interrogating it before alliance is reestablished. Use a structured opening that names the event, validates the difficulty of being back, and offers the client choice in pacing.
- Narrative reconstruction is collaborative; use Socratic questions; respect what the client cannot articulate yet; do not push trauma processing in this phase.
- Updated safety plan must be built freshly — not a copy of the pre-attempt plan — informed by what actually happened during the crisis.
- Means restriction is reverified, not assumed: specific verification (photo, in-person, pharmacy confirmation) within 24–72 h.
- 90-day schedule includes: weekly therapy minimum (often 2x/week first month), prescriber visit within 7–14 days, PCP coordination, mobile-crisis number reviewed, between-session check-ins (text/portal).
- Family / partner involvement is offered; honor client's pace if not ready, but document the offer.
- Coordination with discharging facility includes records request, prior provider call, and any unresolved items (e.g., labs pending, medication changes).
- Risk re-stratification is performed and documented at this re-entry visit; a fresh Columbia-format assessment is referenced.

### Must Not

- Do not skip the event — silence about the attempt is itself a clinical statement that often increases shame.
- Do not interrogate ("walk me through everything that happened") before alliance is reestablished, except as needed for safety.
- Do not assume the previous safety plan is still valid; rebuild.
- Do not assume means restriction agreed to before the attempt is in place; reverify.
- Do not delay first contact more than 48–72 h after discharge if avoidable; document obstacles if delay occurs.
- Do not exclude family / partner without exploration; document client's choice and rationale.
- Do not document "client appears stable, no further intervention needed" at first re-entry visit.
- Do not fabricate; flag missing inputs.

## Instructions

1. Compile re-entry metadata.
2. Open with a stigma-aware reconnection script: name the event, normalize re-entry difficulty, invite collaboration on pacing.
3. Begin narrative reconstruction at the client's pace; capture what the client now sees.
4. Map what helped / what didn't during the hospital stay and the days leading up to the attempt.
5. Build a fresh Stanley-Brown safety plan informed by the crisis.
6. Reverify means-restriction status with concrete verification within 72 h.
7. Coordinate with prescriber: side effects, adherence, plan for next visit; with PCP: medical follow-up, wound or overdose-related sequelae.
8. Offer family / partner involvement; document choice.
9. Build practical re-entry plan: work / school / driving / transportation / finances.
10. Set 90-day follow-up schedule with concrete dates and modality.
11. Re-stratify risk; reference Columbia assessment date.
12. Coordinate with discharging facility (records, calls, unresolved items).
13. Document everything per documentation block.
14. Run verification.

## Verification

- [ ] All labeled sections present and in order.
- [ ] Reconnection frame includes a stigma-aware opening script and pacing choice.
- [ ] Narrative reconstruction captures client's current understanding without forcing detail.
- [ ] What-helped / what-didn't covers both hospital stay and pre-attempt period.
- [ ] Updated safety plan is freshly built (not a copy of prior).
- [ ] Means restriction status verified within 72 h with concrete verification methods.
- [ ] Prescriber and PCP coordination documented with dates.
- [ ] Family / partner involvement offered and choice documented.
- [ ] Practical re-entry plan addresses work / school / driving / transportation / finances.
- [ ] 90-day follow-up schedule has dense early-window contact.
- [ ] Risk re-stratification with linked Columbia.
- [ ] Discharging-facility coordination addressed.
- [ ] No silence about the attempt; no premature interrogation.
- [ ] Gaps flagged; nothing fabricated.
