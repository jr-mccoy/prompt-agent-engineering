**Purpose:** Guide systematic revision of creative writing through structured passes focusing on story, scene, paragraph, and sentence-level improvements.

**When to use:** After completing a first draft; when preparing to submit for publication or beta readers; when feeling stuck in endless revision without progress.

**Input needed:**
- The passage, chapter, or draft to revise
- Stage of revision (first pass, polish, final)
- Specific concerns you have about the work

## Instructions

Apply the Layered Revision Framework - from macro to micro:

### Pass 1: Story-Level Review (Macro)

**Only for full drafts or substantial sections:**

Evaluate against story fundamentals:

| Element | Question | Assessment |
|---------|----------|------------|
| **Promise** | Does this deliver on what was set up? | [Yes/No - what's missing] |
| **Stakes** | Is it clear what characters risk losing? | [Yes/No - how to strengthen] |
| **Arc** | Do characters change meaningfully? | [Yes/No - where's the shift] |
| **Theme** | Does the story explore its central question? | [Yes/No - how] |
| **Pacing** | Does the story move at the right speed? | [Yes/No - where it drags/rushes] |

**Structural Issues to Flag:**
- Scenes that could be cut without losing anything
- Missing scenes that are needed
- Information revealed too early or too late
- Subplots that don't connect

### Pass 2: Scene-Level Review

For each scene, evaluate:

**Scene Diagnostic:**

| Criterion | Check | Notes |
|-----------|-------|-------|
| **Entry Point** | Do we enter late enough? | [Cut opening throat-clearing] |
| **Exit Point** | Do we leave at the right moment? | [End on resonance or tension] |
| **Goal** | Does POV character want something specific? | [Make want concrete] |
| **Conflict** | What opposes them in THIS scene? | [Add friction] |
| **Change** | Is something different by scene end? | [Emotional or situational shift] |
| **Purpose** | What does this scene accomplish? | [If unclear, flag for cut] |

**Scene-Level Fixes:**
- Combine scenes that accomplish the same thing
- Add tension to quiet scenes through subtext
- Ensure each scene has only one POV
- Check transitions between scenes

### Pass 3: Paragraph-Level Review

**Paragraph Diagnostic:**

For key paragraphs, check:

| Issue | What to Look For | Fix |
|-------|------------------|-----|
| **Topic drift** | Paragraph covers multiple ideas | Split into focused paragraphs |
| **Sameness** | Same length, same structure | Vary rhythm and length |
| **Weak opening** | First sentence doesn't hook | Start with stronger image/action |
| **Over-explanation** | Telling after showing | Cut the tell, trust the show |
| **Floating heads** | Dialogue without grounding | Add action beats and setting |

**Paragraph Pacing:**
- Short paragraphs = fast pace, tension, action
- Long paragraphs = contemplation, description, slowing
- Mix paragraph lengths for rhythm

### Pass 4: Sentence-Level Polish

**Sentence Diagnostic:**

| Issue | Example | Fix |
|-------|---------|-----|
| **Passive voice** | "The ball was thrown" | "She threw the ball" |
| **Weak verbs** | "He was walking" | "He trudged / strode / shuffled" |
| **Filter words** | "She felt the cold" | "Cold bit her skin" |
| **Redundancy** | "nodded her head" | "nodded" |
| **Throat clearing** | "It was then that..." | Cut to action |
| **Adverb overload** | "walked quickly" | "rushed" |

**Sentences to Cut or Revise:**
- Any sentence that only tells emotion already shown
- Repeated information (even if phrased differently)
- Stage directions (she turned, she moved, she looked)
- Generic descriptions (beautiful, amazing, very)

### Pass 5: Word-Level Precision

**Word Choice Audit:**

| Category | Weak | Strong |
|----------|------|--------|
| **Verbs** | was, had, went, said | [specific action verbs] |
| **Nouns** | thing, stuff, area | [concrete specifics] |
| **Modifiers** | very, really, just | [cut or replace] |
| **Said-bookisms** | exclaimed, declared | said/asked (usually) |

**Word-Level Checklist:**
- [ ] Cut empty intensifiers (very, really, quite)
- [ ] Replace vague words with specific ones
- [ ] Check for word echoes (same word too close)
- [ ] Verify period vocabulary if historical
- [ ] Confirm character voice consistency

### Pass 6: Technical Polish

**Final Checks:**

| Element | Check |
|---------|-------|
| **Dialogue formatting** | New speaker = new paragraph |
| **Tense consistency** | No unintentional shifts |
| **POV consistency** | No head-hopping |
| **Punctuation** | Comma splices, em dashes, semicolons |
| **Homophone errors** | their/there/they're, its/it's |

## Output Format

Structure the response as:

1. **Overall Assessment** (1-2 paragraphs on strengths and main issues)
2. **Story-Level Feedback** (if applicable)
3. **Scene-Level Recommendations** (specific scenes to adjust)
4. **Paragraph Examples** (before/after samples)
5. **Sentence-Level Examples** (specific lines revised)
6. **Revised Passage** (complete rewrite incorporating changes)
7. **Priority Action List** (top 3-5 things to address first)

---

## Revision Priority Matrix

**Fix first (structural):**
- Missing or broken character arc
- Scenes without purpose
- Pacing problems
- Unclear stakes

**Fix second (execution):**
- Weak scene openings/endings
- Missing conflict in scenes
- Ungrounded dialogue
- Telling instead of showing

**Fix last (polish):**
- Sentence-level issues
- Word choice refinement
- Technical corrections

---

## Quality Indicators

**Strong revision addresses:**
- [ ] Every scene earns its place
- [ ] Pacing matches emotional needs
- [ ] Characters sound distinct
- [ ] Description engages senses
- [ ] Prose rhythm varies appropriately
- [ ] No information repeated unnecessarily

**Common Revision Mistakes:**

| Mistake | Why It Happens | Alternative |
|---------|----------------|-------------|
| **Polishing too early** | Avoiding structural work | Fix story first, then sentences |
| **Cutting all personality** | Over-applying "rules" | Keep what makes your voice unique |
| **Adding not subtracting** | Fear of losing content | Trust that less is often more |
| **Revision paralysis** | Endless cycles | Set deadline, declare done |
| **Ignoring feedback patterns** | Defensive reaction | If multiple readers flag it, address it |
