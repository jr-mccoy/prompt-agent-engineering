# Educational Coloring Page (Letter / Number / Fact + Illustration)

**Purpose:** Generate a coloring page that doubles as a learning worksheet for early-childhood education — a large colorable letter, number, shape, sight word, or short fact paired with a matching illustration the child also colors (e.g. "A is for Apple" with a big block "A" and an apple). Pure black line art on white, print-ready.

**Format / Dimensions:** Single flat page, 8.5" x 11" portrait (letter) at 300 DPI. Pure line art, no color, no grayscale. Text appears as **hollow/outline (colorable) letterforms** so it stays consistent with the line-art aesthetic.

## Page Type Controls

| Type | Big colorable element | Matching illustration | Caption (outline text) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Alphabet | Block letter "A" | Apple | "A is for Apple" |
| Number | Numeral "3" | Three balloons (count-and-color) | "3 — three" |
| Shape | Outline circle | Sun / ball | "Circle" |
| Sight word | Word "cat" in bubble letters | Cat | "cat" |
| Fact | Small heading | Labeled diagram (e.g. parts of a flower) | short fact line |

## Image Generation Prompt (Production-Ready)

```
TASK: Generate ONE FLAT PRINT ARTWORK IMAGE that is an EDUCATIONAL COLORING PAGE (a learning worksheet a child colors in).

IMPORTANT REAL-WORLD CONTEXT:
This is a coloring + learning page for a preschool/early-elementary child.
It will be PRINTED on letter-size paper.
The child COLORS the big letter/number AND the matching picture, and reads/traces the caption.
It teaches a concept (a letter, number, shape, sight word, or simple fact) while being colored.

This is NOT a finished illustration.
This is NOT a grayscale or shaded drawing.
This is NOT a sketch, a mockup, or a photo of a book.
This image IS the literal black ink-on-paper line art sent directly to a printer.

LEARNING CONTENT (fill in):
- Page type: [alphabet / number / shape / sight word / fact]
- Big colorable element: [e.g. block letter "A"]
- Matching illustration: [e.g. an apple]
- Caption text (outline letters): [e.g. "A is for Apple"]

================================================
CRITICAL OUTPUT RULES (NON-NEGOTIABLE)
================================================

- Output EXACTLY ONE IMAGE.
- PURE BLACK line art (#000000) on PURE WHITE (#FFFFFF) background.
- NO color anywhere.
- NO grayscale, NO gray tones.
- NO shading, hatching, crosshatching, or solid black fills.
- NO gradients of any kind.
- NO filled regions — every enclosed space stays WHITE so it can be colored.
- ALL text is HOLLOW / OUTLINE letterforms (colorable) — NOT solid black text.
- Single flat page, viewed straight-on. NOT a 3D render, NOT a book mockup.

If any gray tone, shading, color, or solid black fill (including solid black text) appears, the output is INCORRECT.

================================================
LAYOUT (ENUMERATED ZONES)
================================================

ZONE 1 - TOP (caption): the caption text in large HOLLOW outline letters, centered. Correctly spelled exactly as written above. Bubble or block letters with thick clean outlines.

ZONE 2 - CENTER-LEFT (the concept): the big colorable element (the letter/number/shape) as a large hollow outlined form, thick bold outline, plenty of interior white to color.

ZONE 3 - CENTER-RIGHT (the picture): the matching illustration in simple bold closed line art, sized to balance the concept element.

ZONE 4 - OPTIONAL BOTTOM (practice): if requested, 3-4 light dotted tracing guides of the letter/number for the child to trace (thin dotted lines only).

Keep the layout clean and uncluttered; the concept element and the picture are the two focal points.

================================================
TEXT ACCURACY (IMPORTANT)
================================================

- Spell the caption EXACTLY as provided. Do not invent or misspell words.
- Use only the words given. No extra labels, slogans, or watermark text.
- If a label is requested on the illustration (e.g. "petal"), spell it exactly and render it as outline text.

================================================
LINE ART SPECIFICATIONS
================================================

- Bold outlines suited to young children: 3 pt to 5 pt for the concept and picture; 2-3 pt for caption letterforms.
- All shapes and letterforms have CLOSED outlines (no gaps).
- Large, simple colorable regions; minimal tiny detail.
- Illustration is cheerful, friendly, and instantly recognizable.

================================================
COMPOSITION & LAYOUT
================================================

- White margin: 0.5 inch clean white border on all four sides.
- Balanced, uncluttered composition across the enumerated zones.
- No watermark, no signature, no page number in the art.

================================================
ALLOWED vs FORBIDDEN
================================================

ALLOWED:
- Hollow/outline colorable letters and numbers
- A big concept element + one matching illustration
- Optional thin dotted tracing guides
- A correctly spelled caption

FORBIDDEN:
- Any color, gray, shading, gradient, or solid black fill
- SOLID black text (text must be hollow/colorable)
- Misspelled or invented words
- Thin/delicate lines on the concept or picture
- Open / broken outlines
- Busy background, shadows, or 3D depth
- A picture OF a coloring book (mockup)

================================================
OUTPUT SPECIFICATIONS
================================================

- Dimensions: 8.5 x 11 inches, portrait.
- Resolution: 300 DPI (2550 x 3300 px).
- Background: pure white (#FFFFFF) only — no texture, no vignette, no fade.

================================================
FINAL VALIDATION CHECK
================================================

- [ ] Exactly one image
- [ ] Caption spelled EXACTLY as provided, in hollow outline letters
- [ ] Big colorable concept element present (letter/number/shape/word)
- [ ] Matching illustration present and recognizable
- [ ] All text is hollow/colorable (no solid black text)
- [ ] Pure black lines only — no gray, no color
- [ ] Every interior space is pure white (colorable)
- [ ] NO shading, gradients, or solid black fills; outlines closed
- [ ] Bold child-friendly line weights
- [ ] 0.5 inch clean white margin; no watermark/signature/page number
- [ ] Flat page, not a mockup or 3D render

If text is misspelled, solid black, or any shading/fill/color appears, the output is INCORRECT.

================================================
GENERATE NOW
================================================

Produce a single educational coloring page following all rules above.
```

## Why This Prompt Works

1. **Terminology Steering (SV-11)** — "flat print artwork" / "learning worksheet" steers to print, not UI/render.
2. **Grid Forcing + Enumerated Slots (SV-12)** — explicit ZONE 1-4 layout enumerates caption, concept, picture, and tracing, preventing a cluttered improvised layout.
3. **Printable Worksheet Output Format (SV-05)** — this page is a true worksheet, so the worksheet-format technique applies alongside the coloring constraints.
4. **Constraint Redundancy (SV-13)** — no-fill and hollow-text rules repeat across critical rules, text-accuracy, allowed/forbidden, and checklist.
5. **Negative Space Control (SV-14)** — pure white background, uncluttered zones, explicit margin.
6. **Allowed vs Forbidden (SV-15)** — permits outline letters and dotted tracing while forbidding solid black text (the key educational-page failure).
7. **Physical Context Anchoring (SV-16)** — "a preschooler colors and traces it" sets line weight and the colorable-text requirement.
8. **Deliverables Locking (SV-17) + Validation Checklist (SV-18)** — one image, exact size; final self-audit includes a spelling check, since text-in-image is the highest-risk failure mode here.

## Model-Specific Notes

### gpt-image-2 (OpenAI, primary)
- `quality="high"` — text rendering accuracy improves on the high path; essential for correct spelling.
- gpt-image-2 renders in-image text well; still verify the caption spelling on output.
- Put the ZONE layout and TEXT ACCURACY rules under CONSTRAINTS. Do NOT pass `input_fidelity` (disabled).

### Nano Banana (Gemini 3 Pro Image / Gemini 3.1 Flash Image, primary)
- Nano Banana's autoregressive architecture gives strong in-image text — ideal for captions and labels. Nano Banana Pro renders near-perfect text and lets you name a font.
- Use Markdown + ALL-CAPS; specify `#000000` outlines on `#FFFFFF`, "hollow outline letters, NOT filled."
- If spelling is off, iterate: "fix the caption to read exactly 'A is for Apple' in hollow letters."

### DALL-E 3 (legacy)
Add: `"educational coloring worksheet, hollow outline letters, black line art, no shading, no color, white background"`. DALL-E 3 text is unreliable — double-check spelling and re-roll if wrong.

### Midjourney (legacy)
```
educational coloring page, big hollow outline letter [X], matching [picture], caption "[text]", clean black line art, white background
--ar 17:22 --v 6 --style raw --s 25
--no color shading gray gradient fill solid black 3d mockup photo
```
Midjourney struggles with exact text — keep captions short and verify; `--no color shading` keeps line art clean.

### Stable Diffusion (legacy)
- SD is weakest at in-image text; consider generating the illustration with a **lineart ControlNet**, then adding the caption as real outline-font text in a layout tool.
Positive: `"educational coloring page, outline letters, simple bold line art, closed outlines, white background"`
Negative: `"shading, grayscale, gray, color, gradient, solid black, fill, realistic, texture, misspelled text, gibberish text"`

## Troubleshooting

### Problem: Caption is misspelled or gibberish
**Add:** `"The caption must read EXACTLY: '[your text]'. Verify the spelling letter by letter."` On weaker models, add the text in a layout tool instead.

### Problem: Text rendered as solid black
**Add:** `"All letters are HOLLOW outline letterforms with white interiors to color, NOT solid black."`

### Problem: Concept element too small to color
**Add:** `"Make the [letter/number] LARGE — at least a third of the page — with thick outlines and big white interior."`

### Problem: Picture doesn't match the concept
**Add:** `"The illustration must clearly be a [picture] to match '[caption]'."`

### Problem: Shading/fills appear
**Add:** `"Every area, including inside the letters, stays pure white. NO gray, NO shading, NO solid black fill."`
