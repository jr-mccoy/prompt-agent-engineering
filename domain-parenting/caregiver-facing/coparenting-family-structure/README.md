# Family Structure Prompts (Caregiver-Facing)

Parenting in families that are structured differently from the two-parent default the rest of the library often assumes: a relative who has suddenly become the parent, a new foster placement, a solo parent, and an LGBTQ+-parented family. Each prompt produces a concrete plan for one household — what the child is told and in what words, who backs the parent up, how contact and outside systems (caseworkers, schools, relatives) are handled — with clear thresholds for when the child, the carer, or the placement needs outside help.

## Scope

**In scope:**
- Kinship care: a grandparent, aunt, uncle, adult sibling, or family friend raising a child after a parent's addiction, incarceration, death, illness, or removal.
- The first 30 days of a non-relative foster placement: arrival, felt safety, trauma-informed behaviour response, family-time visits, caseworker updates.
- Solo parenting by choice or circumstance: single-point-of-failure planning, backup care, support network, explaining the family.
- LGBTQ+-parented families: the child's origin and family story, school briefing, questions, and hostility.

**Lives elsewhere (not here):**
- Separation, divorce, custody documents, and two-home co-parenting → `../divorce/`, `../custody/`, `../co-parenting/` (an unsafe or absent other parent → `../co-parenting/parenting_coparenting_with_unsafe_or_absent_parent.md`; stepfamilies → `../co-parenting/parenting_coparenting_blended_family_coordination.md`).
- A relative's **legal** standing to seek custody or visitation → `domain-legal/custody/legal_third_party_custody_visitation_analysis.md`.
- Preparing children already in the home for a new foster or adopted sibling → `../transitions-events/parenting_new_sibling_arrival_prep.md`.
- Household values and rules adapted to family structure → `../cross-age/parenting_household_constitution_designer.md`.
- A depleted parent's load redesign → `../parent-capacity/parenting_parent_overload_load_redesign.md`.
- A child exploring their own gender or sexuality → not covered here; clinician-facing work is in `domain-psychology/populations/lgbtq-affirmative/`.

## Safety notes

Every prompt carries a Safety Block. Common routes: 911 for immediate danger (including a parent arriving intoxicated or trying to remove a child against an order); child-abuse reporting through the caseworker or Childhelp 1-800-422-4453; 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline for self-harm talk; National Parent Helpline 1-855-427-2736 for a parent afraid of losing control; National DV Hotline 1-800-799-7233. Legal status (guardianship, parentage, kinship licensing) varies by jurisdiction and is always routed to a navigator or family-law attorney — none of these prompts gives legal advice. Outside the US, use local equivalents.
