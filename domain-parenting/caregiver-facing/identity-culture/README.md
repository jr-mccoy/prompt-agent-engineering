# Identity & Culture Prompts (Caregiver-Facing)

Helping children build a confident identity in families shaped by race, language, and more than one faith tradition: age-staged conversations about race and racism, a family language plan for keeping a heritage language alive, and a parenting agreement for interfaith or mixed-belief households. Each prompt turns good intentions into concrete words, routines, and decisions for one family, and names when race-related distress, language delay, or family coercion needs outside help.

## Scope

**In scope:**
- Race and racism conversations from toddlers to teens, for any family background — including multiracial and transracially adoptive families — with scripts for unplanned moments and preparation for bias.
- Raising multilingual children: family language policy, heritage-language exposure arithmetic, responding to refusal, and normal bilingual patterns versus language disorder.
- Interfaith or mixed-belief households: parenting logistics only — rites, holidays, education, what the children hear, relatives' pressure.

**Lives elsewhere (not here):**
- **Teaching about or comparing religions** — deliberately out of scope; the interfaith prompt is tradition-neutral and decides family logistics only.
- One-off hard-topic scripts for 4–8-year-olds → `../ages-4-8/parenting_hard_topics_age_appropriate_scripts.md`.
- Under-3 speech and language milestones (including bilingual children) → `../ages-0-3/parenting_speech_language_milestone_check.md`.
- Bullying for race, language, or family background → `../ages-9-12/parenting_bullying_target_response_protocol.md`.
- LGBTQ+-parented families → `../coparenting-family-structure/parenting_lgbtq_parented_family_conversations.md`; a child's own gender or sexual identity → not yet covered (clinician-facing work in `domain-psychology/populations/lgbtq-affirmative/`).
- Separated parents disagreeing about religion → `../co-parenting/parenting_coparenting_shared_decision_framework.md`.

## Safety notes

Every prompt carries a Safety Block. Racial harassment, threats, or violence → document and report to the school and police (ask about hate-incident recording). Race-related distress lasting more than 2–4 weeks → pediatrician and a culturally responsive child therapist. Language-disorder signs are judged across both languages together, by a speech-language pathologist — never by dropping the home language. Religion used to control or threaten a partner or child, or threats to take children abroad → National DV Hotline 1-800-799-7233 and a family-law attorney; medical-care disputes go to the child's clinician. 988 for any self-harm talk. Outside the US, use local equivalents.
