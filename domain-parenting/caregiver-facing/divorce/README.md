# Divorce & Separation Prompts (Caregiver-Facing)

The emotional and relational side of separation and divorce as it affects children and the parenting parent. These prompts help a caregiver tell the kids, answer their hard questions, support them across two homes, manage the parent's own grief without it leaking onto the kids, and recognize when a child needs more help.

These are companion sets to:
- **`../custody/`** — the documents and schedules (parenting plans, timesharing, logs) a parent uses and can present in court.
- **`../co-parenting/`** — the ongoing working relationship between two parents.

## Scope

**In scope:**
- Telling children about a separation; answering their recurring questions honestly and age-appropriately.
- Supporting children's adjustment to two homes, transitions, holidays, and milestones (emotionally).
- The parent's own emotional regulation so children aren't made messengers, spies, or confidants.
- Shielding children from adult conflict and loyalty binds.
- Privacy, narrative, and what to tell teachers/family.
- Introducing dating and new partners.
- Recognizing when a child needs a therapist.

**Out of scope (refuse and refer):**
- Legal advice, custody standards, or whether/how to divorce → attorney, mediator, or decision-making/relationship resources.
- Organizing evidence, finances, allegation responses, hearing/evaluation prep, or a handoff package for your attorney (the legal-case-prep side) → `domain-legal/family-self-advocacy/` (layperson, organize-your-own-side prompts; still not legal advice).
- Diagnosing the other parent or formally labeling "parental alienation" → clinical/legal professionals.
- A child in crisis or an unsafe home → see each prompt's Safety Block (988; Childhelp 1-800-422-4453; National DV Hotline 1-800-799-7233; 911).

## Composition patterns

These prompts compose. Common chains:
- **First disclosure:** `parenting_divorce_telling_the_kids_script` → `parenting_divorce_hard_questions_answer_bank` → `parenting_divorce_child_reaction_by_age_guide`.
- **Living in two homes:** `parenting_divorce_two_homes_transition_support` → `parenting_divorce_milestones_and_holidays_emotional_plan` → (if struggling) `parenting_divorce_signs_child_needs_more_support`.
- **Protecting the kids:** `parenting_divorce_parent_emotional_regulation` → `parenting_divorce_shield_kids_from_conflict`.
- **Moving forward:** `parenting_divorce_new_partner_introduction_timing` (coordinate via `../co-parenting/parenting_coparenting_new_partners_and_blended_coordination`).

## What these prompts refuse

- Coaching a parent to disadvantage, badmouth, or "win against" the other parent.
- Making the child a messenger, spy, or emotional caretaker for the parent.
- False promises ("we'll get back together") or oversharing adult details with the child.
- Diagnosing the other parent or the child.
