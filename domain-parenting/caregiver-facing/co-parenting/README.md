# Co-Parenting Prompts (Caregiver-Facing)

The ongoing operational and relational practice of raising children across two homes after separation. These prompts help a parent communicate with the other parent without escalating, keep some consistency across homes, make joint decisions, coordinate new partners, de-escalate conflict in front of the kids, audit their own behavior honestly, and navigate the hardest cases — a high-conflict, unsafe, or absent co-parent.

Companion sets: **`../divorce/`** (the emotional/relational side for the child) and **`../custody/`** (the documents and schedules).

## Scope

**In scope:**
- Co-parent communication (BIFF messages, information handoffs, de-escalation).
- Consistency across homes; shared decision-making frameworks.
- New partners / blended-family coordination.
- High-conflict and parallel-parenting strategy; honest self-audit.
- Long-distance co-parenting; navigating an unsafe or absent parent (safety-first).

**Out of scope (refuse and refer):**
- Legal advice, custody disputes, or filings → attorney/mediator (and `../custody/` for documents).
- Organizing evidence, finances, allegation responses, or an attorney handoff package for the legal case → `domain-legal/family-self-advocacy/` (layperson, organize-your-own-side prompts; still not legal advice).
- Diagnosing the other parent or formally labeling "parental alienation" → clinical/legal professionals.
- Safety emergencies, DV, abuse → see each prompt's Safety Block (National DV Hotline 1-800-799-7233; Childhelp 1-800-422-4453; 988; 911).

## Composition patterns

- **Calm the channel:** `parenting_coparenting_self_audit_am_i_the_problem` → `parenting_coparenting_message_composer_biff` → `parenting_coparenting_information_handoff_brief`.
- **Decide together:** `parenting_coparenting_shared_decision_framework` → `parenting_coparenting_consistency_across_homes`.
- **High-conflict branch:** `parenting_coparenting_high_conflict_response_strategy` → `parenting_coparenting_deescalation_in_front_of_kids`; pair with `../custody/parenting_custody_communication_log_template`.
- **Try to reset:** `parenting_coparenting_relationship_reset_proposal`.
- **Hard cases:** `parenting_coparenting_with_unsafe_or_absent_parent`; `parenting_coparenting_long_distance_protocol`; `parenting_coparenting_new_partners_and_blended_coordination`.

## What these prompts refuse

- Coaching a parent to "win," disadvantage, or diagnose the other parent.
- Badmouthing/disparaging the other parent to or near the kids.
- Using the child as a messenger, spy, or confidant.
- Strategy that serves the parent's conflict at the child's expense.
