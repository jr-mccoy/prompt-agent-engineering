# Transitions & Life Events Prompts (Caregiver-Facing)

Helping children through the big, one-time changes in family life — a new sibling, a household move, a new school, and the death of someone they love. Each prompt produces a dated plan for one child and one event, from before the change through the weeks after, with clear thresholds for when ordinary adjustment has become something that needs professional help.

## Scope

**In scope:**
- Preparing an older child for a new baby, adoption, or foster/kinship placement.
- The child's emotional side of moving house (losses, rituals, rebuilding friendships).
- Supporting a grieving child or teen in the weeks and months after a death.
- Starting a new school or moving between school levels, including transferring IEP/504 and health plans.

**Lives elsewhere (not here):**
- Separation/divorce and moving between two parents' homes → `../divorce/`, `../co-parenting/`, `../custody/`.
- The first conversation telling a 4–8-year-old about a death, divorce, or diagnosis → `../ages-4-8/parenting_hard_topics_age_appropriate_scripts.md`.
- Ongoing sibling conflict or age-gap dynamics → `../ages-4-8/parenting_sibling_conflict_coach.md`, `../cross-age/parenting_sibling_spacing_dynamics.md`.
- Moving logistics (boxes, utilities, address changes) → `domain-productivity/home-life/home_moving_checklist.md`.
- Under-3 childcare entry → `../ages-0-3/parenting_daycare_transition_plan.md`; school refusal → `../ages-9-12/parenting_school_refusal_decoder_tween.md`.
- An adult's own grief or relocation adjustment → `domain-psychology/client-self-use/grief-loss/`, `domain-personal-development/prompts/life-transitions/`.

## Safety notes

Every prompt carries a Safety Block. Common routes: 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline (US) for any talk of wanting to die or "be with" someone who died; 911 for immediate danger; Postpartum Support International 1-800-944-4773 and the National Maternal Mental Health Hotline 1-833-852-6262 for a parent struggling after a birth; National DV Hotline 1-800-799-7233 when a move is an escape. Complicated or traumatic grief routes to a child bereavement or TF-CBT-trained clinician. Outside the US, use local equivalents.
