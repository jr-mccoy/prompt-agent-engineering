# Custody Resource & Document Builders (Caregiver-Facing)

Tools for building the **documents and records** a parent needs after separation — parenting plans, residential and holiday schedules, communication logs, expense trackers, exchange protocols, and addenda. Each produces a **factual, neutral, child-centered document** a parent can use day-to-day *and* present to a mediator, guardian ad litem (GAL), parenting coordinator, or court.

> **These are not legal prompts.** They organize the parent's own decisions and information. They are **not legal advice, legal filings, or a substitute for an attorney or your jurisdiction's custody law.** Custody standards, required provisions, and terminology vary by state and country and change over time. Have an attorney or mediator review anything before you rely on it or submit it. Where a legal concept appears, these prompts explain it in plain language and flag *confirm with counsel for your jurisdiction* — a **light legal-literacy layer**, not advice. They will not invent statutes or predict court outcomes.

Companion sets: **`../divorce/`** (the emotional/relational side) and **`../co-parenting/`** (the ongoing working relationship).

## Scope

**In scope:**
- Building a complete parenting plan and the schedules that go in it.
- Articulating a child-centered proposal for mediation.
- Keeping factual, court-presentable records (communication log, expense tracker).
- Designing low-conflict exchanges; special-needs addenda; organizing documentation when circumstances change.
- Plain-language literacy on common plan provisions (flagged "confirm with counsel").

**Out of scope (refuse and refer):**
- Legal advice, legal strategy, statute/case citation, custody-outcome prediction, or court filings → attorney/mediator.
- Organizing your own evidence, finances, allegation responses, hearing/custody-evaluation prep, or an attorney handoff package → `domain-legal/family-self-advocacy/` (layperson, organize-your-own-side prompts; still not legal advice).
- Drafting contact/exchange terms around an unsafe parent → counsel/advocate; see Safety Block (National DV Hotline 1-800-799-7233; Childhelp 1-800-422-4453; 911).

## Composition patterns

- **Build the plan:** `parenting_custody_schedule_designer_by_age` + `parenting_custody_holiday_vacation_schedule_builder` → `parenting_custody_parenting_plan_builder` → (if applicable) `parenting_custody_special_needs_plan_addendum`.
- **Prepare for mediation:** `parenting_custody_parenting_plan_builder` → `parenting_custody_child_focused_proposal_articulator`; understand terms via `parenting_custody_common_plan_provisions_explainer`.
- **Keep the record:** `parenting_custody_communication_log_template` + `parenting_custody_expense_and_logistics_tracker` → `parenting_custody_changed_circumstances_documentation_organizer`.
- **Reduce exchange conflict:** `parenting_custody_exchange_and_transition_protocol`.

## What these prompts refuse

- Characterizing, accusing, or disparaging the other parent in any document.
- Inventing legal standards, custody percentages, or predicting what a court will decide.
- Asserting a term is "enforceable," "standard," or "what the court wants."
- Producing inflammatory or strategic language designed to disadvantage the other parent.
- Making the child a messenger or a decision-maker about adult matters.
