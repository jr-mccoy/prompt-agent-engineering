# Parent Capacity Prompts (Caregiver-Facing)

The parent's side of parenting: redesigning a load that has become unsustainable, planning the first 12 weeks after a birth or adoption before exhaustion sets in, and keeping a couple's relationship working under the strain of raising children. Each prompt screens for depression, perinatal mood problems, or abuse first, and routes those to professional and crisis help rather than treating them as planning problems.

## Scope

**In scope:**
- Parental burnout and depletion: four-feature check, load inventory including invisible planning work, drop/defer/delegate/simplify, minimum viable week.
- The fourth trimester: sleep shifts, feeding support, helper briefing, physical-recovery warning signs, scheduled mood check-ins for both parents with an escalation ladder, the return-to-work week.
- Couple strain under parenting load: whole-task division of labour, co-parenting alliance for disagreements, constructive conflict and repair in front of children, therapy threshold — after a private abuse screen.

## Safety notes

Every prompt carries a Safety Block. 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline for any thought of self-harm; 911 / emergency department for postpartum psychosis signs (confusion, hallucinations, strange beliefs — do not leave the parent alone with the baby), urgent post-birth physical signs, or immediate danger. Postpartum Support International 1-800-944-4773; National Maternal Mental Health Hotline 1-833-852-6262. National Parent Helpline 1-855-427-2736 or Childhelp 1-800-422-4453 for a parent afraid of hurting their child. Any sign of abuse or coercive control stops couple exercises → National DV Hotline 1-800-799-7233 (text START to 88788). Depression signs lasting two weeks route to a GP or clinician; load redesign does not treat depression. Outside the US, use local equivalents.
