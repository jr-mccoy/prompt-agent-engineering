# Foster, Kinship & Adoption Prompts (Family-Support Professional)

The worker's side of foster, kinship, and adoptive care — for supervising social workers, visit supervisors, kinship navigators, caseworkers, and family-support workers between a child in care, the carers, and the birth family. Each prompt opens with safeguarding, consent, and scope, gives a structured method and scripts, and names the points where a licensed clinician, the court, or a child-protection report takes over. **No prompt here diagnoses** a child, carer, or birth parent: workers record what they see and hear, the family's own words, and how often.

## Scope

**In scope:**
- Keeping a foster or kinship placement together: carer strain on anchored scales, the dominant driver, behaviour support, respite, training matched to need, and a stability meeting before any disruption notice.
- Supervising and coaching family time between a child in care and birth family: preparation, coaching toward strengths, a redirect–pause–end ladder, goodbyes, and neutral, time-stamped notes for the agency or court.
- Life story work: a sourced history, honest age-matched wording for hard facts, pacing, trigger dates, who leads, and when a therapist must lead.

## Safety notes

Reporting duties vary. Who is a mandated reporter, the threshold (reasonable suspicion or reasonable cause to believe, not proof), timelines, and whether a supervisor consult satisfies the duty all differ by state, province, country, and agency — **verify locally**. In most US states the duty is personal and is not discharged by telling a supervisor; a consult must never delay a report. Concerns about care inside a foster home are a report, not a support issue. Crisis routes: **911** (or the local emergency number) for immediate danger; **988** (US) for suicide talk; **National DV Hotline 1-800-799-7233**; **Poison Control 1-800-222-1222**; **Childhelp 1-800-422-4453** to find the child-protection hotline; outside the US, findahelpline.com. Explain the limits of confidentiality before anyone discloses, get written releases before sharing outside the agency unless the law requires or permits otherwise, share the minimum necessary, and remember children, carers, and parents may read the records. De-identify anything pasted into an AI tool unless the agency has approved it for client data.
