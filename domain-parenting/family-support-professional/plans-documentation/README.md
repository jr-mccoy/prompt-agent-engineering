# Plans & Documentation Prompts (Family-Support Professional)

The paperwork side of family support, for home visitors, family support workers, parent educators, and caseworkers — not caregivers. These prompts help a worker co-create a goal plan the family owns, write fair, factual contact notes, and close a case safely. Each keeps the family's own words at the centre and tells the worker to de-identify anything pasted into an AI tool. **No prompt here diagnoses** a parent or child; records describe observable behaviour, the family's words, and frequency.

## Scope

**In scope:**
- Family goal and service plans: family-chosen goals in their words, small measurable steps, who does what (worker included), family-rated progress, review dates, and court or agency conditions kept in a separate, verbatim section.
- Contact and visit notes: facts vs. observations vs. worker interpretation, quotes, strengths, judgement-free wording, what not to record, and the order of report-then-note when a safeguarding concern arises.
- Ending services: planned vs. unplanned closure, outreach and a safety gate before closing on a family who stopped answering, closure summary, the family's own plan, consented warm hand-offs, and a closure letter.

## Safety notes

Reporting duties, thresholds (reasonable suspicion or reasonable cause to believe — not proof), timelines, and whether a supervisor consult counts **vary by state, province, country, and agency — verify locally**. In most US states the duty is personal and is not discharged by telling a supervisor; a consult never delays a report or an emergency call. Report first, then document. Immediate danger → **911** or the local emergency number; child-protection hotline or law enforcement for reports (**Childhelp 1-800-422-4453** helps find the number). Clinical routes: **988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline** (US), **Poison Control 1-800-222-1222**, **National DV Hotline 1-800-799-7233**; outside the US, local emergency numbers and findahelpline.com. Consent comes first: say the limits of confidentiality before anyone discloses, share with other agencies only under a signed release unless law requires otherwise, send the minimum necessary, and write every record as if the family will read it.
