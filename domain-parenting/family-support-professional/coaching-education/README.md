# Coaching & Education Prompts (Family-Support Professional)

For parent coaches, parent educators, home visitors, and family support workers who
teach parenting skills one-to-one or through a set curriculum. These prompts plan a
coaching session around a goal the parent chose, open work with a parent who was
required to attend by a court, agency, or school, and adapt an evidence-based
parenting program without breaking its core. Each prompt states the limits of
confidentiality before anyone discloses, keeps mandated-reporting steps in view, and
names the signs that mean a licensed clinician should take over. **No prompt here
diagnoses** a parent or child, and none is therapy. Workers describe what they see and
hear; clinicians assess.

## Scope

**In scope:**
- One-to-one coaching sessions: parent-chosen goal, strengths first, OARS, model–practice–feedback, a home step sized to the family's week.
- Mandated or court-ordered parents: what is required and what is the parent's choice, what goes back to the referrer, the separate reporting duty, lowering defensiveness, the parent's own goal.
- Adapting licensed curricula (Triple P, Incredible Years, Nurturing Parenting, Parents as Teachers, and similar): core vs adaptable components, green/yellow/red changes, adaptation logs, fidelity ratings, licensing respect.

**Lives elsewhere (not here):**
- Caregivers working on their own parenting → `domain-parenting/caregiver-facing/` (e.g. `../../caregiver-facing/ages-4-8/parenting_parenting_style_self_assessment.md`, `../../caregiver-facing/cross-age/parenting_when_to_seek_professional_help_all_ages.md`).
- Clinician-delivered treatment and reporting decisions → `domain-psychology/` (`modalities/behavioral-parent-training/`, `modalities/motivational-interviewing/`, `risk-crisis/psychology_mandated_reporter_decision_walkthrough.md`).
- Questions about a court order, a case, custody, or rights → `domain-legal/` and the parent's own attorney.
- Intake and screening → `../intake-assessment/`; service plans and notes → `../plans-documentation/`; group sessions and hard group moments → `../group-facilitation/`; clinician referrals → `../referral-resourcing/`; home visits → `../home-visit-fieldwork/`.

## Safety notes

Mandated-reporting rules — who must report, the threshold (reasonable suspicion, not
proof), deadlines, and whether a supervisor consult counts — vary by state, province,
country, and agency. **Verify locally.** In most US states the duty is personal and is
not discharged by telling a supervisor; a consult never delays a report. Immediate
danger → **911** or the local emergency number. Child-protection hotline or law
enforcement for reports (**Childhelp 1-800-422-4453** helps find the number).
Suicide talk → **988** (US). Partner violence → **National DV Hotline
1-800-799-7233**. Possible poisoning → **Poison Control 1-800-222-1222**. Outside the
US: local emergency number; findahelpline.com. Get informed consent and a written
release before sharing with another agency, share the minimum, and de-identify
anything pasted into an AI tool unless your agency has approved it.
