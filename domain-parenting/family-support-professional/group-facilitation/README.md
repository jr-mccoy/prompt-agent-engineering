# Group Facilitation Prompts (Family-Support Professional)

For parent educators, family-support workers, and community program staff who run parent
groups — weekly parenting classes, home-visiting program group socials, court- or
agency-referred courses, and peer support groups for carers. One prompt plans a session;
the other covers the moments that go off-plan. Both treat the group as education and
support, not therapy, and both put confidentiality limits on the table before anyone
shares. **No prompt here diagnoses** a parent or child: facilitators record what was said
and seen, not labels.

## Scope

**In scope:**
- Designing one session of a parent education group: one practised skill, a timed
  agenda, group agreements with confidentiality limits, access (childcare, food,
  transport, language, literacy, disability, virtual), co-facilitator roles, and the
  after-session check.
- In-the-moment responses to hard moments in group: disclosure of child abuse or partner
  violence, monopolising, harsh-punishment talk, open conflict, a tearful, suicidal, or
  intoxicated attendee — with a tiered decision, private follow-up, reporting steps, an
  incident note, and group repair.

## Safety notes

Mandated-reporting duties — who must report, the threshold (reasonable suspicion, not
proof), timelines, and whether a supervisor consult counts — vary by state, province,
country, and agency: **verify locally**. In most US states the duty is personal and is
not discharged by telling a supervisor; consulting must never delay a report or an
emergency call. Immediate danger → **911** or the local emergency number. Suicide talk →
**988** (US). Partner violence → **National DV Hotline 1-800-799-7233**. Possible
ingestion → **Poison Control 1-800-222-1222**. Finding a child-protection number →
**Childhelp 1-800-422-4453**. Outside the US: local emergency number and
findahelpline.com. Confidentiality limits are explained at enrolment and in session 1;
anything shared with another agency needs a written release unless law requires it.
De-identify anything pasted into an AI tool unless your agency has approved it for client
data.
