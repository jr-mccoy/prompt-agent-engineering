# Referral & Resourcing Prompts (Family-Support Professional)

The worker's side of getting a family to the right help: home visitors, parent educators, family support workers, caseworkers, and community program staff who notice a worry they are not licensed to assess, or who have made referrals that never turned into a real connection. One prompt turns a worry about a parent's or child's mental health, substance use, or development into observations, an urgency tier, an honest conversation, consent, and a referral a clinician can act on. The other tracks referrals to a real outcome, finds the barrier that is actually in the way, respects a family's decline, and keeps the safety duties that a decline does not remove. **No prompt here diagnoses** a parent or child, gives treatment, or gives legal or immigration advice.

## Scope

**In scope:**
- A non-clinician raising a possible mental-health, substance, or developmental concern: observations not labels, emergency / same-day / routine tiers, the direct suicide question, the conversation with the parent, written release, the referral package, follow-up date.
- Closed-loop tracking of community referrals, six-dimension barrier analysis, dominant barrier, trust-building where fear of systems blocks connection, informed decline, and the safety floor.

## Safety notes

Referral never replaces a child-protection report. Who is a mandated reporter, the threshold (reasonable suspicion, not proof), timelines, and whether a supervisor consult satisfies the duty **vary by state, province, country, and agency — verify locally**; in most US states the duty is personal, and consultation must never delay a report or an emergency call. Report routes: the state or county child-protection hotline or law enforcement (Childhelp 1-800-422-4453 can help find the number). Crisis routes: **911** for immediate danger, **988** for suicide talk, **Poison Control 1-800-222-1222**, **National DV Hotline 1-800-799-7233**; outside the US, the local emergency number and findahelpline.com. State the limits of confidentiality in plain words before anyone discloses, get a written release before sharing with any other service (except where law requires or permits otherwise — verify locally), share the minimum, and de-identify anything pasted into an AI tool unless your agency has approved it for client data.
