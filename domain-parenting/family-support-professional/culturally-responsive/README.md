# Culturally Responsive Practice Prompts (Family-Support Professional)

For parent educators, home visitors, family support workers, caseworkers, and community program staff working with families whose language, culture, or migration history differs from their own. These prompts help a worker run a session through a professional interpreter, check honestly whether a family practice is a difference or a safety issue, and build a support plan with an immigrant or refugee family that fears systems. Each prompt is strengths-based and culturally humble: ask, don't assume, and apply one safety standard to every family. **No prompt here diagnoses** a parent or child; workers describe what they saw and heard, in the family's own words, and route to licensed clinicians and legal help.

## Scope

**In scope:**
- Booking and working with a professional interpreter: stakes-based mode, interpreter pre-brief, seating and first-person speech, consent through an interpreter, disclosures, debrief; never children, relatives, or partners as interpreters.
- A reflective check when a family's practice differs from the worker's norms (sleep, discipline, feeding, remedies, extended-family and gender roles): difference vs. safer-version conversation vs. safety or legal line, and how to raise it respectfully.
- Supporting immigrant and refugee families: trust-building, the dominant barrier, service mapping, trauma and acculturation gaps, with status not asked or recorded and every immigration or benefits question routed to legal help.

## Safety notes

Mandated-reporting duties, thresholds (reasonable suspicion, not proof), timelines, and whether a supervisor consult counts vary by state, province, country, and agency — **verify locally**. In most US states the duty is personal and is not discharged by telling a supervisor; consultation never delays a report or an emergency call. Physical-discipline thresholds vary widely between places. Crisis routes: **911** or the local emergency number for immediate danger; **988** (US) for suicide talk; **Poison Control 1-800-222-1222**; **National DV Hotline 1-800-799-7233**; **Childhelp 1-800-422-4453** to find the child-protection hotline; outside the US, findahelpline.com. Say the limits of confidentiality before anyone discloses, through a professional interpreter where needed; get written releases before sharing with other agencies; and de-identify anything pasted into an AI tool.
