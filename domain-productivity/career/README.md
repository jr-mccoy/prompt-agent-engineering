## Usage

These prompts help with career exploration and qualification assessment. Use them to conduct interactive 8-question interviews that evaluate readiness for specific AI and tech roles.

Each assessment provides:
- **Qualification Verdict**: 4 tiers (Qualified Now, Nearly Qualified, Significant Gaps, Not Currently Viable)
- **Personalized Roadmap**: Immediate actions, 3-6 month goals, and milestones
- **Resource Recommendations**: Top 5 resources tailored to specific gaps
- **Salary Reality Check**: Location-based compensation ranges
- **Next Action**: One specific task to complete within 7 days
