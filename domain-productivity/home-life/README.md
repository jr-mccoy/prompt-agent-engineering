# Home Life Prompts

Practical, household-level prompts for running a home — meal planning, chore systems, family schedule coordination, appointment prep, home maintenance, decluttering, moving, and travel. Each prompt produces a concrete, actionable output matched to your household's specific situation rather than generic advice.

---

## Prompts in This Directory

| Filename | Title | What It Does |
|----------|-------|-------------|
| `home_meal_plan_week.md` | Weekly Meal Planner | Generate a 7-day meal plan and consolidated grocery list matched to your household's cooking time, budget, dietary restrictions, and what's already in the pantry. |
| `home_chore_rotation_designer.md` | Household Chore Rotation Designer | Design a sustainable chore rotation — who does what, how often — for solo, couple, or family households. Classifies chores by frequency and effort, distributes load by time (not task count), and produces a one-page weekly snapshot. |
| `home_family_schedule_coordinator.md` | Family Schedule Coordinator | Combine multiple household members' schedules into a weekly snapshot that surfaces conflicts, logistics gaps, and coverage needs before they become crises. |
| `home_appointment_prep.md` | Appointment Prep Generator | Prepare for any professional appointment — medical, school meeting (IEP/504/parent-teacher), financial, home repair estimate, or legal consultation — with a documents-to-bring list, prioritized questions, and a post-appointment action plan. |
| `home_seasonal_maintenance_calendar.md` | Home Seasonal Maintenance Calendar | Build a month-by-month home maintenance calendar tailored to your home type (house/condo/rental), climate zone, ownership status, and installed systems. Prevents seasonal tasks from falling through the cracks. |
| `home_declutter_session.md` | Declutter Session Planner | Plan and execute a single, time-bounded declutter session on one room or area using a keep/donate/trash/relocate framework. Produces decision rules and concrete action lists for everything removed. |
| `home_moving_checklist.md` | Moving Checklist Generator | Generate a comprehensive timeline-organized moving checklist — from 8+ weeks out through the first week in the new place — tailored to renter vs. owner, local vs. long-distance, and movers vs. DIY. |
| `home_travel_trip_planner.md` | Trip Planner | Convert a confirmed trip into three artifacts: a pre-trip planning checklist, a trip-specific packing list (matched to destination, climate, and activities), and a departure-day checklist covering both personal documents and home management. |

## When to Use This Directory

Use the prompts here when the task is **household-level logistics** — planning, coordinating, maintaining, or executing something related to running a home or family.

**Use this directory when you want to:**
- Plan meals and grocery shopping for the week
- Design a chore system or fix one that's breaking down
- Coordinate a week where multiple people have competing schedules
- Prepare for an appointment with something to lose (deposit, diagnosis, school placement)
- Build a home maintenance calendar so seasonal tasks don't get forgotten
- Declutter a specific area in a single bounded session
- Plan a move without forgetting the address change list or deposit documentation
- Plan a trip and produce a specific packing list, not a generic one

## How This Directory Differs from Other Productivity Subdirectories

| Directory | Focus | Use When |
|-----------|-------|----------|
| `home-life/` | Household logistics — meals, chores, schedules, home maintenance, moves, travel | You're running a home and need to plan, coordinate, or execute something household-related |
| `deep-work/` | Personal focus, attention, and deep work sessions | You're managing your own cognitive work environment and need help protecting focus time |
| `bottlenecks/` | Diagnosing and removing personal productivity constraints | You've identified that something systemic is slowing you down — procrastination, capture system, perfectionism |
| `reviews/` | Time audits and weekly/monthly cadence reviews | You're doing a retrospective on how time was actually used and adjusting going forward |
| `daily-planning/` | Day-level task planning and prioritization | You're planning a single day or need to prioritize a task list |
| `automation/` | Workflow automation design | You want to systematize and automate a repetitive process |
| `validation/` | Decision and reasoning checks | You've made a decision and want to pressure-test it |

**Parenting-adjacent tasks:** Some prompts here (family schedule coordinator, appointment prep for school meetings) overlap with the `domain-parenting/` directory. Use `home-life/` when the task is primarily logistical coordination; use `domain-parenting/` when the task is about parenting behavior, child development, or parent-school communication strategy.
